58-DARS. GOING TO vs WILL

Kelajak rejalari va kelajak haqidagi qaror/taxmin
Going to va will ikkalasi ham kelajak haqida gapiradi. Farqi ko‘pincha gapirayotgan odamning niyatiga bog‘liq. Quyidagi qoida — boshlang‘ich darajadagi qulay yo‘l; ayrim vaziyatlarda ikkisi ham ishlatilishi mumkin.

1. Going to — oldindan qilingan reja

Agar ishni oldindan rejalashtirgan yoki qaror qilib qo‘ygan bo‘lsak, ko‘pincha going to ishlatamiz.

I am going to visit my aunt on Saturday.Men shanba kuni xolamnikiga bormoqchiman. (reja oldindan bor)
She is going to study medicine.U tibbiyot o‘qimoqchi. (kelajak rejasi)
We are going to buy a new table.Biz yangi stol sotib olmoqchimiz. (reja qilingan)

2. Will — hozir qabul qilingan qaror

Ba’zan qarorni ayni gapirayotgan paytda qabul qilamiz. Bunday vaziyatda will ko‘p ishlatiladi.

The phone is ringing. I’ll answer it.Telefon jiringlayapti. Men javob beraman. (qarorni hozir qildim)
This bag is heavy. I will help you.Bu sumka og‘ir. Men sizga yordam beraman.

3. Will — va’da yoki fikrga asoslangan taxmin

I will help you tomorrow.Men ertaga sizga yordam beraman. (va’da)
I think our team will win.Menimcha, jamoamiz yutadi. (fikr/taxmin)
Maybe it will snow tonight.Balki bugun tunda qor yog‘ar. (taxmin)

4. Ko‘rinib turgan dalilga asoslangan taxmin

Agar hozir ko‘rib turgan belgimiz biror narsa sodir bo‘lishini ko‘rsatsa, ko‘pincha going to ishlatamiz.

Look at those dark clouds. It is going to rain.Qora bulutlarga qarang. Yomg‘ir yog‘adiganga o‘xshaydi.
Be careful! You are going to fall.Ehtiyot bo‘ling! Yiqilib tushishingiz mumkin.

5. Qoliplarni yonma-yon ko‘ramiz

Will dan keyin fe’lning oddiy shakli keladi: will go. Going to dan oldin esa egaga mos am/is/are kerak: She is going to go.

6. Bir xil vaziyatni ikki xil aytib ko‘ring

Gapiruvchining maqsadiga qarab ikkala shakl ham kelajakni ifodalashi mumkin. Reja oldindan bormi yoki qaror hozir qabul qilindimi — shuni o‘ylang.

I’m going to call Mum tonight.Men bugun kechqurun onamga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmoqchiman. (oldindan reja)
I’ll call Mum now.Men hozir onamga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman. (hozir qaror qildim)

7. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

I going to study. ❌ → I am going to study. ✅
She will goes. ❌ → She will go. ✅
They are going to played. ❌ → They are going to play. ✅

Mashq 1 — Vaziyatga mosini tanlang

1. Oldindan reja: I (am going to / will) visit Sam on Saturday.

2. Telefon jiringlayapti, hozir javob berishga qaror qildim: I (am going to / will) answer it.

3. Osmon juda bulutli, yomg‘ir belgisi ko‘rinib turibdi: It (is going to / will) rain.

4. Va’da: I (am going to / will) help you.

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. am going to. 2. will. 3. is going to. 4. will.

Mashq 2 — Going to yoki Will qolipini yozing

1. She ___ going to cook tonight. (is / will)

2. We ___ visit them tomorrow. (will / are)

3. I am going to ___ a book. (read / reading)

4. He will ___ at home. (stay / stays)

Javoblar:
1. is. 2. will. 3. read. 4. stay.
