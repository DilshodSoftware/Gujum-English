62-AMALIYOT. AND / BUT / OR

And = va · But = lekin · Or = yoki
And ma’lumot qo‘shadi. But qarama-qarshi fikrlarni bog‘laydi. Or tanlov beradi. Ular so‘zlarni yoki butun sodda gaplarni ulashi mumkin.

1-mashq — And, But yoki Or?

1. I like tea ___ coffee. (ikkalasi)

2. The bag is small ___ heavy. (qarama-qarshi)

3. Would you like milk ___ juice? (tanlov)

4. My brother can sing ___ dance. (ikkalasi)

5. It is cold ___ sunny today. (qarama-qarshi)

Javoblar: 1. and; 2. but; 3. or; 4. and; 5. but.

2-mashq — Gapning ma’nosini belgilang

1. She is young but very wise. (qo‘shish / qarama-qarshilik / tanlov)

2. We can walk or take a bus.

3. Ali and Sara are classmates.

Javoblar: 1. qarama-qarshilik; 2. tanlov; 3. qo‘shish.

3-mashq — Ikki gapni birlashtiring

1. I like apples. I like bananas. (and)

2. The room is small. It is clean. (but)

3. We can eat at home. We can eat at a café. (or)

4. Tom can swim. Tom can ride a bike. (and)

Javoblar: 1. I like apples and bananas. 2. The room is small but clean. 3. We can eat at home or at a café. 4. Tom can swim and ride a bike.

4-mashq — Bo‘sh joyni to‘ldiring

1. I have a sister ___ a brother.

2. He is tired, ___ he is not sleepy.

3. Do you want the red pen ___ the blue pen?

Javoblar: 1. and; 2. but; 3. or.

5-mashq — Xatoni yoki noo‘rin bog‘lovchini tuzating

1. I like apples or bananas. (ikkalasini ham yoqtiraman)

2. Do you want tea but coffee? (tanlov)

3. The house is big and expensive, but it has a large garden. (barcha ma’lumotlar qo‘shiladi)

Javoblar: 1. I like apples and bananas. 2. Do you want tea or coffee? 3. So‘nggi qism qo‘shimcha fikr bo‘lsa, and ishlating: The house is big and expensive, and it has a large garden.

6-mashq — O‘zingizdan misol yozing

And bilan bitta gap, but bilan bitta gap, or bilan bitta savol tuzing.

Namunaviy javoblar: I like apples and oranges. It is old but comfortable. Do you want tea or coffee?
