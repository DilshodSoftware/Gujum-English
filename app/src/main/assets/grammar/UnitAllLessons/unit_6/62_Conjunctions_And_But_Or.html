Conjunction — bog‘lovchi. U ikki so‘z, ibora yoki gapni bir-biriga ulaydi. Bu darsda uchta juda ko‘p ishlatiladigan bog‘lovchini o‘rganamiz.

1. And — va

And ikki o‘xshash so‘z yoki fikrni qo‘shadi. O‘zbekchadagi “va” ma’nosiga yaqin.

tea and coffeechoy va qahva
a red and blue bagqizil va ko‘k sumka
Ali and Sara are friends.Ali va Sara do‘stlar.
I read and write.Men o‘qiyman va yozaman.

And ikki odamni subject sifatida birlashtirsa, gapda ikkalasi birga ega bo‘ladi: Ali and Sara are...

2. But — lekin, ammo

But bir-biriga qarama-qarshi yoki kutilmagan ikki fikrni bog‘laydi.

The bag is small but heavy.Sumka kichik, lekin og‘ir.
I like tea, but I don’t like coffee.Men choyni yoqtiraman, ammo qahvani yoqtirmayman.
He is tired, but he is happy.U charchagan, lekin xursand.
It is cold but sunny.Havo sovuq, ammo quyoshli.

3. Or — yoki

Or tanlov yoki ikki imkoniyatdan birini ko‘rsatadi.

Tea or coffee?Choymi yoki qahvami?
You can call or text me.Menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilishingiz yoki xabar yozishingiz mumkin.
Do you want an apple or a banana?Olma xohlaysizmi yoki banan?
We can go today or tomorrow.Bugun yoki ertaga borishimiz mumkin.

4. Bir gapni ikki fikrga ulaymiz

And, but, or ko‘pincha ikki sodda gapni bitta uzunroq gapga aylantiradi.

Ikki sodda gapBog‘langan gap
I like tea. I like coffee.I like tea and coffee.
It is small. It is strong.It is small but strong.
We can walk. We can take a bus.We can walk or take a bus.

5. Qaysi bog‘lovchini tanlaymiz?

And — ma’lumot qo‘shiladi: “va”.
But — qarama-qarshi ma’lumot: “lekin”.
Or — tanlov: “yoki”.
I have a pen ___ a book.Menda ruchka va kitob bor.
The room is small ___ clean.Xona kichik, lekin toza.
Do you want water ___ juice?Suv xohlaysizmi yoki sharbat?

Mashq 1 — And, But yoki Or?

1. I like apples ___ bananas. (ikkalasi ham)

2. The car is old ___ it is reliable. (qarama-qarshi)

3. Do you want tea ___ coffee? (tanlov)

4. She is kind ___ helpful. (ikkita yaxshi sifat)

5. He is tired ___ he is not sleepy. (qarama-qarshi)

Javoblar:
1. and. 2. but. 3. or. 4. and. 5. but.

Mashq 2 — Ikki gapni bitta gapga birlashtiring

1. I have a sister. I have a brother. (and)

2. The phone is cheap. The phone is good. (but)

3. We can eat at home. We can eat at a café. (or)

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. I have a sister and a brother.
2. The phone is cheap but good.
3. We can eat at home or at a café.
