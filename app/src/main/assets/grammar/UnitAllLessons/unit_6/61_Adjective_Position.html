Adjective — sifat, ya’ni odam yoki narsaning qandayligini bildiradi: big — katta, red — qizil, kind — mehribon. Bu darsda sifatni gapning qayeriga qo‘yishni o‘rganamiz.

1. Sifat otning oldidan keladi

Ingliz tilida sifat odatda tasvirlayotgan otning oldida keladi. O‘zbekchada esa ko‘pincha avval sifat, keyin ot aytiladi; inglizchada ham shu tartibga o‘xshash, lekin artikl bo‘lsa u sifatdan oldin turadi.

adjective + noun
a big housekatta uy
a red carqizil mashina
a kind teachermehribon o‘qituvchi
an old bookeski kitob

2. A / An sifatdan oldin keladi

Agar birlikdagi ot oldidan a/an kerak bo‘lsa, artikl sifatning ham oldida turadi. A yoki an tanlashda artikldan keyingi birinchi so‘zning tovushiga qaraymiz.

a / an + adjective + singular noun
a red appleqizil olma
an old houseeski uy
a small orangekichik apelsin
an interesting bookqiziqarli kitob
a small orange deymiz, chunki a dan keyin birinchi bo‘lib small keladi va u undosh tovush bilan boshlanadi. an old house deymiz, chunki old unli tovush bilan boshlanadi.

3. The, My, This ham sifatdan oldin turadi

Ot oldida artikl yoki egalik/ko‘rsatish so‘zi bo‘lsa, u odatda sifatdan oldin keladi.

the big houseo‘sha katta uy
my new phonemening yangi telefonim
this red carbu qizil mashina
those small catsanavi kichik mushuklar
the old book ✅
old the book ❌
my new bag ✅
new my bag ❌

4. Sifat To Be dan keyin keladi

Butun gapda “narsa qanday?” desak, sifat am/is/are dan keyin kelishi mumkin.

Subject + am / is / are + adjective
The house is big.Uy katta.
The car is red.Mashina qizil.
My teachers are kind.O‘qituvchilarim mehribon.
I am tired.Men charchaganman.

Farqiga qarang:

a big housesifat + ot birikmasi
The house is big.to‘liq gap

5. Sifat birlik va ko‘plikda o‘zgarmaydi

Ot ko‘plik bo‘lsa, sifatga -s qo‘shmaymiz. Ko‘plik qo‘shimchasi otga tegishli.

a small dogbitta kichik it
small dogskichik itlar
a red applebitta qizil olma
red applesqizil olmalar
The houses are big. ✅
The houses are bigs. ❌
Big o‘zgarmaydi; ko‘plik houses so‘zida ko‘rsatilgan.

6. Ikki sifat bilan

Bir otni ikkita sifat bilan tasvirlash mumkin. Boshlang‘ich darajada avval rang, hajm yoki yosh bildiruvchi so‘zlarni mashq qiling.

a big red carkatta qizil mashina
a small black catkichik qora mushuk
an old blue bageski ko‘k sumka
Hozircha ko‘p sifat qo‘shib yubormang. Avval a red car kabi bitta sifat bilan ishonchli gap tuzing.

Mashq 1 — So‘zlarni to‘g‘ri tartiblang

1. a / house / big

2. car / red / a

3. is / the room / clean

4. my / new / phone

5. are / the apples / green

Javoblar:
1. a big house. 2. a red car.
3. The room is clean. 4. my new phone.
5. The apples are green.

Mashq 2 — A yoki An ni tanlang

1. (a / an) old car

2. (a / an) small egg

3. (a / an) red apple

4. (a / an) interesting film

Javoblar:
1. an. 2. a. 3. a. 4. an.
