56-AMALIYOT. GOING TO — FUTURE PLANS

Oldindan reja va niyat · darak · inkor · savol
Qolip: ega + am/is/are + going to + fe’lning oddiy shakli. Oldindan qilingan reja yoki niyat uchun ishlatiladi. Inkor: am/is/are not going to. Savolda am/is/are gap boshiga chiqadi.

1-mashq — Am, Is yoki Are?

1. I ___ going to read tonight.

2. My friends ___ going to travel.

3. She ___ going to make a cake.

4. You ___ going to see the doctor.

5. The dog ___ going to eat.

Javoblar: 1. am; 2. are; 3. is; 4. are; 5. is.

2-mashq — Fe’lning oddiy shaklini tanlang

1. We are going to (visit / visited) our aunt.

2. He is going to (buys / buy) a phone.

3. I am going to (study / studying) English.

4. They are going to (play / plays) football.

Javoblar: 1. visit; 2. buy; 3. study; 4. play.

3-mashq — Inkor shaklini tuzing

1. I am going to work tomorrow. (inkor)

2. She is going to cook tonight. (inkor)

3. They are going to travel. (inkor)

Javoblar: 1. I am not going to work tomorrow. 2. She isn’t going to cook tonight. 3. They aren’t going to travel.

4-mashq — Savol shakliga o‘tkazing

1. You are going to study tonight.

2. Ali is going to buy a bike.

3. They are going to move next year.

Javoblar: 1. Are you going to study tonight? 2. Is Ali going to buy a bike? 3. Are they going to move next year?

5-mashq — Kelajak rejasini vaqt iborasi bilan yozing

1. (I / visit / my friend / tomorrow)

2. (We / travel / next week)

3. (She / study / tonight)

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. I am going to visit my friend tomorrow. 2. We are going to travel next week. 3. She is going to study tonight.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. He going to play tennis.

2. They is going to cook.

3. Is she going to studies?

4. Are you going to went home?

Javoblar: 1. He is going to play tennis. 2. They are going to cook. 3. Is she going to study? 4. Are you going to go home?
