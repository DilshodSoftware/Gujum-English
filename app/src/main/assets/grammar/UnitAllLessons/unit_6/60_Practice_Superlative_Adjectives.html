60-AMALIYOT. SUPERLATIVE ADJECTIVES

Eng ... · Guruh ichidan eng yuqori darajani tanlash
Superlative uch yoki undan ortiq odam/narsadan bittasini “eng ...” deb ko‘rsatadi. Ko‘pincha sifat oldidan the keladi: the tallest, the most interesting, the best.

1-mashq — Superlative shaklini yozing

1. tall → ___   2. nice → ___   3. easy → ___

4. big → ___   5. beautiful → ___   6. good → ___

7. bad → ___   8. far → ___

Javoblar: 1. the tallest; 2. the nicest; 3. the easiest; 4. the biggest; 5. the most beautiful; 6. the best; 7. the worst; 8. the farthest.

2-mashq — Qaysi imlo qoidasi?

1. large → largest (faqat -st / -est)

2. happy → happiest (y → iest / y o‘zgarishsiz)

3. hot → hottest (undosh ikkilanadi / most qo‘shiladi)

4. interesting → most interesting (-est / the most)

Javoblar: 1. faqat -st; 2. y→i + est; 3. undosh ikkilanadi; 4. the most.

3-mashq — The ni unutmang

1. This is ___ (small) room in the house.

2. She is ___ (young) person in her family.

3. It is ___ (expensive) restaurant in town.

4. That was ___ (good) day of my holiday.

Javoblar: 1. the smallest; 2. the youngest; 3. the most expensive; 4. the best.

4-mashq — Comparative yoki Superlative?

Ikki narsani solishtirganda comparative; guruh ichidagi “eng” shakli kerak bo‘lsa superlative tanlang.

1. My car is (faster / the fastest) than yours.

2. This is (cheaper / the cheapest) shop in town.

3. Ali is (taller / the tallest) boy in the class.

4. Today is (colder / the coldest) than yesterday.

Javoblar: 1. faster; 2. the cheapest; 3. the tallest; 4. colder.

5-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. This is the most biggest room.

2. She is happiest girl in class.

3. This is the beautifullest picture.

4. He is the most best player.

Javoblar: 1. the biggest room; 2. the happiest girl; 3. the most beautiful picture; 4. the best player.

6-mashq — Guruh ichidan bittasini tanlang

1. Everest is ___ mountain in the group. (high)

2. Of the three bags, this one is ___. (heavy)

Javoblar: 1. the highest; 2. the heaviest.
