57-DARS. WILL — BASIC FUTURE

Will + fe’l · Kelajak haqida aytish
Will kelajakda bo‘ladigan ish, taxmin yoki qarorni bildirishga yordam beradi. O‘zbekchaga vaziyatga qarab “...aman”, “...adi”, “...bo‘ladi” deb tarjima qilinishi mumkin.

1. Will bilan darak gap

Darak gapda will egadan keyin, asosiy fe’l esa undan keyin keladi.

Subject + will + fe’lning oddiy shakli
I will call you tomorrow.Men ertaga sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.
She will help her friend.U dugonasiga yordam beradi.
They will travel next month.Ular kelasi oy sayohat qiladi.
It will be cold tonight.Bugun tunda sovuq bo‘ladi.

2. Will barcha egalar bilan bir xil

I, you, he, she, it, we, they — barchasi bilan will o‘zgarmaydi. He/she/it bilan ham fe’lga -s qo‘shmaymiz.

I will work.Men ishlayman.
He will work.U ishlaydi.
We will work.Biz ishlaymiz.
They will work.Ular ishlaydi.
She will come. ✅
She wills come. ❌
She will comes. ❌
She will to come. ❌

3. Will dan keyin oddiy fe’l keladi

Will dan keyin fe’lning lug‘atdagi oddiy shaklini ishlatamiz. O‘tgan zamon shakli yoki -s qo‘shimchasini qo‘shmaymiz.

will gobormoq / boradi
will eatyemoq / yeydi
will studyo‘qimoq / o‘qiydi
will bebo‘lmoq / bo‘ladi

4. Will qachon ishlatiladi?

Taxmin yoki fikr

I think it will rain tomorrow.Menimcha, ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘adi.
She will be a good teacher.U yaxshi o‘qituvchi bo‘ladi.

Hozir qabul qilingan qaror yoki va’da

I’m tired. I will rest now.Charchadim. Hozir dam olaman.
I will help you.Men sizga yordam beraman.

5. Qisqa shakllar

Gapirishda va kundalik yozuvda will ko‘pincha qisqaradi:

I will → I’llMen ... qilaman
you will → you’llsen/siz ... qilasan/qilasiz
he/she will → he’ll/she’llu ... qiladi
we/they will → we’ll/they’llbiz/ular ... qilamiz/qiladi
I’ll call you later. — Men keyinroq sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.

6. Inkor va savol shakllari bilan tanishuv

Bu darsning asosiy mavzusi darak gap. Keyingi mashqlarda kerak bo‘lishi uchun inkor va savol shakllarini ham ko‘rib qo‘yamiz.

Inkor: I will not go. / I won’t go.Men bormayman.
Savol: Will you come?Siz kelasizmi?
Will o‘zgarmaydi: he will, they will. Savolda will gap boshiga, inkorda not will dan keyin keladi.

7. O‘tgan zamon va kelajakni farqlang

I worked yesterday.Men kecha ishladim. (o‘tgan zamon)
I will work tomorrow.Men ertaga ishlayman. (kelajak)

Mashq 1 — Will + fe’l

1. I ___ (call) you tonight.

2. She ___ (visit) her aunt tomorrow.

3. They ___ (go) to the park next week.

4. He ___ (be) happy.

Javoblar:
1. will call. 2. will visit. 3. will go. 4. will be.

Mashq 2 — Xatolarni tuzating

1. She will goes home.

2. I will to call you.

3. They will studied tomorrow.

Javoblar:
1. She will go home. 2. I will call you. 3. They will study tomorrow.
