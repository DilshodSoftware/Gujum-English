60-DARS. SUPERLATIVE ADJECTIVES

Eng katta, eng kichik, eng yaxshi · Bittasini guruhdagi boshqalar bilan solishtirish
Comparative ikki narsani solishtiradi: kattaroq. Superlative esa uch yoki undan ortiq narsa ichidan eng yuqori darajani ko‘rsatadi: eng katta.

1. Superlative qachon ishlatiladi?

Bir guruhdagi narsalar yoki odamlarni solishtirib, bittasini “eng ...” deb ko‘rsatamiz.

Ali is tall.Ali bo‘yi baland.
Ali is taller than Sam.Ali Samdan balandroq.
Ali is the tallest boy in the class.Ali sinfdagi eng baland bola.

Oxirgi gapda Ali faqat Sam bilan emas, sinfdagi hamma bolalar bilan solishtirilmoqda.

2. Qisqa sifatlar: -est

Ko‘p qisqa sifatlarga -est qo‘shamiz. Superlative oldidan odatda the keladi.

the + adjective-est
SifatSuperlativeMa’nosi
tallthe tallesteng baland
smallthe smallesteng kichik
fastthe fastesteng tez
oldthe oldesteng eski / eng katta yoshli
This is the smallest room.Bu eng kichik xona.
He is the fastest runner.U eng tez yuguruvchi.

3. Sifat E bilan tugasa: -st

Sifat oxirida e bo‘lsa, faqat -st qo‘shamiz.

nice → the nicestchiroyli → eng chiroyli
large → the largestkatta → eng katta
safe → the safestxavfsiz → eng xavfsiz

4. Undosh + Y: Y → IEST

Sifat undosh + y bilan tugasa, y ni i ga o‘zgartirib -est qo‘shamiz.

happy → the happiestxursand → eng xursand
easy → the easiestoson → eng oson
busy → the busiestband → eng band

5. Oxirgi undoshni ikki marta yozish

Ba’zi qisqa sifatlarda oxirgi undosh ikki marta yozilib, -est qo‘shiladi.

big → the biggestkatta → eng katta
hot → the hottestissiq → eng issiq
thin → the thinnestingichka → eng ingichka

6. Uzun sifatlar: The Most

Ko‘p bo‘g‘inli uzun sifatlardan oldin the most ishlatamiz. Bunday sifatga -est qo‘shmaymiz.

the most + adjective
beautiful → the most beautifuleng chiroyli
expensive → the most expensiveeng qimmat
interesting → the most interestingeng qiziqarli
This is the most interesting book.Bu eng qiziqarli kitob.

7. Noto‘g‘ri shaklli sifatlar

Ba’zi sifatlar qoidaga ko‘ra yasalmasdan, butunlay boshqa shaklga o‘tadi.

OddiyComparativeSuperlativeMa’nosi

8. Superlative bilan The ni unutmang

the biggest house ✅
biggest house ❌ (oddiy superlative birikmada the kerak)
the most beautiful flower ✅
the most beautifulest flower ❌ (most va -est ni birga ishlatmang)

Mashq 1 — Superlative shaklni yozing

1. tall → ___   2. large → ___

3. happy → ___   4. big → ___

5. beautiful → ___   6. good → ___

7. bad → ___

Javoblar:
1. the tallest. 2. the largest. 3. the happiest.
4. the biggest. 5. the most beautiful. 6. the best. 7. the worst.

Mashq 2 — To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang

1. This is (the smaller / the smallest) of the three rooms.

2. She is (the most kind / the kindest) person in the family.

3. This is (the best / the goodest) cake.

4. It is (the most expensive / the expensivest) phone in the shop.

Javoblar:
1. the smallest. 2. the kindest. 3. the best. 4. the most expensive.
