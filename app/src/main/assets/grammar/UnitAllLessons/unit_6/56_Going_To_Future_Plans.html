Future — kelajak. Be going to kelajakda qilmoqchi bo‘lgan, oldindan rejalashtirilgan ishni aytishda ko‘p ishlatiladi.

1. Going to nimani bildiradi?

Agar biror ishni kelajakda qilishni oldindan rejalashtirgan bo‘lsak yoki niyat qilgan bo‘lsak, be going to ishlatamiz.

I am going to visit my grandmother tomorrow.Men ertaga buvimnikiga bormoqchiman.
She is going to study tonight.U bugun kechqurun o‘qimoqchi.
They are going to play football.Ular futbol o‘ynamoqchi.

Bu gaplar shunchaki hozir qilinayotgan ish emas. Gapiruvchi kelajak uchun reja yoki niyat haqida xabar beryapti.

2. Asosiy formula

Subject + am / is / are + going to + fe’lning oddiy shakli

Formula ichida uch narsani eslab qoling:

1. Subject — kim?
2. Ega bilan mos am/is/are.
3. Going to dan keyin fe’lning oddiy shakli.
We are going to travel.Biz sayohat qilmoqchimiz.
He is going to buy a phone.U telefon sotib olmoqchi.
I am going to cook dinner.Men kechki ovqat tayyorlamoqchiman.

3. Am / Is / Are ni egaga moslash

O‘tgan darslarda subjectga mos am/is/are ni o‘rgandingiz. Going to da ham o‘sha qoida saqlanadi.

5. Going to dan keyin fe’l o‘zgarmaydi

Going to dan keyin fe’lning oddiy, lug‘atdagi shaklini ishlatamiz. He/she/it bo‘lsa ham bu fe’lga -s qo‘shilmaydi.

She is going to cook. ✅
She is going to cooks. ❌
He is going to play. ✅
He is going to played. ❌

6. Inkor shakli

Reja qilmayotganimizni aytsak, not ni am/is/are dan keyin qo‘yamiz.

Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + verb
I am not going to work tomorrow.Men ertaga ishlamoqchi emasman.
She isn’t going to come.U kelmoqchi emas.
They aren’t going to travel.Ular sayohat qilmoqchi emas.

7. Savol shakli

Savol tuzishda am/is/are gap boshiga o‘tadi.

Am / Is / Are + subject + going to + verb?
Are you going to study tonight?Siz bugun kechqurun o‘qimoqchimisiz?
Is Ali going to buy a car?Ali mashina sotib olmoqchimi?
What are they going to do?Ular nima qilmoqchi?

Mashq 1 — Am, Is yoki Are?

1. I ___ going to read tonight.

2. He ___ going to work tomorrow.

3. We ___ going to visit our aunt.

4. Sara ___ going to cook dinner.

5. They ___ going to play football.

Javoblar:
1. am. 2. is. 3. are. 4. is. 5. are.
