58-AMALIYOT. GOING TO vs WILL

Oldindan reja · hozirgi qaror · va’da/taxmin · ko‘rinib turgan belgi
Going to — oldindan reja yoki ko‘rinib turgan dalilga asoslangan taxmin. Will — ayni paytdagi qaror, va’da yoki fikrga asoslangan taxmin. Vaziyatga qarab ba’zan ikkalasi ham mumkin.

1-mashq — Vaziyatga mosini tanlang

1. Rejam tayyor: I (am going to / will) visit my aunt on Saturday.

2. Telefon jiringlaydi: “Men javob beraman!” I (am going to / will) answer it.

3. Qora bulutlar ko‘rinib turibdi: It (is going to / will) rain.

4. Va’da: I (am going to / will) help you tomorrow.

5. Menimcha, jamoa yutadi: The team (is going to / will) win.

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. am going to; 2. will; 3. is going to; 4. will; 5. will.

2-mashq — Qolipni to‘ldiring

1. She ___ going to study. (is / will)

2. They ___ going to move. (are / will)

3. I ___ call you later. (will / am)

4. He is going to ___ dinner. (cook / cooking)

5. We will ___ them. (visit / visited)

Javoblar: 1. is; 2. are; 3. will; 4. cook; 5. visit.

3-mashq — Inkor shaklini tanlang

1. Oldindan reja: She ___ going to travel. (isn’t / won’t)

2. Will inkori: They ___ come tonight. (aren’t / won’t)

3. Reja qilmaganman: I ___ going to buy a car. (am not / will not going)

Javoblar: 1. isn’t; 2. won’t; 3. am not.

4-mashq — Savolni tuzing

1. Reja haqida: (you / travel / next week / going to)

2. Will savoli: (she / call / tonight)

Javoblar: 1. Are you going to travel next week? 2. Will she call tonight?

5-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. I going to study tomorrow.

2. She will goes shopping.

3. They are going to played tennis.

4. He is going to will call us.

Javoblar: 1. I am going to study tomorrow. 2. She will go shopping. 3. They are going to play tennis. 4. He is going to call us / He will call us.

6-mashq — Vaziyatlarni o‘zingiz belgilang

1. Ertaga qilmoqchi bo‘lgan oldindan rejangiz.

2. Do‘stingizga beradigan va’dangiz.

3. Hozir ko‘rayotgan osmoningizga qarab taxmin.

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. I’m going to study tomorrow. 2. I will help you. 3. It is going to rain.
