57-AMALIYOT. WILL — BASIC FUTURE

Will + oddiy fe’l · taxmin · va’da · hozirgi qaror
Will barcha egalar bilan bir xil. Undan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklda keladi. Inkor: will not / won’t. Savol: Will + ega + fe’l?

1-mashq — Will bilan fe’lni to‘ldiring

1. I ___ (call) you tomorrow.

2. He ___ (be) happy.

3. We ___ (visit) our friends next week.

4. They ___ (go) home later.

5. Sara ___ (help) her mother.

Javoblar: 1. will call; 2. will be; 3. will visit; 4. will go; 5. will help.

2-mashq — Qavsdan to‘g‘ri variantni tanlang

1. She will (come / comes) later.

2. I will (to help / help) you.

3. They will (travel / travelled) next month.

4. He’ll (be / is) a good doctor.

Javoblar: 1. come; 2. help; 3. travel; 4. be.

3-mashq — Inkor va savol tuzing

1. We will go tomorrow. (inkor)

2. She will call me. (savol)

3. They will be late. (inkor)

4. You will help us. (savol)

Javoblar: 1. We won’t go tomorrow. 2. Will she call me? 3. They won’t be late. 4. Will you help us?

4-mashq — Qisqa shaklga o‘tkazing

1. I will → ___   2. you will → ___

3. she will → ___   4. they will → ___

Javoblar: 1. I’ll; 2. you’ll; 3. she’ll; 4. they’ll.

5-mashq — Vaziyatga mos gap tuzing

1. Va’da: “Men senga yordam beraman.”

2. Hozir qaror: “Charchadim. Hozir dam olaman.”

3. Taxmin: “Menimcha, ertaga yomg‘ir yog‘adi.”

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. I will help you. 2. I’m tired. I’ll rest now. 3. I think it will rain tomorrow.

6-mashq — Xatolarni tuzating

1. He will goes home.

2. I will to call you.

3. Will she comes tomorrow?

4. They won’t be go there.

Javoblar: 1. He will go home. 2. I will call you. 3. Will she come tomorrow? 4. They won’t go there.
