5-AMALIYOT. LETTER COMBINATIONS

Sh, ch, th, ph, wh, ck, ng ni tanish va ishlatish
Topshiriqdan oldin eslang: harf birikmasi — ikki harf yonma-yon kelib, ko‘pincha bitta tovush beradigan holat. Ba’zi birikmalarning istisnolari bor, shuning uchun misol so‘zlarni ham yodlang.

1-mashq — Bo‘sh joyga birikmani qo‘ying

shchthphwhckng

1. fi__ (baliq)

2. __air (stul)

3. __ink (o‘ylamoq)

4. __one (telefon)

5. __at (nima)

6. clo__ (soat)

7. si__ (kuylamoq)

Javoblar:
1. fish — sh. 2. chair — ch. 3. think — th.
4. phone — ph. 5. what — wh. 6. clock — ck. 7. sing — ng.

2-mashq — Birikmani ta’rif bilan moslang

sh   ph   ck   th

a) ko‘pincha “f”   b) maxsus inglizcha tovushlar   c) “sh”   d) “k”ga yaqin

Javoblar:
sh—c. ph—a. ck—d. th—b.

3-mashq — Qaysi so‘zda birikma boshqacha o‘qiladi?

1. Ko‘pincha “ch” tovushida: (chair / chef)

2. “sh”ga yaqin ch tovushi: (child / chef)

3. “k”ga yaqin ch tovushi: (chorus / chair)

4. phone so‘zidagi ph odatda qaysi tovushga yaqin? (p / f / h)

Javoblar:
1. chair. 2. chef. 3. chorus. 4. f.

4-mashq — TH jaranglimi yoki jarangsizmi?

So‘zni aytganda tomoq titrash-titramasligini tekshiring.

Jarangsiz th: think, ______

Jarangli th: this, ______

Tanlang: mother, bath.

Javoblar:
Jarangsiz: think, bath. Jarangli: this, mother.

5-mashq — To‘g‘ri yoki noto‘g‘ri?

1. ship so‘zida sh ikki alohida tovush qilib o‘qiladi. (T / N)

2. phone so‘zida ph “f”ga yaqin tovush beradi. (T / N)

3. ch har doim bir xil o‘qiladi. (T / N)

4. think va this so‘zlaridagi th bir xil tovushdir. (T / N)

Javoblar:
1. N. 2. T. 3. N. 4. N.

6-mashq — So‘zni bo‘lib emas, birikma bilan o‘qing

Quyidagi so‘zlarda ikki harfli birikmani belgilang va ovoz chiqarib o‘qing:

shipchairthinkphotoblacksong
Tekshirish:
ship—sh; chair—ch; think—th; photo—ph; black—ck; song—ng.
