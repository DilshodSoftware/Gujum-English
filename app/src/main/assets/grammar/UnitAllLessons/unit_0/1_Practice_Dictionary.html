1-AMALIYOT. BOSHLANG‘ICH LUG‘AT

So‘zlarni tanish · Ma’nosini eslash · Guruhlarga ajratish
Bajarish tartibi: avval inglizcha so‘zni o‘qing, ma’nosini eslashga harakat qiling, keyin javoblar kalitidan tekshiring. Bilmagan so‘zni lug‘atdan topib, ovoz chiqarib takrorlang. Inglizcha so‘z yonidagi 🔊 tugmasini bosib talaffuzini eshiting.

1-mashq — Inglizcha so‘zni ma’nosi bilan moslang

1.apple
2.book
3.cat
4.dog
5.teacher
6.school
7.water
8.friend
9.chair
10.phone

Ma’nolari: do‘st · suv · telefon · mushuk · stul · maktab · olma · o‘qituvchi · it · kitob

Javoblar:
1. olma. 2. kitob. 3. mushuk. 4. it. 5. o‘qituvchi.
6. maktab. 7. suv. 8. do‘st. 9. stul. 10. telefon.

2-mashq — So‘zlarni guruhlarga ajrating

mothercatschoolpenteacherdogparkbook

Odamlar: ______

Hayvonlar: ______

Joylar: ______

Narsalar: ______

Javoblar:
Odamlar: mother, teacher.
Hayvonlar: cat, dog.
Joylar: school, park.
Narsalar: pen, book.

4-mashq — Ta’rifga mos so‘zni tanlang

1. “Suv” qaysi so‘z? (water / rain / juice)

2. “Qiz” qaysi so‘z? (girl / boy / teacher)

3. “Kitob” qaysi so‘z? (book / box / pen)

4. “Maktab” qaysi so‘z? (school / chair / park)

5. “Oy” qaysi so‘z? (moon / sun / tree)

Javoblar:
1. water. 2. girl. 3. book. 4. school. 5. moon.

5-mashq — Katta harfni to‘g‘ri qo‘ying

Ingliz tilida I olmoshi, odam va joy nomlari, hafta kunlari hamda til nomlari katta harf bilan yoziladi.

1. i am Ali.

2. she lives in tashkent.

3. we study english on monday.

4. my friend is from uzbekistan.

Javoblar:
1. I am Ali.
2. She lives in Tashkent.
3. We study English on Monday.
4. My friend is from Uzbekistan.

6-mashq — So‘zlarni harflab ayting

Har bir so‘zning yozilishidagi harf nomlarini ketma-ket ayting. Harf nomi so‘z ichidagi tovush bilan bir xil bo‘lmasligi mumkin.

1.CAT
2.PEN
3.BOX
Namuna:
CAT = C — A — T. PEN = P — E — N. BOX = B — O — X.

7-mashq — O‘rgangan iboralarni tushuning

1. I am a student. = ______

2. Monday is my favorite day. = ______

3. My teacher speaks English. = ______

Javoblar:
1. Men o‘quvchiman. 2. Dushanba mening sevimli kunim.
3. O‘qituvchim ingliz tilida gapiradi.
