How boshqa so‘zlar bilan qo‘shilib, aniqroq savol beradi. Bu darsda yoshni so‘rash va vaqt yoki uzunlikni o‘lchashni o‘rganamiz.

1. How old? — Necha yosh?

How old? odamning yoshini so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi. Savolda am/is/are ni subject bilan moslaymiz.

How old + am / is / are + subject?
How old are you?Siz necha yoshdasiz?
How old is your brother?Akangiz/ukangiz necha yoshda?
How old is Sara?Sara necha yoshda?
How old are they?Ular necha yoshda?

2. Yoshga javob berish

Ingliz tilida yoshni aytganda am/is/are ishlatiladi. O‘zbek tilidan so‘zma-so‘z tarjima qilib “have” ishlatmang.

I am 12 years old.Men 12 yoshdaman.
He is 8 years old.U 8 yoshda.
My sister is 20.Opam/singlim 20 yoshda.
She is 15 years old. ✅
She has 15 years. ❌
Yosh haqida inglizcha gapda be fe’li ishlatiladi.

3. How long? — Qancha vaqt?

How long? biror ish qancha vaqt davom etishini so‘rashi mumkin. Javobda daqiqa, soat, kun yoki yil kabi vaqt aytiladi.

How long is the lesson?Dars qancha davom etadi?
How long do you study every day?Har kuni qancha vaqt o‘qiysiz?
How long does the film last?Film qancha davom etadi?
For 30 minutes.30 daqiqa davomida.
For two hours.Ikki soat davomida.
For three days.Uch kun davomida.

Oddiy fe’l bilan savol tuzganda do/does ishlatamiz: How long do you study?, How long does he work?

4. How long? — Qanchalik uzun?

How long jismoniy uzunlikni ham so‘rashi mumkin. Qaysi ma’no ekanini vaziyatdan bilamiz.

How long is this table?Bu stolning uzunligi qancha?
It is two metres long.Uning uzunligi ikki metr.
How long is the river?Daryo qanchalik uzun?
How old — yosh haqida.
How long — davomiy vaqt yoki uzunlik haqida.

5. When va How long farqi

When ish qachon bo‘lishini so‘raydi. How long esa qancha vaqt davom etishini so‘raydi.

When does the lesson start?Dars qachon boshlanadi?
How long is the lesson?Dars qancha davom etadi?

Mashq 1 — How old yoki How long?

1. ___ are you? (yosh)

2. ___ do you study each day? (davomiy vaqt)

3. ___ is your sister? (yosh)

4. ___ is the bridge? (uzunlik)

5. ___ does the bus ride take? (davomiy vaqt)

Javoblar:
1. How old. 2. How long. 3. How old.
4. How long. 5. How long.

Mashq 2 — To Be, Do yoki Does?

1. How old ___ your brother? (is / does)

2. How long ___ you wait? (do / are)

3. How long ___ the bridge? (is / does)

4. How long ___ the lesson last? (does / is)

5. How old ___ the children? (are / do)

Javoblar:
1. is. 2. do. 3. is. 4. does. 5. are.
