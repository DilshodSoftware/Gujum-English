35-DARS. HOW?

How = qanday? Qay tarzda?
How biror ish qanday bajarilishini yoki odamning holati qandayligini so‘raydi. Javobda usul, holat yoki sifatni aytamiz.

1. How are you? — Ahvol so‘rash

How are you? ingliz tilidagi eng ko‘p ishlatiladigan salomlashuv savollaridan biri. Bu “Siz qanday holatdasiz?” degani; odatda ahvolni so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi.

How are you?Qalaysiz? / Ahvolingiz qanday?
I’m fine, thank you.Yaxshiman, rahmat.
I’m good.Yaxshiman.
I’m tired today.Bugun charchaganman.

How savoliga faqat fine deb emas, to‘liq gap bilan ham javob berish mumkin: I am fine.

2. How + To Be — holat yoki sifat

Odam yoki narsa qandayligini so‘rashda how dan keyin am/is/are ishlatilishi mumkin.

How + am / is / are + subject?
How is your mother?Onangiz qalay?
How are the children?Bolalar qalay?
How is the food?Ovqat qanday?
How are you today?Bugun ahvolingiz qanday?

3. How do you ...? — Ish qanday bajariladi?

Harakatni qanday usulda qilishni so‘rasak, Present Simple da do/does ishlatamiz.

How + do / does + subject + verb?
How do you go to school?Maktabga qanday borasiz?
How does he cook rice?U guruchni qanday pishiradi?
How do they learn English?Ular ingliz tilini qanday o‘rganadi?
How do you go to school? — By bus.Maktabga qanday borasiz? — Avtobusda.
How does she travel? — By train.U qanday sayohat qiladi? — Poyezdda.
How do you spell your name? — A-L-I.Ismingizni harflab ayting. — A-L-I.

4. How va What farqi

What narsa yoki ma’lumotni so‘raydi; how esa holat yoki usulni so‘raydi.

What is your name?Ismingiz nima? (ism so‘raladi)
How are you?Ahvolingiz qanday? (holat so‘raladi)
How do you go to work?Ishga qanday borasiz? (usul so‘raladi)

5. How keyingi darslarda boshqa so‘zlar bilan keladi

How boshqa so‘z bilan birikib, alohida savol ma’nosini berishi mumkin. Keyingi darslarda batafsil o‘rganamiz.

How old?Necha yosh?
How long?Qancha vaqt? Qanchalik uzun?
How many?Nechta?
How much?Qancha?

Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

How do you travel? ✅
How you travel? ❌

How does she cook? ✅
How does she cooks? ❌

How are you? ✅
How do you? ❌ (ahvolni so‘raganda)

Mashq 1 — Do, Does yoki To Be?

1. How ___ you? (are / do)

2. How ___ you go to work? (are / do)

3. How ___ your sister? (is / does)

4. How ___ he make tea? (do / does)

Javoblar:
1. are. 2. do. 3. is. 4. does.

Mashq 2 — Savolga mos javobni tanlang

1. How do you go to school? (By bus. / At seven.)

2. How are you? (I’m fine. / I’m Ali.)

3. How do you spell “Sam”? (S-A-M. / In Tashkent.)

Javoblar:
1. By bus. 2. I’m fine. 3. S-A-M.
