31-DARS. WHERE?

Where = qayerda? Qayerga?
Where odam yoki narsa qayerda turganini, qayerda yashashini yoki qaysi joyga borishini so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi. Javobda odatda joy nomi keladi.

1. Where + To Be — qayerdaligini so‘rash

Am/is/are bilan savol tuzishda avval where, keyin to be, undan keyin ega keladi.

Where + am / is / are + subject?
Where are you?Siz qayerdasiz?
Where is my phone?Telefonim qayerda?
Where is Ali?Ali qayerda?
Where are the children?Bolalar qayerda?

Javobda joyni aytamiz:

Where are you? — I am at home.Qayerdasiz? — Men uydaman.
Where is the book? — It is on the table.Kitob qayerda? — U stol ustida.
Where are they? — They are at school.Ular qayerda? — Ular maktabda.

2. At, In, On bilan joy aytish

Joyni ko‘rsatganda ingliz tilida at, in, on kabi kichik so‘zlar ko‘pincha joy nomidan oldin keladi.

So‘zOddiy misolMa’nosi
atat home / at schooluyda / maktabda
inin the room / in Tashkentxona ichida / Toshkentda
onon the tablestol ustida
Hozircha birikmalarni tayyor holda yodlang: at home, at school, in Tashkent, on the table.

3. Where + Do / Does — harakat qayerda?

Oddiy harakat fe’li bilan Present Simple savolida do/does ishlatamiz. He/she/it yoki bitta ism bilan does, qolgan egalar bilan do keladi.

Where + do / does + subject + fe’l?
Where do you live?Siz qayerda yashaysiz?
Where do they study?Ular qayerda o‘qiydi?
Where does your father work?Otangiz qayerda ishlaydi?
Where does this bus stop?Bu avtobus qayerda to‘xtaydi?
Where does she live? ✅
Where does she lives? ❌
Does dan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklda: live.

4. Where do you go? — Qayerga borasiz?

Where bilan “qayerga borish”ni ham so‘raymiz. Ingliz tilida go fe’lidan keyin ko‘pincha joyni aytamiz.

Where do you go after school?Maktabdan keyin qayerga borasiz?
Where does he go on Sundays?U yakshanba kunlari qayerga boradi?
I go to the park.Men bog‘ga boraman.

5. Where savoliga qanday javob beramiz?

Where savoliga “ha/yo‘q” emas, joy aytib javob beramiz.

Where does Sara work? — She works at a school.Sara qayerda ishlaydi? — U maktabda ishlaydi.
Where do you live? — I live in Samarkand.Qayerda yashaysiz? — Men Samarqandda yashayman.
Where is the cat? — It is under the table.Mushuk qayerda? — U stol tagida.

Mashq 1 — Where bilan savolni to‘ldiring

1. ___ are my shoes?

2. ___ does your mother work?

3. ___ do they live?

4. ___ is the bus stop?

Javoblar:
1–4. Where.

Javoblar:
