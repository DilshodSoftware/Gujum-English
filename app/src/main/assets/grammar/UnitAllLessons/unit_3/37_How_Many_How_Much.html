37-DARS. HOW MANY / HOW MUCH

How many = nechta? · How much = qancha?
Eng muhim savol: sanalayotgan narsa alohida dona-dona bo‘ladimi? Ha bo‘lsa, odatda how many. Sut, suv, guruch yoki vaqt kabi miqdor bilan o‘lchanadigan narsalarda how much.

1. Countable va Uncountable — sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan

Countable — donalab sanash mumkin

Kitob, olma, odam kabi narsalarni one, two, three deb sanash mumkin. Ko‘plikda ular ko‘pincha -s/-es oladi.

one book — three booksbitta kitob — uchta kitob
one apple — five applesbitta olma — beshta olma
one student — ten studentsbitta o‘quvchi — o‘nta o‘quvchi

Uncountable — odatda donalab sanalmaydi

Suv, sut, guruch, pul va vaqt kabi narsalarni odatda bitta-bitta sanamaymiz. Ularning miqdorini o‘lchov bilan aytishimiz mumkin: bir stakan suv, ikki kilo guruch.

watersuv
milksut
riceguruch
moneypul
timevaqt

2. How Many? — Nechta?

How many donalab sanash mumkin bo‘lgan otlar bilan ishlatiladi. Undan keyingi ot odatda ko‘plikda bo‘ladi.

How many + ko‘plikdagi ot ...?
How many books do you have?Sizda nechta kitob bor?
How many apples does she eat?U nechta olma yeydi?
How many students are in the class?Sinfda nechta o‘quvchi bor?
How many brothers do they have?Ularning nechta akasi/ukasi bor?
How many book demaymiz. Book sanaladi va bir nechtasi haqida so‘rayapmiz: How many books?

3. How Much? — Qancha?

How much odatda donalab sanalmaydigan otlar bilan ishlatiladi.

How much + sanalmaydigan ot ...?
How much water do you drink?Siz qancha suv ichasiz?
How much rice do we need?Bizga qancha guruch kerak?
How much money does he have?Unda qancha pul bor?
How much time do you need?Sizga qancha vaqt kerak?
Uncountable otni idish yoki o‘lchov bilan sanash mumkin: two bottles of water — ikki shisha suv. Lekin suvning o‘zini so‘rasak how much water deymiz.

4. How Much? — Narxni so‘rash

Narx haqida so‘raganda ham how much ishlatiladi. Bu yerda “qancha turadi?” degan ma’no beradi.

How much is this book?Bu kitob qancha turadi?
How much are these shoes?Bu oyoq kiyimlar qancha turadi?
How much is the ticket?Chipta qancha turadi?

6. Do / Does bilan savol qolipi

Oddiy Present Simple fe’li bo‘lsa, how many/much dan keyin do yoki does kelishi mumkin.

How many/much + noun + do/does + subject + verb?
How many books do they read?Ular nechta kitob o‘qiydi?
How much milk does the baby drink?Chaqaloq qancha sut ichadi?
Does dan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklda: How much milk does he drink? — drink, drinks emas.

Mashq 1 — Many yoki Much?

1. How ___ chairs are there?

2. How ___ milk do you drink?

3. How ___ money do you need?

4. How ___ students are in your class?

5. How ___ time do we have?

6. How ___ oranges do they buy?

Javoblar:
1. many. 2. much. 3. much. 4. many. 5. much. 6. many.

Mashq 2 — Do yoki Does?

1. How many books ___ you read?

2. How much water ___ she drink?

3. How many brothers ___ he have?

4. How much rice ___ they eat?

Javoblar:
1. do. 2. does. 3. does. 4. do.

Mashq 3 — Savol so‘zini tanlang

1. (How many / How much) apples do you want?

2. (How many / How much) juice is in the glass?

3. (How many / How much) is this sandwich?

Javoblar:
1. How many. 2. How much. 3. How much.
