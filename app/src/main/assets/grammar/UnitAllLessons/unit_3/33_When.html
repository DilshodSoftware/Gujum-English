When biror ish qachon bo‘lishini, qaysi kuni yoki qaysi vaqtda sodir bo‘lishini so‘raydi. Javobda vaqt, kun, oy yoki vaqt iborasi keladi.

1. When + To Be — voqea qachon?

Gapda am/is/are bo‘lsa, to be savol tuzishda When dan keyin keladi.

When + am / is / are + subject?
When is your birthday?Tug‘ilgan kuningiz qachon?
When is the lesson?Dars qachon?
When are your holidays?Ta’tilingiz qachon?
When are they free?Ular qachon bo‘sh?

2. When + Do / Does — harakat qachon?

Oddiy harakat fe’li bilan so‘rasak, do/does yordam beradi. Ega he/she/it yoki bitta ism bo‘lsa does, I/you/we/they bo‘lsa do ishlatamiz.

When + do / does + subject + verb?
When do you wake up?Siz qachon uyg‘onasiz?
When do they study?Ular qachon o‘qiydi?
When does she go to work?U qachon ishga boradi?
When does the bus arrive?Avtobus qachon yetib keladi?
Does dan keyin asosiy fe’l oddiy shaklda qoladi: When does she go? deymiz, goes emas.

3. When savoliga vaqt bilan javob beramiz

Javobda kun, soat yoki boshqa vaqtni aytamiz.

When do you study?I study in the evening. — Men kechqurun o‘qiyman.
When does the film start?It starts at seven. — U soat yettida boshlanadi.
When is your birthday?It is in May. — U may oyida.

1. When ___ you eat breakfast?

2. When ___ she finish work?
