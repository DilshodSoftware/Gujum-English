37-AMALIYOT. HOW MANY / HOW MUCH

Nechta? · Qancha? · Sanaladigan, sanalmaydigan va narx
How many + ko‘plikdagi sanaladigan ot: how many books? How much + sanalmaydigan ot: how much water? Narxni so‘raganda ham how much ishlatiladi.

1-mashq — Many yoki Much?

1. How ___ pencils are in your bag?

2. How ___ juice do you want?

3. How ___ money is in your wallet?

4. How ___ students are in the room?

5. How ___ time do we have?

6. How ___ eggs do we need?

Javoblar: 1. many; 2. much; 3. much; 4. many; 5. much; 6. many.

2-mashq — Otni sanash mumkinmi?

So‘zlarni sanaladigan yoki sanalmaydigan guruhga ajrating: apple, rice, chair, water, money, book, milk, student, time.

Javoblar: Sanaladigan: apple, chair, book, student. Sanalmaydigan: rice, water, money, milk, time.

3-mashq — Do yoki Does bilan savolni tugating

1. How many books ___ you read?

2. How much milk ___ the baby drink?

3. How many sisters ___ Ali have?

4. How much rice ___ they eat?

Javoblar: 1. do; 2. does; 3. does; 4. do.

4-mashq — Narxni so‘rang

1. ___ ___ is this sandwich? (qancha turadi?)

2. ___ ___ are these shoes?

3. ___ ___ is the ticket?

Javoblar: 1–3. How much.

5-mashq — Xatolarni tuzating

1. How much chairs are there?

2. How many water do you drink?

3. How many books does you have?

4. How many money does he need?

5. How much are this apples? (narx)

Javoblar:
1. How many chairs are there? 2. How much water do you drink?
3. How many books do you have? 4. How much money does he need?
5. How much are these apples?

6-mashq — Savol va javobni moslang

1. How many books do you have?   A. Two litres.

2. How much water do you need?   B. Five.

3. How much is the pen?   C. Three dollars.

Javoblar: 1–B; 2–A; 3–C.
