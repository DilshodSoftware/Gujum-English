35-AMALIYOT. HOW?

Qanday? · Ahvol, holat va usul
How are you? ahvolni so‘raydi va to be bilan keladi. How do you…? biror ish qanday bajarilishini so‘raydi va do/does bilan keladi. Usulni javobda aytamiz: By bus.

1-mashq — Are/Is yoki Do/Does?

1. How ___ you today? (are / do)

2. How ___ your father travel to work? (does / is)

3. How ___ the food? (is / does)

4. How ___ they learn new words? (do / are)

5. How ___ your mother? (is / does)

Javoblar: 1. are; 2. does; 3. is; 4. do; 5. is.

2-mashq — Savolga mos javobni tanlang

1. How do you go to work? (By bus. / At 8:00.)

2. How are you? (I’m fine. / I’m a doctor.)

3. How does she travel? (By train. / In Tashkent.)

4. How do you spell “Mina”? (M-I-N-A. / At school.)

Javoblar: 1. By bus. 2. I’m fine. 3. By train. 4. M-I-N-A.

3-mashq — So‘zlarni tartiblang

1. are / how / you / ?

2. how / go / do / they / to school / ?

3. your sister / how / is / ?

4. does / how / he / cook / rice / ?

Javoblar: 1. How are you? 2. How do they go to school? 3. How is your sister? 4. How does he cook rice?

4-mashq — What yoki How?

1. ___ is your name? — My name is Lola.

2. ___ are you? — I’m good.

3. ___ do you travel? — By car.

4. ___ is your favorite color? — Blue.

Javoblar: 1. What; 2. How; 3. How; 4. What.

5-mashq — Xatoni topib tuzating

1. How you go to school?

2. How does she travels?

3. How do you? (ahvol so‘rash)

4. How is your brother go to work?

Javoblar: 1. How do you go to school? 2. How does she travel? 3. How are you? 4. How does your brother go to work?

6-mashq — O‘zingiz haqida javob bering

1. How are you today?

2. How do you come to school or work?

3. How do you spell your first name?

Namunaviy javoblar: I’m fine, thank you. I come by bus. My name is spelled A-L-I.
