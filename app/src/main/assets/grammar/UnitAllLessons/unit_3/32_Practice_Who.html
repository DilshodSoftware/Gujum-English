32-AMALIYOT. WHO?

Kim? · Who subject bo‘lsa yoki object bo‘lsa
Who is…? odamning kimligini so‘raydi. Who do/does + ega + fe’l? kimni/kimga savol qiladi. Ammo Who gapning egasi, ya’ni ishni bajaruvchi bo‘lsa, odatda do/does qo‘ymaymiz: Who lives here?

1-mashq — Who yoki What?

1. ___ is that woman? (odam)

2. ___ is in the box? (narsa)

3. ___ is your doctor? (odam)

4. ___ do you eat for lunch? (narsa/taom)

Javoblar: 1. Who; 2. What; 3. Who; 4. What.

2-mashq — Who is yoki Who are?

1. Who ___ your English teacher?

2. Who ___ those people?

3. Who ___ she?

4. Who ___ your friends?

Javoblar: 1. is; 2. are; 3. is; 4. are.

3-mashq — Who gapning egasimi yoki objectmi?

Kim harakatni bajarayotganini tekshiring.

1. Who calls you? (kim qo‘ng‘iroq qilyapti?)

2. Who do you call? (siz kimga qo‘ng‘iroq qilasiz?)

3. Who works at the shop?

4. Who does Anna visit?

Javoblar: Who ega/subject: 1, 3 — do/does yo‘q. Who object: 2, 4 — do/does ishlatiladi.

4-mashq — Do, Does yoki bo‘sh joy?

1. Who ___ you help?

2. Who ___ lives next door?

3. Who ___ he visit?

4. Who ___ wants some tea?

5. Who ___ they call?

Javoblar: 1. do; 2. bo‘sh; 3. does; 4. bo‘sh; 5. do.

5-mashq — So‘zlarni tartibga soling

1. your teacher / who / is / ?

2. who / here / works / ?

3. does / who / she / call / ?

4. who / they / visit / do / ?

Javoblar: 1. Who is your teacher? 2. Who works here? 3. Who does she call? 4. Who do they visit?

6-mashq — Javobdan savol tuzing

1. Javob: He is my brother. Savol: ___ is he?

2. Javob: I visit my grandmother. Savol: ___ do you visit?

3. Javob: Sara works here. Savol: ___ works here?

Javoblar: 1. Who; 2. Who; 3. Who.
