31-AMALIYOT. WHERE?

Qayerda? Qayerga? · Joy va manzil
Where joyni so‘raydi. Joylashuv/to be: Where is the book? Harakat bilan: Where do you live? He/she/it yoki bitta ism bilan does keladi; fe’l oddiy shaklda qoladi.

1-mashq — Be yoki Do/Does?

1. Where ___ your keys? (are / do)

2. Where ___ she work? (is / does)

3. Where ___ the children? (are / do)

4. Where ___ you go after school? (are / do)

5. Where ___ the bus stop? (is / does)

Javoblar: 1. are; 2. does; 3. are; 4. do; 5. is.

2-mashq — Joy predlogini tanlang

at home, at school, in a room/city, on a surface kabi o‘rgangan birikmalarni qo‘llang.

1. The students are ___ school.

2. My phone is ___ the table.

3. We live ___ Tashkent.

4. The children are ___ the room.

5. My father is ___ home.

Javoblar: 1. at; 2. on; 3. in; 4. in; 5. at.

3-mashq — So‘zlarni joyiga qo‘ying

1. you / where / live / do / ?

2. the cat / where / is / ?

3. your parents / where / work / do / ?

4. where / the children / are / ?

Javoblar: 1. Where do you live? 2. Where is the cat? 3. Where do your parents work? 4. Where are the children?

4-mashq — Where savoliga javob yozing

1. Where is your book? (in my bag)

2. Where do you live? (in Bukhara)

3. Where does your mother work? (at a hospital)

4. Where are the children? (at school)

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. It is in my bag. 2. I live in Bukhara. 3. She works at a hospital. 4. They are at school.

5-mashq — Where yoki When?

1. ___ do you study? — At the library.

2. ___ does the lesson start? — At nine.

3. ___ is your bag? — Under the chair.

4. ___ do they visit their aunt? — On Sundays.

Javoblar: 1. Where; 2. When; 3. Where; 4. When.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. Where you live?

2. Where does he works?

3. Where is live your sister?

4. Where do the keys?

Javoblar: 1. Where do you live? 2. Where does he work? 3. Where does your sister live? 4. Where are the keys?
