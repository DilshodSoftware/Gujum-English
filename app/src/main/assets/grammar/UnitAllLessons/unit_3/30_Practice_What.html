30-AMALIYOT. WHAT?

Nima? · Narsa yoki ma’lumotni so‘rash
What narsa yoki ma’lumotni so‘raydi. To be bilan: What is this? Harakat fe’li bilan: What do you like? He/she/it uchun does ishlatiladi va undan keyingi fe’l oddiy shaklda qoladi.

1-mashq — What savoliga javob turini toping

1. What is this? (narsa / joy)

2. What is your name? (ism / sabab)

3. What do you eat? (taom / vaqt)

4. What color is the bag? (rang / odam)

Javoblar: 1. narsa; 2. ism; 3. taom; 4. rang.

2-mashq — Is, Are, Do yoki Does?

1. What ___ these? (are / do)

2. What ___ your favorite food? (is / does)

3. What ___ you do after school? (do / are)

4. What ___ Sara read? (does / is)

5. What ___ in the box? (is / does)

Javoblar: 1. are; 2. is; 3. do; 4. does; 5. is.

3-mashq — Savolni to‘g‘ri tartibda yozing

1. your name / what / is / ?

2. do / what / they / study / ?

3. she / what / does / want / ?

4. is / what / in the bag / ?

Javoblar: 1. What is your name? 2. What do they study? 3. What does she want? 4. What is in the bag?

4-mashq — What yoki Where?

1. ___ is your phone? — On the table.

2. ___ is your phone? — It is a new model.

3. ___ do you eat for breakfast? — Eggs.

4. ___ do you study? — At home.

Javoblar: 1. Where; 2. What; 3. What; 4. Where.

5-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. What you want?

2. What does he likes?

3. What this is?

4. What are your name?

Javoblar: 1. What do you want? 2. What does he like? 3. What is this? 4. What is your name?

6-mashq — Savolga mos javob yozing

1. What is this? (a pencil)

2. What do you like? (music)

3. What does Ali eat? (rice)

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. It is a pencil. 2. I like music. 3. Ali eats rice.
