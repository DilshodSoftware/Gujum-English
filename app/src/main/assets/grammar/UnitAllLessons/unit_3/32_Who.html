Who odamning kimligini yoki kim biror ish qilayotganini so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi. Javobda odatda odamning ismi, kasbi yoki olmoshi keladi.

1. Who is ...? — “U kim?”

Biror odamning kimligini yoki kasbini so‘rasak, Who + am/is/are + ...? qolipidan foydalanamiz.

Who + am / is / are + ...?
Who is he?U kim?
Who is your teacher?O‘qituvchingiz kim?
Who are they?Ular kim?
Who is that woman?Anavi ayol kim?
Who is she? — She is my sister.U kim? — U mening opam/singlim.
Who are they? — They are my friends.Ular kim? — Ular do‘stlarim.

2. Who do you ...? — Kimni?

Ba’zan savol ishni bajarayotgan odam haqida emas, balki ish kimga qaratilgani haqida bo‘ladi. Bunday savolda ega savol so‘zidan keyin keladi va do/does ishlatiladi.

Who + do / does + subject + verb?
Who do you visit?Siz kimnikiga borasiz / kimni ko‘rgani borasiz?
Who does she call?U kimga qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi?
Who do they help?Ular kimga yordam beradi?
Does dan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklda qoladi: Who does he call? — call (emas calls).

3. Who gapning egasi bo‘lsa

Who o‘zi “kim ish qilyapti?” degan subject bo‘lib turganda, odatda undan keyin darhol fe’l keladi. Bu shaklda do/does ko‘pincha kerak emas.

Who + verb ...?
Who lives here?Bu yerda kim yashaydi?
Who works at this school?Bu maktabda kim ishlaydi?
Who wants tea?Kim choy xohlaydi?
Taqqoslang:
Who calls Ali? — Kim Aliga qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi? (Who — subject, ishni bajaruvchi)
Who does Ali call? — Ali kimga qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi? (Ali — subject, who — object)

4. Who savoliga javob

Who savoliga odamni ko‘rsatib javob beramiz: ism, qarindoshlik nomi yoki boshqa ma’lumot bilan.

Who is your English teacher? — My teacher is Mr Brown.Ingliz tili o‘qituvchingiz kim? — O‘qituvchim janob Brown.
Who do you visit on Sundays? — I visit my grandmother.Yakshanba kuni kimnikiga borasiz? — Buvimnikiga boraman.
Who works here? — Sara works here.Bu yerda kim ishlaydi? — Sara ishlaydi.

Mashq 1 — Mos savol tuzing

1. Javob: She is my teacher. Savol: ___ is your teacher?

2. Javob: I visit my grandmother. Savol: ___ do you visit?

3. Javob: Ali works here. Savol: ___ works here?

4. Javob: He calls his mother. Savol: ___ does he call?

Javoblar:
1. Who. 2. Who. 3. Who. 4. Who.

Mashq 2 — Do/Does yoki yordamchi kerak emasmi?

1. Who ___ you help?

2. Who ___ she visit?

3. Who ___ lives next door? (do / does / bo‘sh)

4. Who ___ they call?

Javoblar:
1. do. 2. does. 3. Bo‘sh — Who bu yerda subject: Who lives next door? 4. do.
