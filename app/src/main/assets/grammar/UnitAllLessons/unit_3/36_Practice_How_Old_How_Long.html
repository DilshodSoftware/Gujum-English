36-AMALIYOT. HOW OLD / HOW LONG

Yosh · davomiy vaqt · jismoniy uzunlik
How old yoshni so‘raydi va javobda am/is/are ishlatiladi. How long qancha vaqt yoki qanchalik uzunligini so‘raydi. Harakat fe’li bilan do/does kerak bo‘lishi mumkin.

1-mashq — How old yoki How long?

1. ___ is your brother? (yosh)

2. ___ is the film? (davomiyligi)

3. ___ is this rope? (uzunligi)

4. ___ do you wait for the bus? (vaqt)

5. ___ are the twins? (yoshi)

Javoblar: 1. How old; 2. How long; 3. How long; 4. How long; 5. How old.

2-mashq — Is/Are yoki Do/Does?

1. How old ___ your sister?

2. How long ___ the bridge?

3. How long ___ you study every evening?

4. How old ___ the children?

5. How long ___ the lesson last?

Javoblar: 1. is; 2. is; 3. do; 4. are; 5. does.

3-mashq — Savolni tuzing

1. your father / how old / is / ?

2. does / how long / the trip / take / ?

3. the table / how long / is / ?

4. study / how long / do / you / ?

Javoblar: 1. How old is your father? 2. How long does the trip take? 3. How long is the table? 4. How long do you study?

4-mashq — Yoshni inglizcha ayting

1. I / 14 years old

2. My brother / 9

3. The twins / 7 years old

Javoblar: 1. I am 14 years old. 2. My brother is 9 years old. 3. The twins are 7 years old.

5-mashq — Savolga mos javobni tanlang

1. How old is Mina? (She is 12. / For two hours.)

2. How long is the lesson? (It is 45 minutes. / She is 12.)

3. How long is the road? (It is 3 km long. / At five.)

4. When does the lesson start? (At nine. / For one hour.)

Javoblar: 1. She is 12. 2. It is 45 minutes. 3. It is 3 km long. 4. At nine.

6-mashq — Have yoki Be?

1. To‘g‘ri gapni tanlang: She (is / has) 10 years old.

2. To‘g‘ri savolni tanlang: (How old are you? / How old have you?)

Javoblar: 1. is; 2. How old are you?
