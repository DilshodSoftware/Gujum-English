33-AMALIYOT. WHEN?

Qachon? · Vaqt, kun va soat
When vaqtni so‘raydi. To be bilan When is the lesson?; harakat bilan When do you study? He/she/it uchun does, va undan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklda qoladi.

1-mashq — To Be, Do yoki Does?

1. When ___ your birthday? (is / does)

2. When ___ the children go to bed? (do / are)

3. When ___ Sara finish work? (do / does)

4. When ___ your holidays? (are / do)

5. When ___ the bus arrive? (does / is)

Javoblar: 1. is; 2. do; 3. does; 4. are; 5. does.

2-mashq — Savolni so‘zlardan tuzing

1. you / when / get up / do / ?

2. when / is / the meeting / ?

3. does / when / the shop / open / ?

4. are / when / they / free / ?

Javoblar: 1. When do you get up? 2. When is the meeting? 3. When does the shop open? 4. When are they free?

3-mashq — Vaqtga mos javobni tanlang

1. When does school start? (At eight. / At the library.)

2. When is your birthday? (In July. / In the kitchen.)

3. When do you study? (In the evening. / By bus.)

4. When are your classes? (On Monday. / Near my house.)

Javoblar: 1. At eight. 2. In July. 3. In the evening. 4. On Monday.

4-mashq — When yoki Where?

1. ___ is the bus stop? — Near the bank.

2. ___ does the film begin? — At six.

3. ___ are my glasses? — On the desk.

4. ___ do you visit your aunt? — Every weekend.

Javoblar: 1. Where; 2. When; 3. Where; 4. When.

5-mashq — Xatoni to‘g‘rilang

1. When you go to work?

2. When does the train arrives?

3. When the party is?

4. When do Sara eat dinner?

Javoblar: 1. When do you go to work? 2. When does the train arrive? 3. When is the party? 4. When does Sara eat dinner?

6-mashq — Vaqt bilan javob bering

1. When do you wake up? (at seven)

2. When is the English lesson? (on Tuesday)

3. When does your father come home? (in the evening)

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. I wake up at seven. 2. It is on Tuesday. 3. He comes home in the evening.
