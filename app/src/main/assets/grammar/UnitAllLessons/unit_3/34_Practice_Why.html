34-AMALIYOT. WHY?

Nega? Nima uchun? · Sabab so‘rash va Because bilan javob
Why sababni so‘raydi. To be bilan: Why are you tired? Harakat bilan: Why do you study? Javobda ko‘pincha because (“chunki”) ishlatamiz.

1-mashq — To Be yoki Do/Does?

1. Why ___ you happy? (are / do)

2. Why ___ he walk to school? (is / does)

3. Why ___ the children at home? (are / do)

4. Why ___ Sara learn English? (does / is)

5. Why ___ I late? (am / do)

Javoblar: 1. are; 2. does; 3. are; 4. does; 5. am.

2-mashq — So‘zlardan Why savoli tuzing

1. are / why / you / sad / ?

2. she / why / does / work / at night / ?

3. they / why / are / late / ?

4. does / why / he / study / English / ?

Javoblar: 1. Why are you sad? 2. Why does she work at night? 3. Why are they late? 4. Why does he study English?

3-mashq — Because bilan sababni ulang

1. Why are you happy? (it is my birthday)

2. Why does he walk? (he likes walking)

3. Why are they at home? (they are sick)

4. Why do you study English? (I need it for work)

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. Because it is my birthday. 2. Because he likes walking.
3. Because they are sick. 4. Because I need it for work.

4-mashq — What yoki Why?

1. ___ do you eat for breakfast? — Eggs.

2. ___ do you eat breakfast early? — Because I go to work.

3. ___ is in the bag? — My book.

4. ___ is she at home? — Because she is sick.

Javoblar: 1. What; 2. Why; 3. What; 4. Why.

5-mashq — Fe’l shaklini tuzating

1. Why does he likes tea?

2. Why they are tired?

3. Why does Sara studies at night?

4. Why do you are here?

Javoblar: 1. Why does he like tea? 2. Why are they tired? 3. Why does Sara study at night? 4. Why are you here?

6-mashq — Sababni o‘zingiz ayting

1. Why are you learning English?

2. Why do you get up in the morning?

Namunaviy javoblar: Because I like English. / Because I go to school.
