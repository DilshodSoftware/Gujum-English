29-AMALIYOT. QUESTION WORDS

What · Where · Who · When · Why · How
Savol so‘zi qanday ma’lumot kerakligini bildiradi: narsa, joy, odam, vaqt, sabab yoki usul. Oddiy fe’l bilan qolip: Question word + do/does + subject + verb? To be bilan: Question word + am/is/are + subject?

1-mashq — Savol so‘zini javob turi bilan moslang

1. What   2. Where   3. Who   4. When   5. Why   6. How

A. odam   B. usul/holat   C. vaqt   D. sabab   E. narsa/ma’lumot   F. joy

Javoblar: 1–E; 2–F; 3–A; 4–C; 5–D; 6–B.

2-mashq — Ma’nosiga mos savol so‘zini tanlang

1. ___ is your name? (narsa/ma’lumot)

2. ___ are my shoes? (joy)

3. ___ is your best friend? (odam)

4. ___ do you get up? (vaqt)

5. ___ are you laughing? (sabab)

6. ___ do you go to work? (usul)

Javoblar: 1. What; 2. Where; 3. Who; 4. When; 5. Why; 6. How.

3-mashq — Savolning ikkinchi qismini tanlang

1. Where do you…? (live / name)

2. What does she…? (cook / at home)

3. When do they…? (study / the library)

4. Why does he…? (run / because he is late)

5. How do you…? (travel / Monday)

Javoblar: 1. live; 2. cook; 3. study; 4. run; 5. travel. Savolga mos javoblar: at home; food; in the evening; because he is late; by bus.

4-mashq — So‘zlarni tartibga solib savol tuzing

1. where / your parents / do / work / ?

2. who / is / that man / ?

3. when / the lesson / is / ?

4. what / you / do / eat / for breakfast / ?

5. how / she / does / come to school / ?

Javoblar:
1. Where do your parents work? 2. Who is that man?
3. When is the lesson? 4. What do you eat for breakfast?
5. How does she come to school?

5-mashq — To Be yoki Do/Does?

Savolni tugating. Holat/kim-qayerda bo‘lsa to be; harakat fe’li bo‘lsa do/does ishlating.

1. Where ___ your sister? (is / does)

2. Where ___ your sister work? (is / does)

3. Who ___ your teacher? (is / does)

4. What ___ you like? (are / do)

5. Why ___ they at home? (are / do)

Javoblar: 1. is; 2. does; 3. is; 4. do; 5. are.

6-mashq — Savolga qanday javob kerak?

1. What is this? → buyum nomi / joy

2. When do you study? → kun yoki vaqt / odam

3. Why is she happy? → sabab / usul

4. Who is your doctor? → odam / narsa

5. How do you travel? → usul / vaqt

Javoblar: 1. buyum nomi; 2. kun yoki vaqt; 3. sabab; 4. odam; 5. usul.

7-mashq — O‘zingiz haqingizda javob bering

1. Where do you live?

2. What do you like?

3. When do you study English?

4. How do you come to school or work?

Namunaviy javoblar: I live in Tashkent. I like music. I study English in the evening. I come by bus. O‘zingizga mos javob bering.
