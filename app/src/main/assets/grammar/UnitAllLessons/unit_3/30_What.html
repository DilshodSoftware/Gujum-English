What noma’lum narsa yoki ma’lumotni so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi. Javobda odatda narsa, ism, rang, faoliyat yoki boshqa ma’lumot aytiladi.

1. What — “Nima?”

What? — “Nima?” degani. Biror narsaning nomini bilmaganimizda so‘raymiz.

What is this?Bu nima?
What is that?Anavi nima?
What are these?Bular nima?
What is this? — It is a pen.Bu nima? — Bu ruchka.
What are these? — They are books.Bular nima? — Bular kitoblar.

2. What is ...? — To Be bilan

Agar savolda am/is/are bo‘lsa, shu fe’l What dan keyin keladi.

What + am/is/are + subject?
What is your name?Ismingiz nima?
What is your favorite color?Sevimli rangingiz qaysi?
What are your favorite foods?Sevimli taomlaringiz qaysilar?
What is in the bag?Sumkada nima bor?

Javob ham odatda to be bilan boshlanadi: My name is Ali. — Mening ismim Ali.

3. What do you ...? — Harakat haqida

Harakat haqida “nima?” deb so‘raganda Present Simple da do yoki does yordam beradi.

What + do/does + subject + fe’l?
What do you eat for breakfast?Nonushtaga nima yeysiz?
What do they study?Ular nimani o‘rganadi?
What does he like?U nimani yoqtiradi?
What does Sara read?Sara nima o‘qiydi?
Does dan keyin fe’lga -s qo‘shmang: What does he like? ✅ · What does he likes? ❌

4. What bilan ko‘p ishlatiladigan savollar

What time is it?Soat necha?
What day is it today?Bugun haftaning qaysi kuni?
What color is your bag?Sumkangiz qanday rangda?
What do you do after school?Maktabdan keyin nima qilasiz?

What time — soat nechaligini, what day — haftaning qaysi kuni ekanini so‘raydi.

5. What savoliga javob qanday bo‘ladi?

What savolida javob “ha” yoki “yo‘q” emas. Savol so‘ragan narsaning nomini yoki ma’lumotni aytamiz.

What is this? — It is a phone.Bu nima? — Bu telefon.
What do you like? — I like music.Nimani yoqtirasiz? — Men musiqani yoqtiraman.
What does he eat? — He eats rice.U nima yeydi? — U guruch yeydi.

6. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

What you like? ❌ → What do you like? ✅
What does she likes? ❌ → What does she like? ✅
What this is? ❌ → What is this? ✅

Mashq 1 — What bilan savolni to‘ldiring

1. ___ is your name?

2. ___ do you eat for lunch?

3. ___ color is the car?

4. ___ does she study?

Javoblar:
1–4. What.

Mashq 2 — So‘zlarni to‘g‘ri tartiblang

1. you / what / do / like / ?

2. is / this / what / ?

3. does / what / he / want / ?

Javoblar:
1. What do you like? 2. What is this? 3. What does he want?
