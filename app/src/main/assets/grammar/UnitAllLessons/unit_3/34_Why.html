Why biror ish yoki holatning sababini so‘raydi. Why savoliga ko‘pincha because (“chunki”) bilan javob beramiz.

1. Why — “Nega?”

Biror narsa nima sababdan bo‘layotganini bilmoqchi bo‘lsak, savol boshida why ishlatamiz.

Why are you happy?Nega xursandsiz?
Why is she at home?Nega u uyda?
Why do you study English?Nega ingliz tilini o‘rganasiz?
Why does he walk to work?Nega u ishga piyoda boradi?

2. To Be bilan Why savoli

Gapda am, is, are bo‘lsa, u Why dan keyin keladi.

Why + am / is / are + subject?
Why are they late?Nega ular kechikdi?
Why is the baby crying?Nega chaqaloq yig‘layapti?
Why am I here?Men nega bu yerdaman?

3. Do / Does bilan Why savoli

Oddiy harakat fe’li bilan do/does ishlatiladi. Does dan keyingi fe’l oddiy shaklda keladi.

Why + do / does + subject + verb?
Why do you get up early?Nega erta turasiz?
Why do they learn English?Nega ular ingliz tilini o‘rganadi?
Why does she work at night?Nega u tunda ishlaydi?
Why does he run? ✅
Why does he runs? ❌

4. Because — “chunki”

Why savoliga sabab bilan javob berish uchun because deymiz. Javobni qisqa yoki to‘liq gap bilan berish mumkin.

Why are you happy? — Because it is my birthday.Nega xursandsiz? — Chunki bugun tug‘ilgan kunim.
Why do you study English? — Because I need it for work.Nega ingliz tilini o‘rganasiz? — Chunki u menga ish uchun kerak.
Why is the child crying? — Because he is tired.Nega bola yig‘layapti? — Chunki u charchagan.

Because dan keyin sababni aytamiz: because I like it — chunki menga yoqadi; because it is cold — chunki sovuq.

5. What va Why farqi

What do you study?Nimani o‘rganasiz? (narsa)
Why do you study?Nega o‘qiysiz? (sabab)
Why is he at home?Nega u uyda? (sabab)

Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

Why do you work? ✅
Why you work? ❌

Why does she study? ✅
Why does she studies? ❌

Mashq 1 — To Be yoki Do / Does?

1. Why ___ you tired? (am / are)

2. Why ___ he study at night? (do / does)

3. Why ___ they at school? (are / do)

4. Why ___ Sara walk to work? (do / does)

Javoblar:
1. are. 2. does. 3. are. 4. does.

Mashq 2 — Savolga Because bilan javob bering

1. Why are you happy? (It is my birthday.)

2. Why does she study English? (She needs it for work.)

3. Why are they at home? (They are sick.)

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. Because it is my birthday.
2. Because she needs it for work.
3. Because they are sick.
