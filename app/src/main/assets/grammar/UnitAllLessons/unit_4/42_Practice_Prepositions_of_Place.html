42-AMALIYOT. PREPOSITIONS OF PLACE

In / On / Under / Next to / Behind / In front of / Between / Near
Joy qolipi: Subject + am/is/are + joy predlogi + joy. Between ko‘pincha ikki joy/narsa orasini bildiradi: between A and B.

1-mashq — Predlogni ma’nosi bilan moslang

1. in   2. on   3. under   4. next to

5. behind   6. in front of   7. between   8. near

Ma’nolar: ichida · ustida · tagida · yonida · orqasida · oldida · orasida · yaqinida

Javoblar: 1. ichida; 2. ustida; 3. tagida; 4. yonida; 5. orqasida; 6. oldida; 7. orasida; 8. yaqinida.

2-mashq — Vaziyatga mos predlogni tanlang

1. The keys are (in / on) the bag. — sumkaning ichida

2. The picture is (on / under) the wall.

3. The cat is (under / between) the chair.

4. The bank is (next to / in) the shop. — yonida

5. The school is (between / behind) the park and the café.

6. The car is (in front of / in) the house.

Javoblar: 1. in; 2. on; 3. under; 4. next to; 5. between; 6. in front of.

3-mashq — Am, Is yoki Are bilan to‘ldiring

1. The book ___ on the desk.

2. The shoes ___ under the bed.

3. My school ___ near the park.

4. The café ___ between the bank and the shop.

Javoblar: 1. is; 2. are; 3. is; 4. is.

4-mashq — Rasmni tasavvur qiling va gap tuzing

1. Kitob stol ustida. (the book / on / the table)

2. Mushuk eshik orqasida. (the cat / behind / the door)

3. Bog‘ uy yonida. (the garden / next to / the house)

4. Dorixona bank va kafe orasida. (the pharmacy / between / the bank and the café)

Javoblar: 1. The book is on the table. 2. The cat is behind the door. 3. The garden is next to the house. 4. The pharmacy is between the bank and the café.

5-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. The cat under the chair.

2. The book is in the table. (stol ustida)

3. The bank are near the school.

4. The café is between the bank. (bank va do‘kon orasida)

Javoblar: 1. The cat is under the chair. 2. The book is on the table. 3. The bank is near the school. 4. The café is between the bank and the shop.

6-mashq — Where bilan javob bering

Where is the phone? (on the desk)

Where are the children? (in the park)

Namunaviy javoblar: It is on the desk. They are in the park.
