43-AMALIYOT. PREPOSITIONS OF TIME

At / On / In · Soat · kun/sana · oy/yil/fasl
At — aniq soat va at night; on — hafta kuni yoki aniq sana; in — oy, yil, fasl va in the morning/evening. This/next/every bilan hafta kunidan oldin odatda predlog qo‘yilmaydi.

1-mashq — At, On yoki In?

1. ___ 6:30   2. ___ Friday   3. ___ April

4. ___ the morning   5. ___ night   6. ___ 2024

7. ___ 12 June   8. ___ winter

Javoblar: 1. at; 2. on; 3. in; 4. in; 5. at; 6. in; 7. on; 8. in.

2-mashq — Vaqt turiga qarab guruhlang

at / on / in guruhlariga ajrating: noon, Monday, September, 8 o’clock, 2030, Saturday morning, the afternoon, night.

Javoblar: at: noon, 8 o’clock, night. on: Monday, Saturday morning. in: September, 2030, the afternoon.

3-mashq — Gaplarni to‘ldiring

1. We have a test ___ Tuesday.

2. The film starts ___ 7:15.

3. My birthday is ___ December.

4. I do my homework ___ the evening.

5. They visit us ___ summer.

Javoblar: 1. on; 2. at; 3. in; 4. in; 5. in.

4-mashq — Predlog kerakmi?

On yoki hech narsa qo‘ymang.

1. ___ Monday (on / —)

2. ___ next Monday (on / —)

3. ___ every Friday (on / —)

4. ___ this weekend (on / —)

Javoblar: 1. on; 2. —; 3. —; 4. —.

5-mashq — Xatoni to‘g‘rilang

1. My class starts on 9 o’clock.

2. We travel at July.

3. I play football in Monday.

4. She visits us on every Sunday.

5. He reads on the evening.

Javoblar: 1. at 9 o’clock; 2. in July; 3. on Monday; 4. every Sunday; 5. in the evening.

6-mashq — O‘zingiz haqingizda yozing

Gaplarni at, on, in bilan yakunlang: I wake up ___ 7. I study English ___ Monday. My birthday is ___ (oy).

Namunaviy javob: I wake up at 7. I study English on Monday. My birthday is in May.
