47-AMALIYOT. OBJECT PRONOUNS

Me · You · Him · Her · It · Us · Them
Subject ishni bajaradi: She sees Ali. Object harakat kimga/nimaga qaratilganini bildiradi va ko‘pincha fe’ldan yoki predlogdan keyin keladi: She sees him. Come with me.

1-mashq — Subjectdan Object shaklini toping

I → ___   you → ___   he → ___   she → ___

it → ___   we → ___   they → ___

Javoblar: me, you, him, her, it, us, them.

2-mashq — Fe’ldan keyin to‘g‘ri olmoshni tanlang

1. I know (he / him).

2. She helps (we / us).

3. They visit (she / her).

4. The teacher sees (they / them).

5. Do you like (I / me)?

6. We call (he / him) every week.

Javoblar: 1. him; 2. us; 3. her; 4. them; 5. me; 6. him.

3-mashq — Ismni object pronoun bilan almashtiring

1. I see Sara. → I see ___.

2. He knows Ali. → He knows ___.

3. They help my sister and me. → They help ___.

4. We visit the children. → We visit ___.

Javoblar: 1. her; 2. him; 3. us; 4. them.

4-mashq — Object olmoshi bilan gapni qayta yozing

1. I call my parents.

2. She listens to the teacher.

3. He sits next to Sara.

4. They look at the picture.

Javoblar: 1. I call them. 2. She listens to him/her. 3. He sits next to her. 4. They look at it.

5-mashq — Subject yoki Object?

1. He likes me.

2. They help her.

3. We see them.

4. She knows us.

Javoblar: Birinchi olmosh — subject; fe’ldan keyingi olmosh — object. 1. He subject, me object. 2. They subject, her object. 3. We subject, them object. 4. She subject, us object.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. Him likes I.

2. She helps they.

3. Come with I.

4. We know she.

Javoblar: 1. He likes me. 2. She helps them. 3. Come with me. 4. We know her.
