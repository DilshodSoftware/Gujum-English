Adjective — sifat. U odam yoki narsaning qandayligini, rangini, hajmini yoki holatini tasvirlaydi. O‘zbek tilida: katta uy, yaxshi kitob, qizil olma.

1. Sifat nima haqida ma’lumot beradi?

Sifatga “qanday?” deb savol beramiz. Masalan: qanday uy? — katta uy. Ingliz tilida sifat otning oldidan yoki am/is/are dan keyin kelishi mumkin.

a big housekatta uy
The house is big.Uy katta.
a good bookyaxshi kitob
The book is good.Kitob yaxshi.
Adjective + noun   Subject + am/is/are + adjective

Old odamga nisbatan “yoshi katta/keksa”, buyumga nisbatan “eski” bo‘lishi mumkin. Ma’no gapdagi otga bog‘liq.

4. Sifat otning oldida keladi

Ingliz tilida sifat odatda tasvirlayotgan otning oldidan keladi. O‘zbek tilidagi tartibga qarama-qarshi bo‘lishi mumkin.

a big housekatta uy
an old careski mashina
a happy childxursand bola
a small dog ✅
a dog small ❌
Sifatni otning oldiga qo‘ying.

5. Sifat am/is/are dan keyin keladi

Odam yoki narsaning qandayligini to‘liq gap bilan aytganda sifat ko‘pincha to be dan keyin keladi.

I am tired.Men charchaganman.
You are kind.Siz mehribonsiz.
He is tall.U bo‘yi baland.
The rooms are clean.Xonalar toza.

6. Sifat ko‘plikda o‘zgarmaydi

Ingliz tilida sifatga ot ko‘plik bo‘lgani uchun -s qo‘shmaymiz. Ko‘plik qo‘shimchasi otga tegishli, sifatga emas.

a small dogbitta kichik it
two small dogsikkita kichik it
a red apple / red applesqizil olma / qizil olmalar
The houses are big. ✅
The houses are bigs. ❌

7. Sifatlar bilan gapni kengaytiramiz

a bookkitob
an old bookeski kitob
an old red bookeski qizil kitob
The old red book is interesting.Eski qizil kitob qiziqarli.

Mashq 1 — Sifatning ma’nosini yozing

1. big   2. small   3. happy

4. cold   5. clean   6. beautiful

Javoblar:
1. katta. 2. kichik. 3. xursand.
4. sovuq. 5. toza. 6. chiroyli.

Mashq 2 — So‘zlarni to‘g‘ri tartiblang

1. a / house / big

2. cat / black / a

3. is / the room / clean

4. are / the apples / green

Javoblar:
1. a big house. 2. a black cat.
3. The room is clean. 4. The apples are green.

Mashq 3 — To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang

1. a (small / smalls) dog

2. The flowers are (beautiful / beautifuls).

3. (A red car / A car red) is here.

4. The tea is (hot / hots).

Javoblar:
1. small. 2. beautiful. 3. A red car. 4. hot.
