43-DARS. PREPOSITIONS OF TIME

At / On / In · Vaqtni bildiruvchi predloglar
At, on, in ba’zan joyni, ba’zan vaqtni bildiradi. Bu darsda ular bilan “qachon?” savoliga javob berishni o‘rganamiz.

1. AT — aniq soat va ayrim vaqtlar

At aniq vaqt yoki soat oldidan keladi. Uni “...da” deb tushunish mumkin.

at 7 o’clocksoat 7 da
at 9:309:30 da
at noontush paytida
at nighttunda
I get up at seven.Men soat yettida turaman.
The film starts at 8:30.Film 8:30 da boshlanadi.

2. ON — kun va sana

On hafta kunlari va aniq sanalar oldidan ishlatiladi.

on Mondaydushanba kuni
on Fridaysjuma kunlari
on 5 May5-may kuni
on Monday morningdushanba kuni ertalab
We have English on Tuesday.Seshanba kuni ingliz tili darsimiz bor.
Her birthday is on 5 May.Uning tug‘ilgan kuni 5-mayda.

3. IN — oy, yil, fasl va kunning katta qismlari

In uzunroq vaqt oralig‘i oldidan ishlatiladi: oy, yil, fasl yoki ertalab/kunduzi/kechqurun kabi vaqt bo‘laklari.

in Maymay oyida
in 20262026-yilda
in summeryozda
in the morningertalab
in the afternoontushdan keyin
in the eveningkechqurun
I was born in May.Men may oyida tug‘ilganman.
We travel in summer.Biz yozda sayohat qilamiz.

4. Uchalasini taqqoslang

PredlogQachon ishlatiladi?Misollar
ataniq soat, ayrim vaqtlarat 6, at night
onhafta kuni, aniq sanaon Friday, on 5 May
inoy, yil, fasl, kun qismiin June, in 2026, in the morning
Yodlash uchun:
AT — soatning aniq nuqtasi.
ON — kalendardagi kun.
IN — uzunroq vaqt oralig‘i.

5. This / Next / Every bilan

This, next yoki every so‘zlari hafta kuni oldidan kelsa, odatda on qo‘ymaymiz.

this Mondayshu dushanba
next Fridaykelasi juma
every Tuesdayhar seshanba
on Monday ✅
this Monday ✅
on this Monday ❌ — boshlang‘ich odatiy gapda on ni ishlatmaymiz.

6. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

at 7 o’clock ✅   on 7 o’clock ❌
on Monday ✅   in Monday ❌
in July ✅   at July ❌
in the morning ✅   on the morning ❌

Mashq 1 — At, On yoki In?

1. ___ 8 o’clock   2. ___ Wednesday

3. ___ October   4. ___ the evening

5. ___ night   6. ___ 2025

7. ___ Saturday morning   8. ___ 1 June

Javoblar:
1. at. 2. on. 3. in. 4. in.
5. at. 6. in. 7. on. 8. on.

Mashq 2 — To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang

1. We study English (on / in) Monday.

2. My lesson starts (at / on) 10:00.

3. She was born (in / at) April.

4. They play football (every / on every) Sunday.

Javoblar:
1. on. 2. at. 3. in. 4. every.
