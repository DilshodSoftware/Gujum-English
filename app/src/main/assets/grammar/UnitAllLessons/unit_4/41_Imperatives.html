41-DARS. IMPERATIVES

Buyruq, iltimos va ko‘rsatmalar · Open, Close, Sit, Go
Imperative — biror kishiga nima qilish kerakligini aytadigan buyruq yoki ko‘rsatma. Darsda o‘qituvchi ko‘p ishlatadigan “Kitobni oching”, “Turing”, “Tinglang” kabi gaplar imperative bo‘ladi.

1. Buyruqning oddiy shakli

Buyruqda gap ko‘pincha fe’lning oddiy shaklidan boshlanadi. “You” (sen/siz) gapda aytilmaydi, lekin kimga buyruq berilayotgani tushuniladi.

Verb + gapning qolgan qismi
Open the book.Kitobni och.
Close the door.Eshikni yop.
Sit down.O‘tir.
Stand up.O‘rningdan tur.
Listen carefully.Diqqat bilan tingla.
Sit down. gapida buyruq berilayotgan odamni “you” deb tushunamiz. Shuning uchun odatda You sit down. demaymiz; oddiy buyruqda Sit down. deymiz.

2. Iltimosni muloyim qilish

Buyruq juda keskin eshitilmasligi uchun please (“iltimos”) qo‘shamiz. Uni gap boshida yoki oxirida ishlatish mumkin.

Please open the window.Iltimos, derazani oching.
Please help me.Iltimos, menga yordam bering.
Come here, please.Iltimos, bu yoqqa keling.
Please gap boshida ham, oxirida ham kelishi mumkin: Please sit down. / Sit down, please.

3. Sinfxonada ko‘p ishlatiladigan ko‘rsatmalar

Look at the board.Doskaga qara.
Read the sentence.Gapni o‘qi.
Write your name.Ismingni yoz.
Repeat the word.So‘zni takrorla.
Raise your hand.Qo‘lingni ko‘tar.
Turn to page ten.O‘ninchi sahifani och.

4. Inkor buyruq: Don’t + fe’l

Biror ishni qilmaslikni buyurish uchun gap boshiga don’t qo‘yamiz. Undan keyin fe’lning oddiy shakli keladi.

Don’t + verb
Don’t run.Yugurma.
Don’t open the door.Eshikni ochma.
Don’t be late.Kech qolma.
Don’t touch that.Anavi narsaga tegma.
Don’t sit here. ✅
Don’t to sit here. ❌
Don’t sits here. ❌

5. Buyruq, savol va darak gap farqi

Gap turiInglizchaMa’nosi
DarakYou open the book.Sen kitobni ochasan.
BuyruqOpen the book.Kitobni och.
Iltimosli buyruqPlease open the book.Iltimos, kitobni oching.
Inkor buyruqDon’t open the book.Kitobni ochma.

6. Qadam-baqadam tuzamiz

“Iltimos, derazani yoping” demoqchimiz:
1. Harakat fe’lini tanlang: close — yopmoq.
2. Nimaning yopilishini qo‘shing: the window — deraza.
3. Muloyim qilish uchun please qo‘shing: Please close the window.

Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

Oddiy buyruqda you ni aytmaymiz: Open the book! ✅
Don’t to run. ❌ → Don’t run. ✅
Don’t runs. ❌ → Don’t run. ✅

Mashq 1 — Fe’ldan buyruq tuzing

1. open the book (iltimos bilan)

2. sit down (iltimos bilan)

3. run (qilma deb ayting)

4. touch the screen (qilma deb ayting)

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. Please open the book. 2. Sit down, please.
3. Don’t run. 4. Don’t touch the screen.

Mashq 2 — Buyruqni tarjima qiling

1. Close the door, please.

2. Don’t be late.

3. Read the question.

4. Please wait here.

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. Iltimos, eshikni yoping. 2. Kech qolmang.
3. Savolni o‘qing. 4. Iltimos, shu yerda kuting.
