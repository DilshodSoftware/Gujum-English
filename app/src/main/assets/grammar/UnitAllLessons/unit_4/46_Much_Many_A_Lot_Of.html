Oldingi dars: How many sanaladigan narsalar, how much esa sanalmaydigan narsalar bilan ishlaydi. Bu darsda many, much va a lot of ni gaplarda ishlatamiz.

1. Many — sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan

Many donalab sanash mumkin bo‘lgan ko‘plikdagi ot bilan ishlatiladi: books, apples, students, chairs.

many booksko‘p kitoblar
many studentsko‘p o‘quvchilar
many applesko‘p olmalar
How many books do you have?Sizda nechta kitob bor?
There aren’t many chairs.Stullar ko‘p emas.

2. Much — sanalmaydigan ot bilan

Much donalab sanalmaydigan so‘zlar bilan ishlatiladi: water, milk, rice, time, money.

much waterko‘p suv
much timeko‘p vaqt
much moneyko‘p pul
How much water do you drink?Qancha suv ichasiz?
We don’t have much time.Bizda ko‘p vaqt yo‘q.

3. A Lot Of — ikkalasi bilan ishlaydi

A lot of “ko‘p” degani va sanaladigan ko‘plikdagi ot bilan ham, sanalmaydigan ot bilan ham ishlatiladi.

Ot turiMisolMa’nosi
Sanaladigan, ko‘plika lot of booksko‘p kitoblar
Sanaladigan, ko‘plika lot of friendsko‘p do‘stlar
Sanalmaydigana lot of waterko‘p suv
Sanalmaydigana lot of moneyko‘p pul
She has a lot of books.Unda ko‘p kitob bor.
They drink a lot of water.Ular ko‘p suv ichadi.

4. Darak, inkor va savolda

Kundalik ingliz tilida a lot of darak gaplarda juda ko‘p ishlatiladi. Many/much savol va inkor gaplarda ko‘p uchraydi.

Darak: I have a lot of friends.Mening ko‘p do‘stlarim bor.
Savol: Do you have many friends?Do‘stlaringiz ko‘pmi?
Inkor: I don’t have much free time.Menda bo‘sh vaqt ko‘p emas.
Many va much darak gapda ham ishlatilishi mumkin, ammo boshlang‘ich kundalik gaplarda “ko‘p” ma’nosida a lot of juda qulay: We have a lot of work.

5. Many va Much ni adashtirmang

many apples ✅ — olmalarni sanash mumkin
much apples ❌

much water ✅ — suvni odatda dona qilib sanamaymiz
many water ❌

a lot of apples / a lot of water ✅

6. Qoliplar

How many + ko‘plikdagi ot?

How much + sanalmaydigan ot?

a lot of + ikkala turdagi ot
How many eggs?Nechta tuxum?
How much milk?Qancha sut?
A lot of eggs / a lot of milkKo‘p tuxum / ko‘p sut

Mashq 1 — Many, Much yoki A Lot Of?

1. How ___ chairs are there?

2. How ___ water do you need?

3. She has ___ friends. (darak gap, “ko‘p”)

4. We don’t have ___ time.

5. He eats ___ apples. (darak gap, “ko‘p”)

6. How ___ money is in your bag?

Javoblar:
1. many. 2. much. 3. a lot of. 4. much. 5. a lot of. 6. much.
