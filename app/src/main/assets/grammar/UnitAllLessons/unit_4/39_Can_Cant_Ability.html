39-DARS. CAN / CAN’T — ABILITY

Can = qila olmoq · Can’t = qila olmaslik

Dars lug‘ati

Bu darsda uchraydigan so‘zlar — oldin eshitib, keyin darsni o‘qing.

can — qila olmoq
can’t — qila olmaydi
cannot — qila olmaydi
adult — katta yoshli
baby — chaqaloq
birds — qushlar
car — mashina
come — kelmoq
cook — pishirmoq
drive — haydamoq
english — ingliz tili
fish — baliq
fly — uchmoq
help — yordam
read — o‘qimoq
sing — kuylamoq
speak — gapirmoq
swim — suzmoq
today — bugun
Can biror ishni qilish qobiliyati yoki imkoniyati borligini bildiradi. Can’t esa qila olmaslikni bildiradi.

1. Can — qila olaman / oladi

Can bilan odam nimalarni qila olishini, bilishini yoki uddalashini aytamiz.

I can swim.Men suza olaman.
She can cook.U ovqat pishira oladi.
He can drive a car.U mashina hayday oladi.
Birds can fly.Qushlar ucha oladi.

2. Can barcha egalar bilan bir xil

I, you, he, she, it, we, they bilan can o‘zgarmaydi. He/she/it bo‘lsa ham can ga -s qo‘shmaymiz.

I can read.Men o‘qiy olaman.
You can read.Sen / siz o‘qiy olasan / olasiz.
She can read.U o‘qiy oladi.
They can read.Ular o‘qiy oladi.
He can swim. ✅
He cans swim. ❌

3. Can dan keyin fe’lning oddiy shakli keladi

Can dan keyingi fe’lga -s ham, to ham qo‘shilmaydi.

Subject + can + verb
She can sing.U kuylay oladi.
He can speak English.U inglizcha gapira oladi.
We can help.Biz yordam bera olamiz.
She can dance. ✅
She can dances. ❌
She can to dance. ❌

4. Can’t — qila olmayman / olmaydi

Can’t — cannot ning qisqa shakli. U qobiliyat yoki imkoniyat yo‘qligini bildiradi.

I can’t swim.Men suza olmayman.
He can’t drive.U mashina hayday olmaydi.
We cannot come today.Biz bugun kela olmaymiz.
Can’t barcha egalar bilan bir xil: I can’t, she can’t, they can’t.

5. Can yoki Can’t?

A fish can swim.Baliq suza oladi.
A fish can’t fly.Baliq ucha olmaydi.
A baby can’t drive a car.Chaqaloq mashina hayday olmaydi.
An adult can drive a car.Katta odam mashina hayday oladi.

Biror narsa mumkin yoki mumkin emasligini ham can/can’t bilan aytish mumkin. Ruxsat so‘rashni keyingi darsda o‘rganamiz.

6. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

He cans cook. ❌ → He can cook. ✅
They can to swim. ❌ → They can swim. ✅
She can’t sings. ❌ → She can’t sing. ✅

Mashq 1 — Can yoki Can’t?

Oddiy hayotdagi qobiliyatga qarab mos so‘zni tanlang.

1. Birds (can / can’t) fly.

2. Fish (can / can’t) walk to school.

3. A baby (can / can’t) drive a bus.

4. Many people (can / can’t) speak their first language.

Javoblar:
1. can. 2. can’t. 3. can’t. 4. can.

Mashq 2 — Gapni Can bilan to‘ldiring

1. I ___ read English.

2. My sister ___ cook.

3. We ___ help you.

4. They ___ play football.

Javoblar:
1–4. can.
