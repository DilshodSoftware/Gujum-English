44-DARS. COUNTABLE AND UNCOUNTABLE NOUNS

Sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar
Bu farq nima uchun kerak?
Ingliz tilida narsani donalab sanash mumkinmi yoki yo‘qmi, bu a/an, ko‘plik, some/any, much/many tanlashga yordam beradi.

1. Countable — sanaladigan otlar

Countable noun — donalab sanash mumkin bo‘lgan ot. Ularning oldiga one, two, three kabi sonlarni qo‘yish mumkin.

one book — two booksbitta kitob — ikkita kitob
one apple — three applesbitta olma — uchta olma
one chair — four chairsbitta stul — to‘rtta stul
one student — ten studentsbitta o‘quvchi — o‘nta o‘quvchi

Sanaladigan otning bitta shakli birlik, bir nechta shakli ko‘plik bo‘ladi.

BirlikKo‘plikMa’nosi
a penpensbitta ruchka / ruchkalar
a boxboxesbitta quti / qutilar
a babybabiesbitta chaqaloq / chaqaloqlar

2. Birlikdagi sanaladigan otga aniqlovchi kerak

Ingliz tilida bitta oddiy sanaladigan otni ko‘pincha yolg‘iz ishlatmaymiz. Uning oldidan a/an, the, my, this yoki son kabi so‘z keladi.

I have book. ❌
I have a book. ✅
the book ✅ · my book ✅ · one book ✅

Ko‘plikda esa odatda a/an ishlatilmaydi: books, two books, some books.

3. Uncountable — sanalmaydigan otlar

Uncountable noun odatda bitta-bitta sanalmaydigan modda, mahsulot yoki tushunchani bildiradi. Masalan, suvni odatda one water, two waters deb sanamaymiz.

watersuv
milksut
riceguruch
breadnon
moneypul
timevaqt
Umumiy qoida: sanalmaydigan ot odatda -s olib ko‘plik yasamaydi va oldidan a/an qo‘yilmaydi: water, some water, lekin odatiy ma’noda a water emas.

4. Miqdorni o‘lchov bilan sanash

Sanalmaydigan narsaning o‘zini dona qilib sanamaymiz. Lekin uni solingan idish, bo‘lak yoki o‘lchov bilan sanash mumkin.

a glass of waterbir stakan suv
two bottles of milkikki shisha sut
a piece of breadbir bo‘lak non
three cups of teauch piyola choy

Bu birikmalarda glass, bottle, piece, cup sanaladi. Water, milk, bread, tea esa moddaning nomi bo‘lib qoladi.

5. Ba’zi otlar vaziyatga qarab farqlanishi mumkin

Ko‘p ishlatiladigan so‘zlarning odatiy ma’nosini yodlang. Ba’zi otlar boshqa vaziyatda sanaladigan ma’noda ham kelishi mumkin, lekin hozircha eng oddiy ishlatilishini o‘rganamiz.

some chickenbiroz tovuq go‘shti (modda)
a chickenbitta tovuq (hayvon)
some paperbiroz qog‘oz (material)
a paperbitta hujjat/maqola (alohida narsa)

6. Countable va Uncountable farqini tekshirish

O‘zingizdan so‘rang:
“Buni one, two, three deb donalab sanay olamanmi?”
Ha → countable: one apple, two apples.
Odatda yo‘q → uncountable: water, milk, rice.
an applebitta olma — countable, birlik
applesolmalar — countable, ko‘plik
watersuv — odatda uncountable

Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

a book / two books ✅
a books ❌

some water ✅
a water (oddiy, umumiy ma’noda) ❌
a glass of water ✅

Mashq 1 — Countable yoki Uncountable?

1. apple   2. water   3. chair

4. rice   5. student   6. milk

7. bread   8. box

Javoblar:
Countable: apple, chair, student, box.
Uncountable: water, rice, milk, bread.

Mashq 2 — To‘g‘ri shaklni tanlang

1. (a book / a books)

2. (two apple / two apples)

3. (a water / some water)

4. (a piece of bread / a bread)

5. (three cups of tea / three teas) — oddiy choy miqdori

Javoblar:
1. a book. 2. two apples. 3. some water.
4. a piece of bread. 5. three cups of tea.
