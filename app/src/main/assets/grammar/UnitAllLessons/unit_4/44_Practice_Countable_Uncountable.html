44-AMALIYOT. COUNTABLE / UNCOUNTABLE

Sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar · birlik · ko‘plik · o‘lchov
Countable: donalab sanaladi, birlik/ko‘plik shakli bo‘ladi: one apple, two apples. Birlik sanaladigan ot oldidan a/an yoki boshqa aniqlovchi keladi. Uncountable: odatda donalab sanalmaydi: water, rice, money.

1-mashq — Countable yoki Uncountable?

1. orange   2. bread   3. chair   4. milk

5. time   6. student   7. rice   8. box

Javoblar: Countable: orange, chair, student, box. Uncountable: bread, milk, time, rice.

2-mashq — Birlikmi yoki ko‘plikmi?

1. one ___ (apple / apples)

2. four ___ (box / boxes)

3. a ___ (student / students)

4. three ___ (baby / babies)

5. some ___ (water / waters) — ichimlik suvi

Javoblar: 1. apple; 2. boxes; 3. student; 4. babies; 5. water.

3-mashq — A/An, ko‘plik yoki sanalmaydigan shakl?

1. I have ___ pen. (a/an)

2. She has two ___. (book/books)

3. We need ___ rice. (a/some)

4. He eats ___ apple. (a/an)

5. They have three ___. (child/children)

Javoblar: 1. a; 2. books; 3. some; 4. an; 5. children.

4-mashq — Sanalmaydigan narsani o‘lchov bilan ayting

1. one glass ___ water

2. two bottles ___ milk

3. a piece ___ bread

4. three cups ___ tea

Javoblar: 1–4. of: a glass of water, two bottles of milk, a piece of bread, three cups of tea.

5-mashq — Gapdagi xatoni tuzating

1. I have a books.

2. She needs a milk.

3. There are two rice in the bowl.

4. He buys three bread.

Javoblar:
1. I have a book / I have books. 2. She needs some milk.
3. There is some rice in the bowl. 4. He buys three loaves/pieces of bread.

6-mashq — Chicken so‘zining ma’nosini aniqlang

1. some chicken (ovqat/modda yoki bitta hayvon?)

2. a chicken (ovqat/modda yoki bitta hayvon?)

Javoblar: 1. tovuq go‘shti — uncountable; 2. bitta tovuq — countable.
