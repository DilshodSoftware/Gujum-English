40-AMALIYOT. CAN — SAVOL VA ILTIMOS

Can + ega + oddiy fe’l? · qobiliyat · iltimos · ruxsat
Can savolda gap boshiga chiqadi: Can you swim? Kontekstga qarab qobiliyatni so‘raydi, iltimos qiladi yoki ruxsat so‘raydi. Fe’lga -s yoki to qo‘shilmaydi.

1-mashq — So‘zlarni savol tartibiga qo‘ying

1. you / can / cook / ?

2. can / your sister / swim / ?

3. I / can / use your phone / ?

4. help / can / they / us / ?

Javoblar: 1. Can you cook? 2. Can your sister swim? 3. Can I use your phone? 4. Can they help us?

2-mashq — Qobiliyat, iltimos yoki ruxsat?

1. Can you speak English? (qobiliyat / iltimos)

2. Can you open the window, please? (qobiliyat / iltimos)

3. Can I sit here? (ruxsat / qobiliyat)

4. Can your brother drive? (qobiliyat / ruxsat)

Javoblar: 1. qobiliyat; 2. iltimos; 3. ruxsat; 4. qobiliyat.

3-mashq — Qisqa javobni to‘ldiring

1. Can you swim? (ha) — Yes, ___ ___.

2. Can he cook? (yo‘q) — No, ___ ___.

3. Can your friends come? (ha) — Yes, ___ ___.

4. Can I use your pen? (ha, ruxsat) — Yes, you ___.

Javoblar: 1. I can; 2. he can’t; 3. they can; 4. can.

4-mashq — Muloyim iltimosni tanlang

1. (Can you help me? / You can help me?) — yordam so‘rang.

2. Iltimosni muloyim qilish uchun gapga qaysi so‘zni qo‘shamiz? (please / yesterday)

3. “Iltimos, takrorlang” — Can you ___ that, please? (repeat / repeats)

Javoblar: 1. Can you help me? 2. please; 3. repeat.

5-mashq — Can yoki Can’t?

1. ___ I come in? (ruxsat so‘rash)

2. Can you lend me a pencil? — Sorry, you ___ use mine now. (mumkin emas)

3. ___ you repeat the answer, please? (iltimos)

Javoblar: 1. Can; 2. can’t; 3. Can.

6-mashq — Xatolarni tuzating

1. Can she sings?

2. Do you can drive?

3. Can I to come in?

4. Can you opens the door, please?

Javoblar: 1. Can she sing? 2. Can you drive? 3. Can I come in? 4. Can you open the door, please?
