42-DARS. PREPOSITIONS OF PLACE

Joy predloglari · Narsa qayerda turganini aytish
Preposition — boshqa so‘zlar orasidagi munosabatni ko‘rsatadigan kichik so‘z. Bu darsda joyni bildiradigan predloglarni o‘rganamiz: ichida, ustida, tagida, yonida kabi.

1. Gapda qayerda ekanini qanday aytamiz?

Biror odam yoki narsa qayerda turganini aytishda ko‘pincha subject + am/is/are + joy predlogi + joy qolipidan foydalanamiz.

The book + is + on + the table.
The book is on the table.Kitob stol ustida.
The cat is under the chair.Mushuk stul tagida.

3. In — ichida

In narsa boshqa joy yoki idishning ichida ekanini bildiradi.

The keys are in the bag.Kalitlar sumkaning ichida.
The children are in the room.Bolalar xonada.
My family lives in Tashkent.Oilam Toshkentda yashaydi.

4. On va Under

On — ustida; under — tagida. Buyum qayerda turganini tasavvur qiling.

The cup is on the table.Piyola stol ustida.
The shoes are under the bed.Oyoq kiyimlar karavot tagida.
The picture is on the wall.Rasm devorda.

5. Next to, Behind, In front of

The bank is next to the shop.Bank do‘kon yonida.
The garden is behind the house.Bog‘ uyning orqasida.
The car is in front of the house.Mashina uyning oldida.

Next to ikki so‘zdan iborat bo‘lsa ham, bitta joy ma’nosini beradi: “yonida”.

6. Between va Near

Between odatda ikki odam yoki narsaning orasini bildiradi. Ko‘pincha between A and B shaklida keladi.

The bank is between the shop and the café.Bank do‘kon va kafe orasida.
The school is near my home.Maktab uyimga yaqin.

7. Where bilan savol beramiz

Joyni bilmasak, Where? — “Qayerda?” deb so‘raymiz. Javobda joy predlogi va joy nomi kelishi mumkin.

Where is the phone?Telefon qayerda?
It is on the desk.U parta ustida.
Where are the children?Bolalar qayerda?
They are in the park.Ular bog‘da.

8. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

The book is on the table. ✅
The book is in the table. ❌ (agar stol ichida bo‘lmasa)
The cat is under the chair. ✅
The cat under the chair. ❌ (to be kerak)

Mashq 1 — Mos predlogni tanlang

1. The milk is (in / under) the glass.

2. The book is (on / behind) the table.

3. The shoes are (under / in front of) the bed. (karavot tagida)

4. The shop is (next to / in) the bank. (yonida)

5. The ball is (between / on) the box and the chair. (orasida)

Javoblar:
1. in. 2. on. 3. under. 4. next to. 5. between.

Mashq 2 — O‘zbekchadan inglizchaga

1. Kitob sumkaning ichida.

2. Mushuk stol tagida.

3. Maktab uyimning yonida.

4. Bank do‘kon va kafe orasida.

Javoblar:
1. The book is in the bag.
2. The cat is under the table.
3. The school is next to my house.
4. The bank is between the shop and the café.
