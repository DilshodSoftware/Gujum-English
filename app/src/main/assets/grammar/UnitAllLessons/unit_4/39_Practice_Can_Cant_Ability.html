39-AMALIYOT. CAN / CAN’T — ABILITY

Qila olmoq / qila olmaslik · qobiliyat va imkoniyat
Can barcha egalar bilan bir xil. Undan keyin fe’lning oddiy shakli keladi: She can swim. Inkor shakli can’t; to yoki -s qo‘shilmaydi.

1-mashq — Can yoki Can’t?

1. A bird ___ fly.

2. A fish ___ climb a tree.

3. A baby ___ speak three languages.

4. A horse ___ run.

5. A person with no wings ___ fly like a bird.

Javoblar: 1. can; 2. can’t; 3. can’t; 4. can; 5. can’t.

2-mashq — Fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing

1. He can ___ (swim).

2. They can ___ (speak) English.

3. My sister can ___ (cook).

4. I can ___ (ride) a bike.

5. A cat can ___ (climb) a tree.

Javoblar: 1. swim; 2. speak; 3. cook; 4. ride; 5. climb.

3-mashq — Gaplarni inkor shakliga o‘tkazing

1. I can drive.

2. She can sing.

3. They can come today.

4. A baby can read.

Javoblar: 1. I can’t drive. 2. She can’t sing. 3. They can’t come today. 4. A baby can’t read.

4-mashq — To‘g‘ri gapni tanlang

1. a) She can dances.   b) She can dance.

2. a) He cans cook.   b) He can cook.

3. a) We can to help.   b) We can help.

4. a) They can’t swim.   b) They don’t can swim.

Javoblar: 1. b; 2. b; 3. b; 4. a.

5-mashq — Qobiliyat va harakatni juftlang

1. I can draw.   A. U suza oladi.

2. He can swim.   B. Men rasm chiza olaman.

3. We can speak English.   C. Biz inglizcha gapira olamiz.

Javoblar: 1–B; 2–A; 3–C.

6-mashq — O‘zingiz haqingizda gapiring

1. Bir nimalarni qila olishingiz haqida can bilan gap yozing.

2. Qila olmaydigan narsangiz haqida can’t bilan gap yozing.

Namunaviy javoblar: I can cook. I can’t drive a bus.
