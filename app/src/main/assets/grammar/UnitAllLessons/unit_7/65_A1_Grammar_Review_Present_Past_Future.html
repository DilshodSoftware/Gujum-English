65-DARS. A1 GRAMMAR REVIEW

Present · Past · Future · Hozirgi, o‘tgan va kelasi zamonlarni takrorlash
Bu darsning maqsadi: biror gap qaysi vaqtda ekanini aniqlash va mos fe’l shaklini tanlash. Har bir qoidani avval o‘qing, keyin misollarni tarjimasi bilan solishtiring.

1. Vaqtni aniqlash

VaqtQachon ishlatamiz?Misol
Presentodat, hozirgi holat yoki hozirgi ishI work. / I am working.
Pasto‘tmishda bo‘lib tugagan ish yoki holatI worked. / I was tired.
Futurekelajak rejasi, qarori yoki taxminiI am going to work. / I will work.
I work every day.Men har kuni ishlayman. (odat)
I worked yesterday.Men kecha ishladim. (o‘tgan zamon)
I will work tomorrow.Men ertaga ishlayman. (kelajak)

2. Present Simple — odatlar va umumiy haqiqatlar

Har kuni yoki tez-tez takrorlanadigan ishlar, shuningdek umumiy haqiqatlar uchun ishlatiladi.

I / you / we / they + fe’l

he / she / it + fe’l-s / -es / -ies
I drink tea every morning.Men har tong choy ichaman.
She works in a school.U maktabda ishlaydi.
The sun rises in the east.Quyosh sharqdan chiqadi.

He/she/it bilan fe’lning oxiri o‘zgaradi: play → plays, watch → watches, study → studies.

Present Simple inkor va savol

Inkor: I don’t work. / She doesn’t work.Men ishlamayman. / U ishlamaydi.
Savol: Do you work? / Does she work?Siz ishlaysizmi? / U ishlaydimi?
Does/doesn’t dan keyin asosiy fe’l oddiy shaklda keladi: Does she work?, She doesn’t work. (works emas).

3. Present Continuous — hozir davom etayotgan ish

Ayni hozir yoki shu davrda vaqtincha davom etayotgan ishni bildiradi.

Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing
I am reading now.Men hozir o‘qiyapman.
He is cooking.U ovqat pishiryapti.
They are playing at the moment.Ular ayni paytda o‘ynashyapti.

Fe’lga ko‘pincha -ing qo‘shiladi: read → reading, make → making, run → running.

Inkor: She isn’t sleeping.U uxlamayapti.
Savol: Are they working?Ular ishlayaptimi?

4. Present Simple va Present Continuous

Odat — Present SimpleHozir — Present Continuous
I read every evening.
Men har oqshom o‘qiyman.		I am reading now.
Men hozir o‘qiyapman.
She works at a bank.
U bankda ishlaydi.		She is working at home today.
U bugun uyda ishlayapti.
They play on Sundays.
Ular yakshanba kunlari o‘ynaydi.		They are playing now.
Ular hozir o‘ynashyapti.
Every day / usually / often — odatga ishora qiladi.
Now / right now / at the moment — ayni hozir davom etayotgan ishga ishora qiladi.

5. Past of To Be — Was / Were

O‘tgan zamonda kim yoki nima bo‘lganini, qanday yoki qayerda bo‘lganini aytishda was/were ishlatamiz.

I / he / she / it → was

you / we / they → were
I was at home yesterday.Men kecha uyda edim.
They were happy.Ular xursand edilar.
Inkor: He wasn’t tired.U charchagan emas edi.
Savol: Were you at school?Siz maktabda edingizmi?

6. Past Simple — o‘tgan zamondagi harakat

O‘tmishda boshlanib, tugagan ishni aytamiz.

Muntazam fe’l: -ed qo‘shiladi

work → workedishlamoq → ishladi
play → playedo‘ynamoq → o‘ynadi
study → studiedo‘rganmoq → o‘rgandi

Irregular fe’l: shakli o‘zgaradi

go → wentbormoq → bordi
see → sawko‘rmoq → ko‘rdi
eat → ateyemoq → yedi
have → hadega bo‘lmoq → bor edi

Past Simple inkor va savol

Inkor: subject + didn’t + oddiy fe’l

Savol: Did + subject + oddiy fe’l?
Inkor: She didn’t go.U bormadi.
Savol: Did you work?Siz ishladingizmi?
Qisqa javob: Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.Ha, ishladim. / Yo‘q, ishlamadim.
Did/didn’t dan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklda qoladi: Did she go?, She didn’t go. (went emas).

7. Future — kelajak

Kelajak haqida gapirishning ushbu darajadagi ikki asosiy shakli: going to va will.

Going to — oldindan qilingan reja

Subject + am/is/are + going to + fe’l
I am going to visit my aunt tomorrow.Men ertaga xolamnikiga bormoqchiman.
She is going to study tonight.U bugun kechqurun o‘qimoqchi.

Will — tezda qilingan qaror, va’da yoki taxmin

Subject + will + fe’lning oddiy shakli
I will help you.Men sizga yordam beraman. (va’da)
I think it will rain.Menimcha, yomg‘ir yog‘adi. (taxmin)

8. Qaysi zamonni tanlashni tekshiring

1. Odatmi yoki umumiy haqiqatmi? → Present Simple.
2. Hozir davom etyaptimi? → Present Continuous.
3. O‘tmishda tugaganmi? → Past Simple yoki was/were.
4. Kelajak uchun oldindan reja bormi? → Going to.
5. Va’da, fikr yoki hozir qabul qilingan qarormi? → Will.

Mashq 1 — Zamonni aniqlang

Har bir gap Present Simple, Present Continuous, Past yoki Future ekanini belgilang.

1. She goes to school every day.

2. They are watching a film now.

3. I visited my friend yesterday.

4. We are going to travel next week.

5. He will call you later.

6. They were at home last night.

Javoblar:
1. Present Simple. 2. Present Continuous. 3. Past Simple.
4. Future — going to. 5. Future — will. 6. Past of To Be.

Mashq 2 — To‘g‘ri shaklni tanlang

1. He (works / is working) every day.

2. He (works / is working) right now.

3. We (went / go) to the park last Sunday.

4. She (will visit / visited) us tomorrow.

5. I (was / am) tired yesterday.

6. They (are going to / went to) study tonight. (reja)

Javoblar:
1. works. 2. is working. 3. went.
4. will visit. 5. was. 6. are going to.
