65-AMALIYOT. A1 GRAMMAR REVIEW

Present · Past · Future · Aralash takrorlash va javoblar kaliti
Ishlash tartibi: avval mashqlarni javoblar kalitiga qaramasdan bajaring. Har bir gapda vaqtni aniqlang: odatmi, ayni hozirmi, o‘tganmi yoki kelajakmi? So‘ng yordamchi fe’l va asosiy fe’l shaklini tekshiring.

1-qism — Zamonni aniqlang

Gaplarning zamonini belgilang: Present Simple, Present Continuous, Past Simple yoki Future.

1. I walk to school every day.

2. My sister is cooking now.

3. We visited Samarkand last year.

4. They were at home yesterday.

5. He is going to buy a new phone.

6. I think it will rain tonight.

Javoblar: 1. Present Simple; 2. Present Continuous; 3. Past Simple; 4. Past of To Be; 5. Future — going to; 6. Future — will.

2-qism — Present Simple yoki Present Continuous?

7. She (works / is working) at a bank every day.

8. Look! The children (play / are playing) outside.

9. I (read / am reading) a book right now.

10. My brother usually (walks / is walking) to work.

11. They (stay / are staying) with their aunt this week. (vaqtinchalik)

Javoblar: 7. works; 8. are playing; 9. am reading; 10. walks; 11. are staying.

3-qism — To Be: am/is/are, was/were

12. I ___ tired today. (am / is)

13. The students ___ in the classroom now. (is / are)

14. We ___ at the museum yesterday. (was / were)

15. My father ___ busy last night. (was / were)

16. Inkor qiling: She was at work.

17. Savol tuzing: They are ready.

Javoblar: 12. am; 13. are; 14. were; 15. was; 16. She wasn’t at work; 17. Are they ready?

4-qism — Present Simple: darak, inkor va savol

18. My mother ___ (watch) TV in the evening.

19. The boys ___ (study) English after school.

20. Inkor qiling: He likes coffee.

21. Savol tuzing: Sara reads every night.

22. ___ your friends live nearby? (Do / Does)

23. Does Tom ___ (go / goes) to school by bus?

Javoblar: 18. watches; 19. study; 20. He doesn’t like coffee; 21. Does Sara read every night?; 22. Do; 23. go.

5-qism — O‘tgan harakat va Did

24. clean → Past Simple: ___

25. buy → Past Simple: ___

26. They ___ (go) to the park yesterday.

27. Inkor qiling: She saw the film.

28. Savol tuzing: You worked last night.

29. Did he ___ (eat / ate) breakfast?

Javoblar: 24. cleaned; 25. bought; 26. went; 27. She didn’t see the film; 28. Did you work last night?; 29. eat.

6-qism — Kelajak: Going to yoki Will

30. Oldindan reja: We ___ going to visit our cousins. (are / will)

31. Going to dan keyin fe’lni tanlang: He is going to ___ (study / studies).

32. Va’da: I ___ help you. (will / was)

33. Hozir qaror: “Eshikni men ochaman!” I ___ open the door. (will / am going)

34. Bulutlarni ko‘rib taxmin: It ___ going to rain. (is / will)

Javoblar: 30. are; 31. study; 32. will; 33. will; 34. is.

7-qism — Xatoni tuzating

35. She is go to school every day.

36. He didn’t went home yesterday.

37. They was tired last night.

38. I am study English now.

Javoblar: 35. She goes to school every day. 36. He didn’t go home yesterday. 37. They were tired last night. 38. I am studying English now.

8-qism — Tarjima qiling

39. Men har kuni nonushta qilaman.

40. Ular hozir film tomosha qilyapti.

41. Biz kecha bu yerda emas edik.

42. Men ertaga senga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmoqchiman. (oldindan reja)

Namunaviy javoblar:
39. I have breakfast every day. 40. They are watching a film now.
41. We weren’t here yesterday. 42. I am going to call you tomorrow.
O‘zini tekshirish: xato qilgan savollaringizni tegishli darsdagi qoida bilan qayta ko‘rib chiqing. Javobni yodlab qolishdan tashqari, nima uchun shu zamon va fe’l shakli ishlatilganini tushuntirib ko‘ring.
