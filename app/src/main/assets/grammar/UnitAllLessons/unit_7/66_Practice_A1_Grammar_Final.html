66-AMALIYOT. A1 GRAMMAR FINAL PRACTICE

70 ta aralash topshiriq · Savollar · Inkor · Gap tuzish · Javoblar kaliti
Qanday ishlash kerak? Har bo‘limni mustaqil bajaring. Ba’zi savollarda variant tanlaysiz, ba’zilarida gapni o‘zgartirasiz, tartibga keltirasiz yoki o‘zingiz yozasiz. Avval javoblar kalitiga qaramang. Ochiq topshiriqlarda namunaviy javoblar berilgan; mazmuni to‘g‘ri boshqa javoblar ham qabul qilinadi.

1-bo‘lim — Gap qurilishi, olmosh va otlar

1. “The girl reads a book.” Gapdan subject, verb va objectni toping.

2. So‘zlarni tartiblang: every day / I / English / study.

3. I see (he / him) at school.

4. We visit Ali and Sara. → We visit ___.

5. This is (my / mine) phone.

6. Yaqinda turgan bir nechta kalit: (This / These) are my keys.

7. So‘zlarni tartiblang: a / red / car.

Javoblar:
1. Subject: the girl; verb: reads; object: a book. 2. I study English every day.
3. him. 4. them. 5. my. 6. These. 7. a red car.

2-bo‘lim — To Be, There Is/Are va egalik

8. I ___ tired now. (am / is / are)

9. Inkor qiling: She is busy.

10. Savol tuzing: You are at home.

11. Are you ready? (ha, qisqa javob)

12. My parents ___ at the park yesterday. (was / were)

13. There ___ two apples on the plate. (is / are)

14. “Qizning sumkasi”ni ’s bilan yozing.

Javoblar:
8. am. 9. She isn’t busy. 10. Are you at home? 11. Yes, I am.
12. were. 13. are. 14. the girl’s bag.

3-bo‘lim — Present Simple: darak, inkor va savol

15. My sister ___ (study) every evening.

16. Inkor qiling: We drink coffee.

17. Does he ___ (play / plays) tennis?

18. Bo‘sh joyni to‘ldiring: Where ___ Anna live? (do / does)

19. Usually so‘zini to‘g‘ri joyga qo‘ying: I / get up / at seven.

20. Does your brother work here? (yo‘q, qisqa javob)

21. He ___ a new bicycle. (have / has)

Javoblar:
15. studies. 16. We don’t drink coffee. 17. play. 18. does.
19. I usually get up at seven. 20. No, he doesn’t. 21. has.

4-bo‘lim — Present Continuous va odatni farqlash

22. They ___ watching TV now. (am / is / are)

23. make fe’liga -ing qo‘shing.

24. Inkor qiling: He is sleeping.

25. Savol tuzing: You are listening.

26. I (walk / am walking) to work every day.

27. Look! The dog (runs / is running) after the ball.

28. Xatoni tuzating: She is know my name.

Javoblar:
22. are. 23. making. 24. He isn’t sleeping. 25. Are you listening?
26. walk. 27. is running. 28. She knows my name.

5-bo‘lim — Past Simple va Was/Were

29. stop fe’lini Past Simple ga o‘tkazing.

30. go fe’lining Past Simple shaklini yozing.

31. Inkor qiling: He went to school.

32. Savol tuzing: They saw the film.

33. Did you call your friend? (ha, qisqa javob)

34. We ___ at home yesterday. (was / were)

35. Xatoni tuzating: Did she went home?

Javoblar:
29. stopped. 30. went. 31. He didn’t go to school.
32. Did they see the film? 33. Yes, I did. 34. were.
35. Did she go home?

6-bo‘lim — Kelajak va Can

36. I ___ going to study tonight. (am / are)

37. Going to dan keyin to‘g‘ri fe’lni tanlang: She is going to (cook / cooks).

38. I think they ___ win tomorrow. (will / were)

39. Savol tuzing: He will come later.

40. Oldindan reja uchun mosini belgilang: (going to / will)

41. He ___ swim. (can / cans)

42. “Yugurma!” deb buyruq bering.

Javoblar:
36. am. 37. cook. 38. will. 39. Will he come later?
40. going to. 41. can. 42. Don’t run!

7-bo‘lim — Savol so‘zlari va miqdor

43. ___ is your name? (What / Where)

44. ___ is the library? (Who / Where)

45. ___ lives next door? (Who subject bo‘lsa: do / yordamchisiz)

46. ___ old is your sister? (How / What)

47. How ___ books do you have? (many / much)

48. ___ ___ is this ticket? (narxni so‘rang)

49. When ___ you study English? (do / are)

Javoblar: 43. What; 44. Where; 45. yordamchisiz — Who lives next door?; 46. How; 47. many; 48. How much; 49. do.

8-bo‘lim — Artikllar, miqdor va predloglar

50. I have ___ umbrella. (a / an)

51. Darak gap: There is ___ milk in the fridge. (some / any)

52. We don’t have ___ eggs. (some / any)

53. How ___ money do you need? (many / much)

54. Countable yoki uncountable: rice?

55. The keys are ___ the bag. (in / on) — ichida.

56. The lesson starts ___ 8 o’clock. (at / on / in)

Javoblar: 50. an; 51. some; 52. any; 53. much; 54. uncountable; 55. in; 56. at.

9-bo‘lim — Sifatlar va bog‘lovchilar

57. Superlative shakl: tall → ___

58. Comparative shakl: happy → ___

59. To‘g‘ri yozuvni tanlang: two (small / smalls) cats.

60. I was tired, ___ I went to bed. (because / so)

61. I stayed home ___ I was sick. (because / so)

62. I like tea ___ coffee. (ikkalasi ham)

63. “This is ___ most interesting book.” (a / the)

Javoblar: 57. the tallest; 58. happier; 59. small; 60. so; 61. because; 62. and; 63. the.

10-bo‘lim — Would Like va yakuniy gaplar

64. “Men biroz suv xohlardim.” Would like bilan tarjima qiling.

65. would like dan keyin fe’l kelsa, to kerakmi?

66. Muloyim taklif tuzing: “Choy xohlaysizmi?”

67. So‘zlarni tartiblang: yesterday / my friends / a film / watched.

68. So‘zlarni tartiblang: because / I / stayed home / was tired.

69. Xatoni tuzating: She would likes coffee.

70. Inglizchaga tarjima qiling: “Biz ertaga buvimiznikiga bormoqchimiz.”

Javoblar:
64. I would like some water. 65. Ha: would like to + fe’l.
66. Would you like some tea? 67. My friends watched a film yesterday.
68. I stayed home because I was tired. 69. She would like coffee.
70. We are going to visit our grandmother tomorrow.
O‘zini baholash: noto‘g‘ri javoblaringizni alohida belgilang, tegishli mavzuning qoidasi bilan qayta tanishing va javobni yana bir marta mustaqil yozing. Maqsad faqat ball emas — gap nima uchun shunday tuzilishini tushunish.
