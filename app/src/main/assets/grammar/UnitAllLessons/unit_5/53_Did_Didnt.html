53-DARS. DID / DIDN’T

Past Simple yordamchi fe’li va “do” fe’lining o‘tgan shakli

Dars lug‘ati

Bu darsda uchraydigan so‘zlar — oldin eshitib, keyin darsni o‘qing.

did — qildi
didn’t — qilmadi
day — kun
dishes — idishlar
every — har
go — borish
home — uy
homework — uy vazifasi
last — oxirgi
my — mening

play — o‘ynamoq

your — sening
Bu darsda bir xil yoziladigan did so‘zining ikki vazifasini farqlaymiz: u “qildi” ma’nosidagi asosiy fe’l bo‘lishi mumkin yoki savol/inkor tuzuvchi yordamchi fe’l bo‘lishi mumkin.

1. Do — “qilmoq” fe’li

Ba’zi gaplarda do o‘zi asosiy fe’l bo‘lib, “qilmoq” degan ma’noni beradi. Uning Past Simple shakli — did.

I do my homework every day.Men har kuni uy vazifamni qilaman.
I did my homework yesterday.Men kecha uy vazifamni qildim.
She did the dishes last night.U kecha tunda idishlarni yuvdi.

2. Did — yordamchi fe’l

Savol yoki inkor gapda did o‘tgan zamonni bildirishga yordam beradi. Bu vazifada u odatda alohida “qildi” deb tarjima qilinmaydi.

Did you work yesterday?Kecha ishladingizmi? (did — yordamchi)
I didn’t work yesterday.Men kecha ishlamadim. (didn’t — yordamchi)

3. Bitta gapda ikkita “do” kelishi mumkin

Agar asosiy fe’lning o‘zi “qilmoq” bo‘lsa, savol yoki inkor gapda birinchi did yordamchi bo‘ladi, undan keyingi do esa asosiy fe’lning oddiy shakli bo‘ladi.

Did you do your homework?Uy vazifangni qildingmi?
I didn’t do my homework.Men uy vazifamni qilmadim.
Gapdagi so‘zVazifasi
Did you do homework?Birinchi did — yordamchi; do — asosiy “qilmoq” fe’li.
I did homework.Did — “qildim” ma’nosidagi asosiy fe’l.

4. Did barcha egalar bilan bir xil

O‘tgan zamondagi savol va inkorda subject he/she/it bo‘lsa ham did ishlatiladi. Does hozirgi zamon uchun, did o‘tgan zamon uchun.

Do you play? → Did you play?O‘ynaysizmi? → O‘ynadingizmi?
Does he work? → Did he work?U ishlaydimi? → U ishladimi?
She doesn’t go. → She didn’t go.U bormaydi. → U bormadi.

5. Did yordamchi bo‘lsa, asosiy fe’l oddiy shaklda

Did o‘tgan zamonni allaqachon ko‘rsatadi. Shuning uchun undan keyin fe’lning asosiy shaklini ishlatamiz.

Did she go? ✅   Did she went? ❌
He didn’t see it. ✅   He didn’t saw it. ❌
Did they work? ✅   Did they worked? ❌

6. Was/Were yoki Did?

Was/were — to be fe’lining o‘tgan shakli. Oddiy harakat fe’li bilan did ishlatiladi.

Was she tired?U charchagan edimi? (to be)
Did she work?U ishladimi? (harakat fe’li)
He wasn’t at home.U uyda emas edi. (to be inkori)
He didn’t go home.U uyga bormadi. (harakat inkori)

Mashq 1 — Did yordamchimi yoki asosiy fe’lmi?

Qalin yozilgan shaklning vazifasini belgilang.

1. I did my homework yesterday.

2. Did you see Ali?

3. She didn’t call me.

4. They did the work last week.

Javoblar:
1. Asosiy “qilmoq” fe’li. 2. Yordamchi fe’l.
3. Yordamchi fe’l. 4. Asosiy “qilmoq” fe’li.

Mashq 2 — To‘g‘ri fe’lni tanlang

1. Did you (go / went) home?

2. She didn’t (eat / ate) breakfast.

3. Did he (do / did) his homework?

4. We didn’t (see / saw) the film.

Javoblar:
1. go. 2. eat. 3. do. 4. see.
