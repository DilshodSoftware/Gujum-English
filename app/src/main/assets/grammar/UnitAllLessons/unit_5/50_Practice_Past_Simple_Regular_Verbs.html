50-AMALIYOT. PAST SIMPLE — REGULAR VERBS

-ed qo‘shimchasi · o‘tgan va tugagan harakat
Muntazam fe’lning Past Simple shakli odatda -ed bilan yasaladi va barcha egalar bilan bir xil: I worked, she worked, they worked. Oxiri e, undosh+y yoki ayrim qisqa fe’llardagi imlo qoidalariga e’tibor bering.

1-mashq — Fe’lning o‘tgan shaklini yozing

1. work → ___   2. help → ___   3. live → ___

4. study → ___   5. play → ___   6. stop → ___

7. watch → ___   8. enjoy → ___

Javoblar: 1. worked; 2. helped; 3. lived; 4. studied; 5. played; 6. stopped; 7. watched; 8. enjoyed.

2-mashq — Imlo qoidasini aniqlang

1. like → liked (e bilan tugaydi / y o‘zgaradi)

2. carry → carried (undosh+y / unli+y)

3. play → played (undosh+y / unli+y)

4. plan → planned (qisqa fe’l, oxirgi undosh ikki marta yoziladi / oddiy -ed)

Javoblar: 1. e bilan tugaydi, faqat -d; 2. undosh+y, y→i + ed; 3. unli+y, faqat -ed; 4. qisqa fe’lning oxirgi undoshi ikki marta yoziladi.

3-mashq — Gapni Past Simple bilan to‘ldiring

1. We ___ the room yesterday. (clean)

2. He ___ his aunt last week. (visit)

3. I ___ the door an hour ago. (open)

4. Sara ___ English yesterday. (study)

5. The bus ___ near the school. (stop)

Javoblar: 1. cleaned; 2. visited; 3. opened; 4. studied; 5. stopped.

4-mashq — Vaqt iborasini gapga moslang

yesterday · last night · two days ago · every day

1. We watched a film ___.

2. I visit my grandmother ___. (o‘tgan zamon)

3. She called me ___.

4. “Har kuni” qaysi ibora? ___

Javoblar (namuna): 1. last night; 2. two days ago; 3. yesterday; 4. every day. Eslatma: every day odatni bildiradi, Past Simple vaqt belgisi emas.

5-mashq — -ED talaffuzini tasniflang

1. worked   2. played   3. wanted

Tanlang: “t”ga yaqin / “d”ga yaqin / alohida “id” bo‘g‘ini

Javoblar: 1. “t”ga yaqin; 2. “d”ga yaqin; 3. alohida “id” bo‘g‘ini.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. She studyed yesterday.

2. We stoped at the shop.

3. They liveed in a small town.

4. He plaied football last Sunday.

Javoblar: 1. She studied yesterday. 2. We stopped at the shop. 3. They lived in a small town. 4. He played football last Sunday.
