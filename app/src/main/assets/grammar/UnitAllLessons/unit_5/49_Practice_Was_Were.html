49-AMALIYOT. WAS / WERE

O‘tgan zamondagi holat, kimlik yoki joy · inkor · savol · qisqa javob
I/he/she/it/bitta ega → was. You/we/they/ko‘plik → were. Inkor: wasn’t/weren’t. Savolda was/were egadan oldinga chiqadi. Bu fe’llar bilan did qo‘shilmaydi.

1-mashq — Was yoki Were?

1. I ___ at home last night.

2. My friends ___ tired yesterday.

3. The weather ___ cold.

4. You ___ very kind.

5. Ali and Sara ___ at the park.

6. The book ___ on the desk.

Javoblar: 1. was; 2. were; 3. was; 4. were; 5. were; 6. was.

2-mashq — Inkor shakliga o‘tkazing

1. She was busy yesterday.

2. We were at school.

3. The film was interesting.

4. They were late.

Javoblar: 1. She wasn’t busy yesterday. 2. We weren’t at school. 3. The film wasn’t interesting. 4. They weren’t late.

3-mashq — Gapni savolga aylantiring

1. He was at work.

2. You were happy.

3. The children were hungry.

4. It was sunny yesterday.

Javoblar: 1. Was he at work? 2. Were you happy? 3. Were the children hungry? 4. Was it sunny yesterday?

4-mashq — Qisqa javob yozing

1. Were you at home? (ha, o‘zingiz haqingizda)

2. Was Sara tired? (yo‘q)

3. Were they friends? (ha)

4. Was the shop open? (yo‘q)

Javoblar: 1. Yes, I was. 2. No, she wasn’t. 3. Yes, they were. 4. No, it wasn’t.

5-mashq — Yesterday, Last yoki Ago bilan to‘ldiring

1. I was at the cinema ___ night.

2. They were in Bukhara ___ week.

3. He was sick two days ___.

4. We were busy ___.

Javoblar: 1. last; 2. last; 3. ago; 4. yesterday.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. They was happy.

2. Did she was at school?

3. I were tired.

4. He didn’t was at home.

Javoblar: 1. They were happy. 2. Was she at school? 3. I was tired. 4. He wasn’t at home.
