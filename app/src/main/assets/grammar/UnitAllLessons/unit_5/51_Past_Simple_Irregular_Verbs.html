51-DARS. PAST SIMPLE — IRREGULAR VERBS

O‘tgan zamondagi qoidaga bo‘ysunmaydigan fe’llar
Oldingi dars: muntazam fe’llarga o‘tgan zamonda -ed qo‘shdik: work → worked. Lekin ayrim juda ko‘p ishlatiladigan fe’llar -ed olmaydi — ularning shakli o‘zgaradi. Ularni irregular verbs deyiladi.

1. Irregular verb nima?

Irregular — “qoidaga bo‘ysunmaydigan”. Bunday fe’lning o‘tgan zamon shaklini alohida yodlaymiz.

3. Barcha egalar bilan o‘tgan shakl bir xil

Past Simple darak gapida irregular fe’lning o‘tgan shakli ega o‘zgarsa ham o‘zgarmaydi.

I went home.Men uyga bordim.
She went home.U uyga bordi.
They went home.Ular uyga borishdi.
Hozirgi zamonda he/she/it bilan fe’lga -s qo‘shiladi: He goes. O‘tgan zamonda esa hamma egalar bilan went: I went, he went, they went.

4. Irregular fe’llar bilan darak gaplar

6. Past of Be alohida

Be fe’lining o‘tgan zamon shakli oddiy fe’llar kabi emas: was/were. Uni 49-darsda ko‘rdik.

I was at home.Men uyda edim.
They were happy.Ular xursand edilar.
I went home.Men uyga bordim. (harakat fe’li)

7. Past Simple darak gap qolipi

Subject + irregular verb (past form) + ...
I + went + to school.Men maktabga bordim.
She + ate + an apple.U olma yedi.
They + saw + a film.Ular film ko‘rdi.
Ushbu dars darak gaplarni o‘rgatadi. Irregular fe’llar bilan inkor va savol yasashda keyingi darslarda did/didn’t ishlatamiz.

Mashq 1 — Past shaklini toping

1. go → ___   2. see → ___   3. eat → ___

4. have → ___   5. make → ___   6. buy → ___

7. run → ___   8. write → ___

Javoblar:
1. went. 2. saw. 3. ate. 4. had.
5. made. 6. bought. 7. ran. 8. wrote.

Mashq 2 — Qavsdagi fe’lni Past Simple ga o‘tkazing

1. We ___ home late. (come)

2. I ___ my homework yesterday. (do)

3. He ___ a sandwich. (eat)

4. They ___ their keys. (find)

5. My friend ___ me a story. (tell)

Javoblar:
1. came. 2. did. 3. ate. 4. found. 5. told.

Mashq 3 — To‘g‘ri fe’lni tanlang

1. Yesterday we (go / went) to the park.

2. She (buy / bought) a new bag last week.

3. I (saw / see) him two days ago.

4. They (maked / made) dinner.

Javoblar:
1. went. 2. bought. 3. saw. 4. made.
