54-AMALIYOT. PAST SIMPLE — SAVOLLAR

Did + ega + oddiy fe’l? · What / Where / When / Why
Oddiy harakat fe’li: Did + ega + fe’lning oddiy shakli? To be: Was/Were egadan oldinga chiqadi. WH so‘roqda savol so‘zi boshida keladi: Where did they go?

1-mashq — Did yoki Was/Were?

1. ___ you see the movie? (Did / Were)

2. ___ they at the station? (Did / Were)

3. ___ Sara call you? (Did / Was)

4. ___ he tired last night? (Did / Was)

5. ___ your parents travel last year? (Did / Were)

Javoblar: 1. Did; 2. Were; 3. Did; 4. Was; 5. Did.

2-mashq — Darak gapdan yes/no savol tuzing

1. You cleaned your room.

2. He bought a new phone.

3. They visited their cousins.

4. She was at school.

Javoblar: 1. Did you clean your room? 2. Did he buy a new phone? 3. Did they visit their cousins? 4. Was she at school?

3-mashq — Savol so‘zini qo‘shib tuzing

1. ___ did you go? (joy)

2. ___ did they arrive? (vaqt)

3. ___ did he eat? (narsa)

4. ___ did she stay home? (sabab)

Javoblar: 1. Where; 2. When; 3. What; 4. Why.

4-mashq — So‘zlarni savol tartibiga keltiring

1. did / what / you / buy / ?

2. where / they / did / stay / ?

3. she / did / when / call / ?

4. was / why / he / late / ?

Javoblar: 1. What did you buy? 2. Where did they stay? 3. When did she call? 4. Why was he late?

5-mashq — Does, Did yoki Was?

1. ___ he work here now? (hozirgi zamon)

2. ___ he work here last year? (o‘tgan zamon)

3. ___ she at home yesterday?

4. ___ they play tennis last Sunday?

Javoblar: 1. Does; 2. Did; 3. Was; 4. Did.

6-mashq — Xatolarni tuzating

1. Did you went to the park?

2. Where she did live?

3. Did they were at home?

4. What did he ate?

Javoblar: 1. Did you go to the park? 2. Where did she live? 3. Were they at home? 4. What did he eat?
