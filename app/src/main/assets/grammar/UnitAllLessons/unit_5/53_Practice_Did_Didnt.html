53-AMALIYOT. DID / DIDN’T

Did yordamchimi yoki “qildi” fe’limi? · asosiy fe’l shakli
Did savol/inkorda yordamchi bo‘ladi: Did you work? Did yana “qildi” ma’nosidagi do fe’lining o‘tgan shakli bo‘lishi ham mumkin: I did my homework.

1-mashq — Did ning vazifasini aniqlang

1. Did you call your friend?

2. She did her homework yesterday.

3. We didn’t clean the kitchen.

4. They did the exercise last night.

Javoblar: 1. yordamchi; 2. asosiy “qilmoq” fe’li; 3. yordamchi; 4. asosiy “qilmoq” fe’li.

2-mashq — Did yoki Didn’t dan keyin fe’lni tanlang

1. Did she (go / went) home?

2. They didn’t (saw / see) the dog.

3. Did he (do / did) the dishes?

4. I didn’t (bought / buy) anything.

5. Did you (played / play) chess?

Javoblar: 1. go; 2. see; 3. do; 4. buy; 5. play.

3-mashq — “Do” asosiy fe’l bo‘lganda

1. I ___ my homework yesterday. (did / do)

2. ___ you ___ your homework last night? (Did / do)

3. He ___ the work yesterday. (did / does)

4. They didn’t ___ the dishes. (did / do)

Javoblar: 1. did; 2. Did, do; 3. did; 4. do.

4-mashq — Do/Does ni Did ga o‘zgartiring

1. Do you play football? → o‘tgan zamon

2. Does she work here? → o‘tgan zamon

3. He doesn’t eat meat. → o‘tgan zamon

Javoblar: 1. Did you play football? 2. Did she work here? 3. He didn’t eat meat.

5-mashq — Was/Were yoki Did/Didn’t?

1. ___ he at home yesterday? (to be savoli)

2. ___ he go home yesterday? (harakat savoli)

3. They ___ not ready. (to be inkori)

4. They ___ not finish the work. (harakat inkori)

Javoblar: 1. Was; 2. Did; 3. were; 4. did.

6-mashq — Xatolarni tuzating

1. Did she did her homework?

2. I didn’t did the dishes.

3. Did he went to school?

4. Did they were tired?

Javoblar: 1. Did she do her homework? 2. I didn’t do the dishes. 3. Did he go to school? 4. Were they tired?
