51-AMALIYOT. IRREGULAR VERBS

Go/went · see/saw · eat/ate · o‘tgan shaklini yodlang
Irregular fe’llar odatda -ed olmaydi; shaklini alohida eslab qolamiz. Past Simple darak gapida fe’l barcha egalar bilan bir xil o‘tgan shaklda qoladi.

1-mashq — Juft shakllarni moslang

1. go   2. come   3. see   4. eat   5. drink

A. drank   B. saw   C. went   D. came   E. ate

Javoblar: 1–C; 2–D; 3–B; 4–E; 5–A.

2-mashq — Past shaklini yozing

1. have → ___   2. do → ___   3. buy → ___

4. make → ___   5. take → ___   6. give → ___

7. find → ___   8. think → ___   9. read → ___

Javoblar: 1. had; 2. did; 3. bought; 4. made; 5. took; 6. gave; 7. found; 8. thought; 9. read (talaffuzi “red”).

3-mashq — Gapni Past Simple bilan to‘ldiring

1. We ___ to the park yesterday. (go)

2. She ___ a sandwich for lunch. (eat)

3. I ___ my friend at the shop. (see)

4. They ___ a new table last month. (buy)

5. He ___ a letter last night. (write)

Javoblar: 1. went; 2. ate; 3. saw; 4. bought; 5. wrote.

4-mashq — Muntazammi yoki irregular?

1. wanted   2. went   3. cleaned   4. bought   5. visited   6. saw

Javoblar: Muntazam (-ed): wanted, cleaned, visited. Irregular: went, bought, saw.

5-mashq — Mos fe’lni tanlang

1. I (goed / went) home early.

2. She (eated / ate) an orange.

3. We (saw / seed) a bird.

4. Tom (buyed / bought) a book.

5. They (comed / came) late.

Javoblar: 1. went; 2. ate; 3. saw; 4. bought; 5. came.

6-mashq — O‘tgan kun haqida gap tuzing

1. (I / go / to the market / yesterday)

2. (My sister / make / a cake / last night)

3. (We / see / our teacher / two days ago)

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. I went to the market yesterday. 2. My sister made a cake last night. 3. We saw our teacher two days ago.
