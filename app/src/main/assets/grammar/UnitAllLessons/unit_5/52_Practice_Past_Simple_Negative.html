52-AMALIYOT. PAST SIMPLE — INKOR

Didn’t + fe’lning oddiy shakli · didn’t go, didn’t play
Qolip: ega + didn’t + fe’lning oddiy shakli. Did o‘tgan zamonni ko‘rsatadi, shuning uchun asosiy fe’lga -ed ham, irregular past shakli ham qo‘ymang. Was/were esa alohida inkor qilinadi.

1-mashq — Didn’t bilan gapni to‘ldiring

1. I ___ watch TV yesterday.

2. He ___ visit his uncle.

3. We ___ go to the park.

4. Sara ___ eat breakfast.

5. They ___ play tennis last week.

Javoblar: 1–5. didn’t.

2-mashq — Asosiy fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing

1. She didn’t ___ (go / went) to work.

2. They didn’t ___ (played / play) football.

3. I didn’t ___ (saw / see) the film.

4. Tom didn’t ___ (studied / study) yesterday.

5. We didn’t ___ (ate / eat) lunch.

Javoblar: 1. go; 2. play; 3. see; 4. study; 5. eat.

3-mashq — Darak gapni inkorga aylantiring

1. He worked last night.

2. We went to school by bus.

3. Anna bought a new bag.

4. They watched the match.

Javoblar: 1. He didn’t work last night. 2. We didn’t go to school by bus. 3. Anna didn’t buy a new bag. 4. They didn’t watch the match.

4-mashq — Didn’t yoki Wasn’t/Weren’t?

1. I ___ at home yesterday. (to be inkori)

2. She ___ call me. (harakat inkori)

3. They ___ tired last night. (to be inkori)

4. We ___ see the teacher. (harakat inkori)

Javoblar: 1. wasn’t; 2. didn’t; 3. weren’t; 4. didn’t.

5-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. He didn’t went to work.

2. I didn’t watched TV.

3. They didn’t saw us.

4. She didn’t was happy.

5. We not play yesterday.

Javoblar: 1. He didn’t go to work. 2. I didn’t watch TV. 3. They didn’t see us. 4. She wasn’t happy. 5. We didn’t play yesterday.

6-mashq — Tarjima qiling

1. Men kecha ishlamadim.

2. U (qiz) filmni ko‘rmadi.

3. Biz o‘tgan hafta bu yerga kelmadik.

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. I didn’t work yesterday. 2. She didn’t see the film. 3. We didn’t come here last week.
