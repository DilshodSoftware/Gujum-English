50-DARS. PAST SIMPLE — REGULAR VERBS

Past Simple · O‘tgan zamon · To‘g‘ri fe’llar
Past Simple o‘tmishda boshlangan va tugagan ish yoki hodisa haqida gapirish uchun ishlatiladi. Bu darsda o‘tgan zamoni odatda -ed bilan yasaladigan muntazam fe’llarni o‘rganamiz.

1. Regular verb — muntazam fe’l

Muntazam fe’llarning o‘tgan zamon shakli odatda fe’l oxiriga -ed qo‘shish orqali yasaladi.

oddiy fe’l + ed = Past Simple
work → workedishlamoq → ishladi
play → playedo‘ynamoq → o‘ynadi
clean → cleanedtozalamoq → tozaladi
watch → watchedtomosha qilmoq → tomosha qildi

O‘tgan zamon shakli barcha egalar bilan bir xil qoladi: I, you, he, she, we, they — hammasi worked bilan ishlatiladi.

I worked yesterday.Men kecha ishladim.
She worked yesterday.U kecha ishladi.
They worked yesterday.Ular kecha ishladi.
Present Simple da he works deb -s qo‘shiladi. Past Simple darak gapda esa barcha egalar bilan worked — -ed shakli ishlatiladi.

2. Ko‘p fe’llarga -ed qo‘shamiz

Fe’lning oxiri odatiy harf bilan tugasa, oxiriga -ed yozing.

3. Fe’l oxiri E bilan tugasa: faqat -d

Fe’l oxirida allaqachon e bo‘lsa, yana -ed emas, faqat -d qo‘shamiz.

live → livedyashamoq → yashadi
like → likedyoqtirmoq → yoqtirdi
use → usedfoydalanmoq → foydalandi
We lived in Tashkent.Biz Toshkentda yashadik.

4. Undosh + Y: Y → IED

Fe’l undosh harf + y bilan tugasa, y ni i ga almashtirib, -ed qo‘shamiz. Oxirida -ied bo‘ladi.

study → studiedo‘rganmoq → o‘rgandi
try → triedurinmoq → urindi
cry → criedyig‘lamoq → yig‘ladi
study so‘zida y dan oldin d undoshi bor: study → studied.

5. Unli + Y: faqat -ed qo‘shiladi

Y dan oldingi harf unli bo‘lsa, y o‘zgarmaydi. Fe’l oxiriga -ed qo‘shamiz.

play → playedo‘ynamoq → o‘ynadi
enjoy → enjoyedzavqlanmoq → zavqlandi
stay → stayedqolmoq → qoldi
study → studied (undosh + y)
play → played (unli + y)

6. Qisqa fe’l oxiridagi undosh ikki marta yozilishi mumkin

Ba’zi qisqa fe’llarda oxirgi undosh ikki marta yozilib, keyin -ed qo‘shiladi. Bu ko‘pincha qisqa unli tovushli, bir bo‘g‘inli fe’llarda uchraydi.

stop → stoppedto‘xtamoq → to‘xtadi
plan → plannedrejalashtirmoq → rejalashtirdi
The bus stopped here.Avtobus shu yerda to‘xtadi.
Hozircha eng ko‘p uchraydigan stop → stopped shaklini eslab qoling. Barcha fe’llarga oxirgi harfni ikki marta yozavermaymiz.

8. -ED qanday aytiladi?

Yozuvda -ed bir xil ko‘rinsa ham, talaffuzi har doim alohida “ed” bo‘g‘ini bo‘lavermaydi.

9. Darak gap qolipi

Subject + regular verb-ed + ...
I worked yesterday.Men kecha ishladim.
She visited her friend.U dugonasining oldiga bordi.
They played football last week.Ular o‘tgan hafta futbol o‘ynadi.
Bu darsda Past Simple darak gaplarini o‘rganamiz. Inkor va savol uchun fe’lning boshqa qolipi bo‘ladi; ularni keyingi darslarda o‘rganamiz.

Mashq 1 — Fe’lni Past Simple ga o‘tkazing

1. work   2. live   3. study

4. play   5. stop   6. visit

Javoblar:
1. worked. 2. lived. 3. studied.
4. played. 5. stopped. 6. visited.

Mashq 2 — Qavsdagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri yozing

1. I ___ my room yesterday. (clean)

2. Sara ___ English last year. (study)

3. We ___ at home last night. (stay)

4. The bus ___ near the school. (stop)

5. They ___ their grandmother last week. (visit)

Javoblar:
1. cleaned. 2. studied. 3. stayed. 4. stopped. 5. visited.

Mashq 3 — Xatoni tuzating

1. I work yesterday.

2. She studyed English.

3. They stoped the car.

4. He play football last week.

Javoblar:
1. I worked yesterday. 2. She studied English.
3. They stopped the car. 4. He played football last week.
