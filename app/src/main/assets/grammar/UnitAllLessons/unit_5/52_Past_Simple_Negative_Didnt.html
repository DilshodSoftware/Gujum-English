52-DARS. PAST SIMPLE — NEGATIVE

Did not / Didn’t · O‘tgan zamondagi inkor gap
Oldingi darslar: o‘tgan zamon darak gapida worked yoki went kabi o‘tgan fe’l ishlatamiz. Endi “qilmadim”, “bormadi” kabi inkor gaplarni tuzamiz.

1. Did not / Didn’t nima?

Did not yoki uning qisqa shakli didn’t harakat o‘tmishda bajarilmaganini bildiradi. O‘zbekchaga ko‘pincha “...madi / ...madim” deb tarjima qilinadi.

Subject + did not / didn’t + fe’lning oddiy shakli
I didn’t work yesterday.Men kecha ishlamadim.
She didn’t call me.U menga qo‘ng‘iroq qilmadi.
They did not play football.Ular futbol o‘ynamadi.

2. Did barcha egalar bilan bir xil

Past Simple inkorida I, you, he, she, it, we, they — barcha egalar bilan didn’t ishlatiladi. Ega he yoki she bo‘lsa ham doesn’t ishlatmaymiz, chunki gap o‘tgan zamonda.

EgaInkor yordamchisiMisol
Ididn’tI didn’t go.
youdidn’tYou didn’t go.
he / she / itdidn’tShe didn’t go.
we / theydidn’tThey didn’t go.

3. Didn’t dan keyin fe’l oddiy shaklga qaytadi

Did o‘tgan zamonni bildirib turibdi. Shuning uchun undan keyin asosiy fe’lga -ed qo‘shmaymiz va irregular fe’lning o‘tgan shaklini ishlatmaymiz.

Darak: I worked.Men ishladim.
Inkor: I didn’t work.Men ishlamadim.
Darak: She went.U bordi.
Inkor: She didn’t go.U bormadi.
He didn’t play. ✅
He didn’t played. ❌
They didn’t see me. ✅
They didn’t saw me. ❌

5. Past of To Be bilan aralashtirmang

Was/were ning o‘z inkor shakli bor: was not / wasn’t va were not / weren’t. To be bilan didn’t ishlatmaymiz.

I wasn’t at home.Men uyda emas edim.
They weren’t tired.Ular charchamagan edi.
I didn’t work.Men ishlamadim. (oddiy harakat fe’li)
She wasn’t happy. ✅
She didn’t be happy. ❌
She didn’t work. ✅

6. O‘tgan zamon ko‘rsatkichlari bilan

We didn’t go out yesterday.Biz kecha tashqariga chiqmadik.
He didn’t call last night.U kecha tunda qo‘ng‘iroq qilmadi.
They didn’t visit us last week.Ular o‘tgan hafta biznikiga kelmadi.
I didn’t see him two days ago.Men uni ikki kun oldin ko‘rmadim.

Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

She doesn’t go yesterday. ❌ → She didn’t go yesterday. ✅
He didn’t went. ❌ → He didn’t go. ✅
They didn’t played. ❌ → They didn’t play. ✅
I didn’t was tired. ❌ → I wasn’t tired. ✅

Mashq 1 — Didn’t qo‘ying

1. I ___ watch TV yesterday.

2. He ___ go to work.

3. We ___ eat breakfast early.

4. They ___ see the film.

5. Sara ___ study last night.

Javoblar:
1–5. didn’t.

Mashq 2 — Inkor shakliga o‘tkazing

1. She played tennis.

2. We went to the park.

3. Ali ate lunch.

4. They visited their friends.

Javoblar:
1. She didn’t play tennis.
2. We didn’t go to the park.
3. Ali didn’t eat lunch.
4. They didn’t visit their friends.
