4. Inkor: Was Not / Were Not

Biror narsa o‘tgan paytda bunday bo‘lmaganini aytish uchun was yoki were dan keyin not qo‘yamiz.

Subject + was / were + not + ...
I was not tired.Men charchagan emas edim.
He was not at school.U maktabda emas edi.
They were not ready.Ular tayyor emas edilar.

5. Savol: Was/Were gap boshiga chiqadi

To be bilan o‘tgan zamon savolini tuzishda was yoki were subject dan oldinga o‘tadi.

Was / Were + subject + ...?
Was she at home?U uyda edimi?
Were you tired?Siz charchaganmidingiz?
Were they friends?Ular do‘st edimi?

6. Qisqa javoblar

Was he sick?Yes, he was. / No, he wasn’t.
Were you at home?Yes, I was. / No, I wasn’t.
Were they late?Yes, they were. / No, they weren’t.
Savolda Were you...? deb sizdan so‘ralsa, o‘zingiz javob berasiz: Yes, I was. — Ha, men ... edim.

7. Was/Were bilan Did ishlatmaymiz

Was va were o‘zlari to be fe’lining o‘tgan zamon shakli. Savol yoki inkor yasash uchun ularga do/did qo‘shmaymiz.

Was he at home? ✅
Did he was at home? ❌
She wasn’t tired. ✅
She didn’t was tired. ❌

Mashq 1 — Was yoki Were?

1. I ___ at home yesterday.

2. The children ___ happy.

3. Ali ___ tired last night.

4. You ___ late.

5. The books ___ on the desk.

6. My mother ___ at work.

Javoblar:
1. was. 2. were. 3. was. 4. were. 5. were. 6. was.

Mashq 2 — Inkor shakliga o‘tkazing

1. She was at school.

2. We were tired.

3. I was busy yesterday.

Javoblar:
1. She was not at school. 2. We were not tired.
3. I was not busy yesterday.

Mashq 3 — Savol shakliga o‘tkazing

1. He was a student.

2. They were at the park.

3. You were ready.

Javoblar:
1. Was he a student? 2. Were they at the park? 3. Were you ready?
