55-DARS. PAST SIMPLE — SHORT ANSWERS

Did you...? · Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.

Dars lug‘ati

Bu darsda uchraydigan so‘zlar — oldin eshitib, keyin darsni o‘qing.

arrive — kelmoq
call — qo‘ng‘iroq qilmoq
eat — yemoq
go — borish
home — uy
time — vaqt
tired — charchagan
when — qachon
where — qayerda
work — ishlamoq
yesterday — kecha
Short answer — qisqa javob. Savoldagi butun gapni takrorlamasdan, “ha” yoki “yo‘q” deb kerakli yordamchi fe’l bilan javob beramiz.

1. Did bilan savolga qisqa javob

Past Simple savoli Did bilan boshlangan bo‘lsa, qisqa javobda ham did ishlatamiz.

Yes, + subject pronoun + did.

No, + subject pronoun + didn’t.
Did you work yesterday?Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.
Did she call?Yes, she did. / No, she didn’t.
Did they arrive on time?Yes, they did. / No, they didn’t.

2. Javobda subject pronounni tanlang

Javob kim haqida ekanini ko‘rsatuvchi olmoshni ishlatamiz. Ismni odatda olmoshga almashtiramiz.

Savoldagi egaQisqa javobdagi olmoshJavob namunasi
you (sizdan so‘raldi)I (o‘zim javob beraman)Yes, I did.
AliheNo, he didn’t.
SarasheYes, she did.
the dog / ititYes, it did.
weweNo, we didn’t.
theytheyYes, they did.
Do you...? deb so‘rashsa, siz o‘zingiz haqida I deb javob berasiz: Do you work? — Yes, I do.
O‘tgan zamonda: Did you work? — Yes, I did.

3. Qisqa va to‘liq javob

Qisqa ijobiy javobda o‘tgan zamon fe’lini takrorlamaymiz. To‘liq javobda esa asosiy fe’lni ham aytamiz.

Did you go home?Qisqa: Yes, I did.
Did you go home?To‘liq: Yes, I went home.
Did she eat?Qisqa: No, she didn’t.
Did she eat?To‘liq: No, she didn’t eat.
Did you see him? — Yes, I did. ✅
Yes, I saw. ❌ (qisqa javobda did ishlatamiz)
Did she go? — No, she didn’t. ✅
No, she didn’t went. ❌

4. Wh-question ga Yes/No bilan javob bermaymiz

What, where, when, why, who, how bilan boshlangan savol aniq ma’lumot so‘raydi. Shuning uchun savolga kerakli ma’lumotni aytamiz.

When did you arrive?I arrived at six. — Soat oltida yetib keldim.
Where did she go?She went home. — U uyga ketdi.
Savol Did you...? bo‘lsa Yes/No qisqa javob mos. Savol Where did you...? bo‘lsa, joyni aytish kerak.

5. Was / Were savollarining qisqa javobi

To be ning o‘tgan shakli bilan berilgan savolga was/were bilan javob beramiz, did bilan emas.

Were you at home?Yes, I was. / No, I wasn’t.
Was he tired?Yes, he was. / No, he wasn’t.

Mashq 1 — Qisqa javobni to‘ldiring

1. Did you study? (ha) — Yes, I ___.

2. Did Ali go? (yo‘q) — No, he ___.

3. Did they eat? (ha) — Yes, they ___.

4. Did Sara call? (yo‘q) — No, she ___.

5. Were you tired? (ha) — Yes, I ___.

Javoblar:
1. did. 2. didn’t. 3. did. 4. didn’t. 5. was.

Mashq 2 — Qisqa javobni tanlang

1. Did she work? (Yes, she did. / Yes, she was.)

2. Was he at home? (No, he didn’t. / No, he wasn’t.)

3. Did you see the film? (No, I didn’t. / No, I wasn’t.)

Javoblar:
1. Yes, she did. 2. No, he wasn’t. 3. No, I didn’t.
