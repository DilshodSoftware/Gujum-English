55-AMALIYOT. PAST SIMPLE — SHORT ANSWERS

Did savoliga did/didn’t · Was/Were savoliga was/were
Past Simple harakat savoliga: Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t. Was/Were savoliga esa shu fe’l bilan javob beramiz: Yes, I was. / No, I wasn’t. “Did you…?” savoliga o‘zingiz javob bersangiz I deysiz.

1-mashq — Did savoliga javob bering

1. Did you call your friend? (ha)

2. Did he play football? (yo‘q)

3. Did they come early? (ha)

4. Did Sara buy a new bag? (yo‘q)

5. Did we finish the work? (ha)

Javoblar: 1. Yes, I did. 2. No, he didn’t. 3. Yes, they did. 4. No, she didn’t. 5. Yes, we did.

2-mashq — Was/Were savoliga javob bering

1. Were you tired? (ha)

2. Was Ali at home? (yo‘q)

3. Were the children happy? (ha)

4. Was it cold yesterday? (yo‘q)

Javoblar: 1. Yes, I was. 2. No, he wasn’t. 3. Yes, they were. 4. No, it wasn’t.

3-mashq — Savolga mos yordamchini tanlang

1. Did you work? — Yes, I (did / was).

2. Were they late? — No, they (didn’t / weren’t).

3. Was she busy? — Yes, she (did / was).

4. Did he see you? — No, he (didn’t / wasn’t).

Javoblar: 1. did; 2. weren’t; 3. was; 4. didn’t.

4-mashq — Ega olmoshini moslang

1. Did you…? (men javob beraman) → Yes, ___ did.

2. Did Anna…? → No, ___ didn’t.

3. Did the boys…? → Yes, ___ did.

4. Was your father…? → Yes, ___ was.

Javoblar: 1. I; 2. she; 3. they; 4. he.

5-mashq — To‘liq javobni qisqartiring

1. Did you watch the film? — Yes, I watched the film.

2. Did she go to work? — No, she didn’t go to work.

3. Were they at school? — Yes, they were at school.

Javoblar: 1. Yes, I did. 2. No, she didn’t. 3. Yes, they were.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. Did you see Ali? — Yes, I was.

2. Were you tired? — No, I didn’t.

3. Did he call? — Yes, he do.

4. Was she at work? — No, she weren’t.

Javoblar: 1. Yes, I did. 2. No, I wasn’t. 3. Yes, he did. 4. No, she wasn’t.
