54-DARS. PAST SIMPLE — QUESTIONS

O‘tgan zamonda savol tuzish · Did ...?

Dars lug‘ati

Bu darsda uchraydigan so‘zlar — oldin eshitib, keyin darsni o‘qing.

did — qildi
arrive — kelmoq
call — qo‘ng‘iroq qilmoq
eat — yemoq
go — borish
home — uy
leave — tark etmoq
play — o‘ynamoq
played — o‘ynadi
sara — Sara
tennis — tennis
tired — charchagan
went — bordi
work — ishlamoq
worked — ishladi
yesterday — kecha
Past Simple savoli o‘tmishda nima bo‘lganini yoki kim nima qilganini so‘raydi. Oddiy harakat fe’llari bilan savol tuzishda did yordam beradi.

1. Ha yoki yo‘q bilan javob beriladigan savol

Past Simple savolida did gap boshiga keladi. Subject undan keyin, fe’l esa oddiy shaklda turadi.

Did + subject + fe’lning oddiy shakli?
You worked yesterday.Siz kecha ishladingiz. (darak)
Did you work yesterday?Siz kecha ishladingizmi? (savol)

Did barcha egalar bilan bir xil: I, you, he, she, it, we, they va ismlar.

Did I call you?Men sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qildimmi?
Did he call you?U sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qildimi?
Did they call you?Ular sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qildimi?
Did Sara call you?Sara sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qildimi?

2. Savolda fe’lning oddiy shakli

Darak gapda fe’l o‘tgan zamon shaklida bo‘ladi: played, went, saw. Lekin savolda did o‘tgan zamonni bildirib turadi. Asosiy fe’l oddiy shaklga qaytadi.

Darak: She played tennis.U tennis o‘ynadi.
Savol: Did she play tennis?U tennis o‘ynadimi?
Darak: They went home.Ular uyga ketdi.
Savol: Did they go home?Ular uyga ketdimi?
Did he see the film? ✅
Did he saw the film? ❌
Did they visit you? ✅
Did they visited you? ❌

3. Wh-question: What, Where, When bilan

Savolga vaqt, joy yoki narsa haqida qo‘shimcha ma’lumot kerak bo‘lsa, savol so‘zini did dan oldinga qo‘yamiz.

Question word + did + subject + base verb?
Where did you go?Siz qayerga bordingiz?
What did she eat?U nima yedi?
When did they arrive?Ular qachon yetib keldi?
Why did he leave?U nima uchun ketdi?

4. Past of To Be savollari boshqacha

Agar gapda was/were bo‘lsa, did ishlatmaymiz. Was yoki were gap boshiga chiqadi.

Was she tired?U charchagan edimi?
Were they at home?Ular uyda edimi?
Did you work? ✅ — oddiy fe’l work.
Were you tired? ✅ — to be ning o‘tgan shakli.
Did you were tired? ❌

5. Savol belgisi

Yozma savol oxiriga ? qo‘yiladi. Ingliz tilida savol tartibi o‘zgaradi, shuning uchun faqat ovozni ko‘tarish yetarli emas.

You went to school yesterday? ❌ (bu mavzuda savolni bunday tuzmang)
Did you go to school yesterday? ✅

Mashq 1 — Darak gapni savolga aylantiring

1. You watched TV.

2. He went to work.

3. They ate breakfast.

4. Anna wrote a letter.

Javoblar:
1. Did you watch TV? 2. Did he go to work?
3. Did they eat breakfast? 4. Did Anna write a letter?

Mashq 2 — Did yoki Was/Were?

1. ___ you see the film? (Did / Were)

2. ___ she at home? (Did / Was)

3. ___ they play tennis? (Did / Were)

4. ___ Ali tired? (Did / Was)

Javoblar:
1. Did. 2. Was. 3. Did. 4. Was.
