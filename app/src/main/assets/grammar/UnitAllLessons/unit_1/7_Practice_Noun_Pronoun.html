7-AMALIYOT. NOUN AND PRONOUN

Otlarni tanish · Takrorlangan otlarni olmosh bilan almashtirish
Eslatma: noun — odam, hayvon, joy yoki narsaning nomi. Pronoun — shu nomni qayta aytmaslik uchun uning o‘rnida ishlatiladigan so‘z.

1-mashq — Nounmi yoki Pronounmi?

Har bir so‘zni N (noun/ot) yoki P (pronoun/olmosh) deb belgilang.

1. teacher   2. she   3. Tashkent

4. they   5. book   6. we

7. cat   8. I   9. school

Javoblar:
1. N. 2. P. 3. N. 4. P. 5. N. 6. P. 7. N. 8. P. 9. N.

2-mashq — Subject pronounni ismga moslang

1. Ali → (he / she)

2. Madina → (he / she)

3. a book → (it / they)

4. Ali and I → (we / they)

5. Ali and Sam → (we / they)

6. you and your friends → (you / we)

Javoblar:
1. he. 2. she. 3. it. 4. we. 5. they. 6. you.

3-mashq — Takrorlangan ismni almashtiring

Ikkinchi gapda ismni qayta yozmay, mos subject pronounni ishlating.

1. Ali is a student. Ali is happy.

2. Sara is at home. Sara is tired.

3. The book is new. The book is red.

4. Ali and Madina are friends. Ali and Madina are students.

5. My sister and I are here. My sister and I are ready.

Javoblar:
1. Ali is a student. He is happy.
2. Sara is at home. She is tired.
3. The book is new. It is red.
4. Ali and Madina are friends. They are students.
5. My sister and I are here. We are ready.

4-mashq — Gapdagi subjectni aniqlang

Gapning egasi kim yoki nima? Subjectni ko‘chirib yozing.

1. She is a teacher.

2. The dog is small.

3. They are at school.

4. My brother and I are friends.

Javoblar:
1. She. 2. The dog. 3. They. 4. My brother and I.

5-mashq — Atoqli otlarni katta harf bilan yozing

To‘g‘ri yozing: ali, madina, tashkent, uzbekistan

Javob: Ali, Madina, Tashkent, Uzbekistan.

6-mashq — To‘g‘ri yoki noto‘g‘ri?

1. book — noun.

2. they — pronoun.

3. Ali and I o‘rniga they ishlatamiz.

4. I har doim katta harf bilan yoziladi.

Javoblar:
1. To‘g‘ri. 2. To‘g‘ri. 3. Noto‘g‘ri — we. 4. To‘g‘ri.
