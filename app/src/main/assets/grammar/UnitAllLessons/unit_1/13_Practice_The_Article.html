13-AMALIYOT. THE — ANIQ ARTIKL

A / An / The / artiklsiz · ma’lum va umumiy narsalar
Ma’no farqi: a/an — yangi yoki hozircha noma’lum bitta sanaladigan narsa; the — qaysi narsa ekanini bilamiz; artiklsiz ko‘plik yoki sanalmaydigan ot ko‘pincha umumiy ma’noni bildiradi.

1-mashq — A, An, The yoki —?

Gap mazmuniga qarab artikl yozing. Umumiy ma’noda kerak bo‘lmasa, — belgilang.

1. I see ___ cat. ___ cat is black.

2. Please open ___ door. (shu xonadagi aniq eshik)

3. ___ dogs are friendly. (umuman itlar)

4. ___ water in this glass is cold.

5. Ali is ___ student.

6. ___ Tashkent is a big city.

7. She has ___ orange. ___ orange is sweet.

8. I like ___ music. (umuman musiqa)

Javoblar:
1. a, the. 2. the. 3. —. 4. the. 5. a. 6. —.
7. an, the. 8. —.

2-mashq — Hikoyadagi artikllarni to‘ldiring

There is ___ book on the table. ___ book is red. Next to it, I see ___ apple. ___ apple is green. ___ books on the shelf are old.

Javoblar: There is a book on the table. The book is red. Next to it, I see an apple. The apple is green. The books on the shelf are old.

3-mashq — Umumiy fikrmi yoki aniq narsa?

Har bir gapda ot umumiy ma’nodami yoki aniq narsa haqida ekanini belgilang.

1. I like books.

2. I like the books on this table.

3. Milk is good for children.

4. The milk in my cup is warm.

5. Birds can fly.

6. The birds in that tree are noisy.

Javoblar: 1. umumiy; 2. aniq; 3. umumiy; 4. aniq; 5. umumiy; 6. aniq.

4-mashq — To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang

1. I have (a / the) bag. (tinglovchi hali bilmaydi)

2. (A / The) bag is beside the chair. (oldingi gapdagi sumka)

3. She likes (the / —) cats. (umuman mushuklarni)

4. Where is (a / the) teacher? (ikkalamiz bilgan o‘qituvchi)

5. He lives in (the / —) Uzbekistan.

6. We can see (a / the) moon tonight. (yagona, vaziyatda ma’lum)

Javoblar: 1. a; 2. The; 3. —; 4. the; 5. —; 6. the.

5-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. I have the cat. (birinchi marta aytyapman, qaysi mushuk noma’lum)

2. A cat is black. (muayyan, yuqorida aytilgan mushuk)

3. I like the water. (umuman suv haqida)

4. The Ali is my brother.

5. She is teacher.

Javoblar:
1. I have a cat. 2. The cat is black.
3. I like water. 4. Ali is my brother.
5. She is a teacher.

6-mashq — Qisqa voqeani davom ettiring

Rasm yo‘q: tasavvur qiling, stol ustida bitta yangi koptok va yonida ma’lum kitob bor.

1. I see ___ ball. (birinchi marta)

2. ___ ball is blue. (endi ma’lum)

3. ___ book beside the ball is mine. (qaysi kitob ekani ko‘rsatilgan)

4. I like ___ balls. (umuman koptoklarni)

Javoblar: 1. a; 2. The; 3. The; 4. —.

7-mashq — O‘zingizdan misol

1. A/an bilan bir narsani tanishtiradigan 2 ta gap yozing.

2. Keyingi gapda shu narsa haqida the bilan davom eting.

3. Umumiy ma’nodagi ko‘plik ot bilan bitta gap yozing.

Namunaviy javoblar:
I see a bird. The bird is yellow. I like birds.
