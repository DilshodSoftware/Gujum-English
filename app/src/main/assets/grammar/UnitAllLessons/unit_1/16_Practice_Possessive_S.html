16-AMALIYOT. POSSESSIVE ’S

Kimning? · Ega + apostrof + s + narsa
Qoidani eslang: bitta egaga ’s qo‘shamiz: Ali’s book. -s bilan tugagan ko‘plik egada faqat apostrof qo‘shiladi: students’ room. Children kabi -s bilan tugamaydigan ko‘plikda ’s ishlatiladi: children’s toys.

1-mashq — Egalik birikmasini tuzing

Qavsdagi egaga tegishlilikni inglizcha ’s bilan yozing.

1. the book / Ali

2. the bag / the girl

3. the car / my father

4. the tail / the cat

5. the room / the teacher

Javoblar: 1. Ali’s book; 2. the girl’s bag; 3. my father’s car; 4. the cat’s tail; 5. the teacher’s room.

2-mashq — ’s yoki faqat apostrof?

Egani ko‘plik/birlik ekanini ko‘rib, egalik shaklini to‘ldiring.

1. the boy___ bicycle (bitta bola)

2. the boys___ bicycles (bir nechta o‘g‘il bola)

3. the students___ books (students allaqachon -s bilan tugaydi)

4. the children___ toys (children -s bilan tugamaydi)

5. the woman___ hat (bitta ayol)

6. the women___ bags (bir nechta ayol)

Javoblar: 1. boy’s; 2. boys’; 3. students’; 4. children’s; 5. woman’s; 6. women’s.

3-mashq — Ma’no juftini toping

1. the boy’s book   2. the boys’ book

A. bir nechta bolaning kitobi   B. bitta bolaning kitobi

3. the teacher’s room   4. the teachers’ room

C. bir nechta o‘qituvchining xonasi   D. bitta o‘qituvchining xonasi

Javoblar: 1–B; 2–A; 3–D; 4–C.

4-mashq — So‘zlarni to‘g‘ri tartiblang

1. phone / Sara / the

2. toys / children / the

3. house / my parents / the

4. classroom / students / the

Javoblar:
1. Sara’s phone. 2. the children’s toys.
3. my parents’ house. 4. the students’ classroom.

5-mashq — Inglizchaga tarjima qiling

1. Alining ruchkasi

2. qizning velosipedi

3. o‘quvchilarning kitoblari

4. bolalarning o‘yinchoqlari

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. Ali’s pen. 2. the girl’s bicycle.
3. the students’ books. 4. the children’s toys.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. This is the girls bag. (bitta qiz)

2. My parents’s house is big.

3. The childrens’ toys are here.

4. Put the apostrophe after the thing: the bag’s Sara.

Javoblar:
1. This is the girl’s bag. 2. My parents’ house is big.
3. The children’s toys are here. 4. Sara’s bag.

7-mashq — Atrofingizdan misol yozing

1. Bir kishiga tegishli narsa: ______

2. Ko‘plikdagi egalar tegishli narsa: ______

Namunaviy javoblar: my brother’s phone; my friends’ classroom.
