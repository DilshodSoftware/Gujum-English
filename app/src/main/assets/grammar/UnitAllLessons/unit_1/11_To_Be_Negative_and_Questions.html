11-DARS. TO BE — INKOR VA SO‘ROQ

am / is / are bilan “emas” deyish va savol berish
Oldingi dars: to be ning darak shakllari — I am, you are, he/she/it is, we/they are. Endi shu fe’l yordamida inkor va savol tuzamiz.

1. Inkor gap nima?

Inkor gap biror narsa yoki holat emasligini bildiradi. Ingliz tilida to be dan keyin not qo‘yamiz.

Subject + am / is / are + not + ...
I am not tired.Men charchagan emasman.
She is not at home.U uyda emas.
They are not students.Ular o‘quvchi emas.

2. Not qayerga qo‘yiladi?

Not doimo am/is/are dan keyin keladi. Gapning egasini almashtirmaymiz.

He is happy.U xursand.
He is not happy.U xursand emas.
She is not a teacher. ✅
She not is a teacher. ❌
She is a not teacher. ❌

3. Am / Is / Are bilan not

EgaInkor shakliMisol
Iam notI am not sad.
youare notYou are not late.
heis notHe is not a doctor.
sheis notShe is not tired.
itis notIt is not cold.
weare notWe are not at school.
theyare notThey are not friends.
I → am not   he / she / it → is not   you / we / they → are not

4. Qisqartma shakllar

Og‘zaki nutqda va kundalik yozuvda ayrim inkor shakllari qisqartiriladi. To‘liq va qisqa shaklning ma’nosi bir xil.

To‘liqQisqaMisol
is notisn’tHe isn’t ready.
are notaren’tThey aren’t here.
I am notI’m notI’m not tired.
Oddiy ingliz tilida amn’t shaklini ishlatmang. I am not yoki I’m not deng.

5. So‘roq gap qanday tuziladi?

Ha/yo‘q deb javob beriladigan savolda am/is/are gap boshiga o‘tadi. Ega esa undan keyin keladi.

Am / Is / Are + subject + ... ?
You are ready.Darak: Sen tayyorsan.
Are you ready?Savol: Sen tayyormisan?

Savol yozganda gap oxiriga so‘roq belgisi ? qo‘yamiz.

6. Barcha olmoshlar bilan savollar

Am I late?Men kech qoldimmi?
Are you a student?Sen / siz o‘quvchimisan / o‘quvchimisiz?
Is he your brother?U sening akangmi / ukangmi?
Is she at home?U uydami?
Is it new?U yangimi?
Are we ready?Biz tayyormizmi?
Are they friends?Ular do‘stmi?

7. Savolga qisqa javob berish

Qisqa javobda savoldagi to be ni takrorlaymiz. Yes — ha, No — yo‘q.

Are you a student?Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.
Is he at home?Yes, he is. / No, he isn’t.
Is she happy?Yes, she is. / No, she isn’t.
Are they ready?Yes, they are. / No, they aren’t.
Are you…? deb so‘ralganda, o‘zingiz haqingizda javob berasiz: Yes, I am (“Ha, men ...man”), No, I’m not (“Yo‘q, men ... emasman”).

8. Darak, inkor va savolni taqqoslang

ShaklMisolMa’nosi
DarakShe is a teacher.U o‘qituvchi.
InkorShe is not a teacher.U o‘qituvchi emas.
SavolIs she a teacher?U o‘qituvchimi?
Darak: They are friends. ✅
Inkor: They are not friends. ✅
Savol: Are they friends? ✅

9. Ism bilan ham shu qoida

Ism yoki bir nechta ism ham ega bo‘la oladi. Bitta ism bilan ko‘pincha is, bir nechta ism bilan are ishlatamiz.

Ali is a student.Ali o‘quvchi.
Ali is not a teacher.Ali o‘qituvchi emas.
Is Ali at school?Ali maktabdami?
Ali and Sara are friends.Ali va Sara do‘stlar.
Are Ali and Sara at home?Ali va Sara uydami?

10. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

She not is happy. ❌ → She is not happy. ✅
You is ready? ❌ → Are you ready? ✅
Is they students? ❌ → Are they students? ✅
Do you are a student? ❌ → Are you a student? ✅
No, I am. (savolga “yo‘q” javobi) ❌ → No, I’m not. ✅

Mashq 1 — Inkor shakliga o‘tkazing

1. I am tired.

2. He is a doctor.

3. We are at school.

4. The cat is black.

5. They are friends.

Javoblar:
1. I am not tired. 2. He is not a doctor.
3. We are not at school. 4. The cat is not black. 5. They are not friends.

Mashq 2 — Savol shakliga o‘tkazing

1. You are ready.

2. She is a student.

3. It is cold.

4. They are at home.

Javoblar:
1. Are you ready? 2. Is she a student?
3. Is it cold? 4. Are they at home?

Mashq 3 — Qisqa javobni to‘ldiring

1. Are you tired? Yes, I ___.

2. Is he a teacher? No, he ___.

3. Are they friends? Yes, they ___.

4. Is she at school? No, she ___.

Javoblar:
1. am. 2. isn’t / is not. 3. are. 4. isn’t / is not.
