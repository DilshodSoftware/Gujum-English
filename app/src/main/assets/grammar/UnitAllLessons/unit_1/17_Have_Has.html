17-DARS. HAVE / HAS

Have / Has · “Bor” yoki “ega bo‘lmoq”

Have va has biror odamda yoki narsada nima borligini aytadi. O‘zbekchaga ko‘pincha “bor” deb tarjima qilinadi.

I have a book.Menda kitob bor.
She has a cat.Unda mushuk bor.

Have/has faqat buyumlar uchun emas. Oila a’zosi, uy hayvoni yoki boshqa narsaga ega ekanimizni aytishda ham ishlatamiz.

We have a small house.Bizning kichkina uyimiz bor.
He has two sisters.Uning ikkita opasi/singlisi bor.

1. Have yoki Has?

Qaysi so‘zni ishlatishimiz gapning egasiga bog‘liq.

EgaFe’lMisol
IhaveI have a pen.
YouhaveYou have a pen.
WehaveWe have a pen.
TheyhaveThey have a pen.
HehasHe has a pen.
ShehasShe has a pen.
IthasIt has a tail.
I / you / we / they + have

he / she / it + has

You bitta odamga yoki bir nechta odamga murojaat qilsa ham, undan keyin have ishlatiladi.

2. He / She / It bilan Has

He, she, it yoki ularning o‘rnidagi bitta ism bilan has ishlatiladi.

He has a brother.Uning akasi/ukasi bor.
She has two sisters.Uning ikkita opasi/singlisi bor.
The dog has a long tail.Itning dumi uzun.
Ali has a new bag.Alida yangi sumka bor.
Ali — he kabi bitta odam. Shuning uchun Ali has deymiz.

3. Ko‘plikdagi ega bilan Have

Bir nechta odam yoki narsa haqida gapirganda have ishlatiladi.

Ali and Sam have a ball.Ali va Samda koptok bor.
My parents have a car.Ota-onamda mashina bor.
The students have books.O‘quvchilarda kitoblar bor.

4. Inkor gap: Don’t have / Doesn’t have

“... yo‘q” demoqchi bo‘lsak, don’t yoki doesn’t ishlatamiz.

I / you / we / they + don’t have

he / she / it + doesn’t have
I don’t have a car.Menda mashina yo‘q.
They don’t have a dog.Ularda it yo‘q.
He doesn’t have a bike.Unda velosiped yo‘q.
She doesn’t have a phone.Unda telefon yo‘q.
He don’t have a car. ❌
He doesn’t have a car. ✅

5. Doesn’t dan keyin Have ishlatamiz

Doesn’t ichida has uchun kerakli -s bor. Shuning uchun undan keyin fe’lning oddiy shakli — have keladi.

She doesn’t have a cat. ✅
She doesn’t has a cat. ❌

He doesn’t have a pen. ✅
He doesn’t has a pen. ❌

6. Savol: Do / Does ... have?

Have / has bilan savol tuzishda do yoki does gap boshiga qo‘yiladi.

Do + I / you / we / they + have ...?

Does + he / she / it + have ...?
Do you have a pen?Sizda ruchka bormi?
Do they have a car?Ularda mashina bormi?
Does he have a brother?Uning akasi/ukasi bormi?
Does Sara have a cat?Sarada mushuk bormi?
Does she have a book? ✅
Does she has a book? ❌
Does dan keyin ham have ishlatiladi.

7. Qisqa javoblar

Do / Does bilan berilgan savollarga ham do / does bilan qisqa javob beramiz. Yes — ha, No — yo‘q.

Do you have a sister?Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.
Does he have a bike?Yes, he does. / No, he doesn’t.
Do they have a car?Yes, they do. / No, they don’t.
Javobda egani to‘g‘ri tanlang: Do you...? deb so‘rashsa va o‘zingiz javob bersangiz, Yes, I do deysiz.

Mashq 1 — Have yoki Has?

1. I ___ a blue pen.

2. She ___ a little brother.

3. They ___ a big house.

4. The cat ___ green eyes.

5. You ___ a nice bag.

6. Ali and Sara ___ two books.

Javoblar:
1. have   2. has   3. have
4. has   5. have   6. have

Mashq 2 — Inkor shakliga o‘tkazing

1. I have a car.

2. He has a dog.

3. We have a garden.

4. My sister has a bike.

Javoblar:
1. I don’t have a car.
2. He doesn’t have a dog.
3. We don’t have a garden.
4. My sister doesn’t have a bike.

Mashq 3 — Do yoki Does?

1. ___ you have a pencil?

2. ___ she have a sister?

3. ___ they have a teacher?

4. ___ Tom have a computer?

Javoblar:
1. Do   2. Does   3. Do   4. Does

Mashq 4 — Qisqa javob yozing

1. Do you have a pen? (ha)

2. Does Ali have a dog? (yo‘q)

3. Do they have a car? (ha)

4. Does she have a sister? (yo‘q)

Javoblar:
1. Yes, I do. 2. No, he doesn’t.
3. Yes, they do. 4. No, she doesn’t.
