12-AMALIYOT. A / AN

Noaniq artikl · birlikdagi sanaladigan ot · birinchi tovush
Tezkor qoida: bitta sanaladigan ot oldidan artikl kerak. Birinchi talaffuz tovushi undosh bo‘lsa a, unli bo‘lsa an. Ko‘plik va umumiy sanalmaydigan ot oldidan a/an qo‘yilmaydi.

1-mashq — A yoki An?

So‘zning yozilishiga emas, birinchi eshitiladigan tovushiga qarab tanlang.

1. ___ banana

2. ___ orange

3. ___ egg

4. ___ teacher

5. ___ umbrella

6. ___ house

7. ___ hour (h aytilmaydi)

8. ___ university (“yu” tovushi)

Javoblar: 1. a; 2. an; 3. an; 4. a; 5. an; 6. a; 7. an; 8. a.

2-mashq — Sifatli birikmada artiklni tanlang

Artikl bevosita otdan emas, undan keyin kelayotgan birinchi so‘zning tovushidan tanlanadi.

1. ___ old car

2. ___ red apple

3. ___ interesting book

4. ___ small elephant

5. ___ beautiful flower

Javoblar: 1. an old car; 2. a red apple; 3. an interesting book; 4. a small elephant; 5. a beautiful flower.

3-mashq — A/An yoki artiklsiz qoldiring

Bo‘sh joyga a, an yoki — (hech narsa) yozing.

1. I have ___ pencil.

2. She likes ___ cats. (umuman mushuklarni)

3. We drink ___ water.

4. He is ___ actor.

5. They are ___ students. (ko‘plik)

6. This is ___ apple.

7. ___ milk is in the fridge. (umumiy sut)

8. I see ___ bird.

Javoblar: 1. a; 2. —; 3. —; 4. an; 5. —; 6. an; 7. —; 8. a.

4-mashq — Birinchi marta yoki yana eslatish

Hikoyani o‘qing. Birinchi tanishtirishda a/an, keyingi gapda aniq bo‘lgan narsada the ishlating.

I see ___ dog. ___ dog is brown.

She has ___ umbrella. ___ umbrella is blue.

We find ___ old key. ___ key is under the book.

Javoblar:
I see a dog. The dog is brown.
She has an umbrella. The umbrella is blue.
We find an old key. The key is under the book.

5-mashq — Nega shu artikl?

1. an hour — nega an?

2. a university — nega a?

3. an apple — birinchi tovush unlimi yoki undoshmi?

4. a books to‘g‘rimi? Nega?

Javoblar:
1. h aytilmaydi, so‘z unli tovush bilan boshlanadi. 2. “University” “yu” undosh tovushi bilan boshlanadi. 3. Unli. 4. Noto‘g‘ri: books ko‘plik, a faqat birlikdagi ot bilan ishlatiladi.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. She has an car.

2. He is a engineer.

3. I need a water.

4. There are an apples.

5. It is an small room.

Javoblar:
1. She has a car. 2. He is an engineer.
3. I need water. 4. There are apples.
5. It is a small room.

7-mashq — O‘zingiz misol tuzing

1. a bilan boshlanadigan 2 ta ot birikmasi yozing.

2. an bilan boshlanadigan 2 ta ot birikmasi yozing.

3. Bitta sanalmaydigan otni artiklsiz gapda ishlating.

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. a book, a green bag. 2. an egg, an old house. 3. I drink milk.
