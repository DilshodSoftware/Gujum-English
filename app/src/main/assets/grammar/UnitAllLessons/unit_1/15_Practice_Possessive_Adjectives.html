15-AMALIYOT. POSSESSIVE ADJECTIVES

My / Your / His / Her / Its / Our / Their · Kimning narsasi?
Asosiy qolip: egalik bildiruvchi so‘zdan keyin ot keladi: my book, her bag, their house. Shakl narsaning soniga emas, egasiga qarab tanlanadi.

1-mashq — Egasiga mos so‘zni tanlang

1. I am Lola. This is (my / her) desk.

2. Tom has a bike. (His / Her) bike is blue.

3. Anna has a cat. (His / Her) cat is small.

4. We live here. This is (our / their) house.

5. The boys have books. (Their / His) books are new.

6. You have a nice bag. Is this (your / our) bag?

Javoblar: 1. my; 2. His; 3. Her; 4. our; 5. Their; 6. your.

2-mashq — Subject pronoundan egalik so‘zini toping

Har bir olmoshga mos possessive adjective yozing.

1. I → ___   2. you → ___   3. he → ___   4. she → ___

5. it → ___   6. we → ___   7. they → ___

Javoblar: 1. my; 2. your; 3. his; 4. her; 5. its; 6. our; 7. their.

3-mashq — Qisqa voqeani to‘ldiring

So‘zlar: my, his, her, its, our, their.

1. I am Ben. ___ family is small.

2. My sister is Mia. ___ room is pink.

3. My brother is Leo. ___ room is blue.

4. We have a dog. ___ name is Max.

5. We love ___ home.

6. My parents have a car. ___ car is white.

Javoblar: 1. My; 2. Her; 3. His; 4. Its; 5. our; 6. Their.

4-mashq — Its yoki It’s?

Its egalikni, it’s esa it is ni bildiradi.

1. The cat is eating ___ food.

2. ___ a very small kitten. (It is)

3. The tree is old. ___ leaves are green.

4. ___ my new pencil. (It is)

Javoblar: 1. its; 2. It’s; 3. Its; 4. It’s.

5-mashq — Inglizchaga tarjima qiling

1. Mening ismim Sara.

2. Uning (erkak) telefoni yangi.

3. Bizning o‘qituvchimiz mehribon.

4. Ularning uyi katta.

5. Itning dumi uzun.

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. My name is Sara. 2. His phone is new.
3. Our teacher is kind. 4. Their house is big. 5. Its tail is long.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. Me book is here.

2. Tom is a boy. Her bag is black.

3. The dog has a ball. It’s ball is red. (egalik)

4. They classroom is large.

5. She name is Amina.

Javoblar:
1. My book is here. 2. His bag is black.
3. Its ball is red. 4. Their classroom is large.
5. Her name is Amina.

7-mashq — O‘zingiz haqingizda yozing

Quyidagi boshlanishlardan foydalanib 3 ta gap tuzing: My name… / My family… / Our home…

Namunaviy javob: My name is Ali. My family is kind. Our home is small.
