15-DARS. POSSESSIVE ADJECTIVES

My / Your / His / Her / Its / Our / Their · Egalik bildiruvchi so‘zlar

Biror narsa kimga tegishli ekanini aytish uchun possessive adjectives ishlatiladi. O‘zbek tilidagi “mening”, “sening”, “uning”, “bizning”, “ularning” kabi ma’nolarni bildiradi.

Possessive adjective + ot
my book — mening kitobim
her bag — uning sumkasi
our house — bizning uyimiz

2. My — mening

I olmoshiga mos egalik so‘zi — my.

My name is Ali.Mening ismim Ali.
This is my book.Bu mening kitobim.
My friends are here.Do‘stlarim shu yerda.

3. Your — sening / sizning

You bitta odamga ham, bir nechta odamga ham murojaat qilishi mumkin. Ikkala holatda ham your ishlatiladi.

What is your name?Ismingiz nima?
Your bag is nice.Sumkangiz chiroyli.
You and your friends are here.Siz va do‘stlaringiz shu yerdasiz.

4. His va Her — uning

His — erkak kishi yoki o‘g‘il bolaga tegishli narsa. Her — ayol kishi yoki qiz bolaga tegishli narsa.

He is Tom. His car is red.U Tom. Uning mashinasi qizil.
She is Anna. Her bike is new.U Anna. Uning velosipedi yangi.
His sister is a student.Uning (o‘g‘il bolaning) singlisi o‘quvchi.
Her brother is at home.Uning (qiz bolaning) akasi uyda.
Egalik so‘zi narsaning egasiga qarab tanlanadi: Tomning sumkasi — his bag; Annaning sumkasi — her bag.
Tom has a sister. His sister is kind.Tomning singlisi bor. Uning singlisi mehribon.
Anna has a brother. Her brother is kind.Annaning akasi/ukasi bor. Uning akasi/ukasi mehribon.

Bu yerda his/her narsaning turiga emas, egasiga qarab tanlangan: singil ham, aka ham har xil jinsdagi odam bo‘lishi mumkin, lekin possessive adjective baribir egaga moslanadi.

5. Its — uning (hayvon yoki narsa)

Its hayvon yoki narsaga tegishli narsani bildiradi.

The dog is eating its food.U ovqatini yeyapti.
The tree is big. Its leaves are green.Daraxt katta. Uning barglari yashil.
The cat has a ball. Its ball is blue.Mushukning koptogi bor. Uning koptogi ko‘k.
Its — egalik bildiradi.
It’s — it is ning qisqa shakli.
The dog likes its food. ✅
It’s a dog. = It is a dog. ✅

6. Our va Their

Our — bizning; their — ularning.

Our teacher is kind.Bizning o‘qituvchimiz mehribon.
Our house is small.Bizning uyimiz kichkina.
They are students. Their books are new.Ular o‘quvchilar. Ularning kitoblari yangi.
Their mother is a doctor.Ularning onasi shifokor.

7. Egalik so‘zi ot oldidan keladi

Egalik bildiruvchi so‘zdan keyin odatda ot keladi. U otning oldida turadi.

my / your / his / her / its / our / their + noun
my pencilmening qalamim
your pencilssening qalamlaring
their houseularning uyi
their housesularning uylari

Egalik so‘zi otning birlik yoki ko‘plik ekaniga qarab o‘zgarmaydi: my book, my books.

8. A / An egalik so‘zi bilan ishlatilmaydi

My, your, his kabi so‘zlardan oldin a/an qo‘ymaymiz.

a my book ❌ → my book ✅
the her bag ❌ → her bag ✅
an our teacher ❌ → our teacher ✅

9. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

He is my friend. Her name is Tom. ❌
He is my friend. His name is Tom. ✅

She is my sister. His name is Sara. ❌
She is my sister. Her name is Sara. ✅

Its a cat. ❌
It’s a cat. (= It is a cat.) ✅

This is a my pen. ❌
This is my pen. ✅

Mashq 1 — Mos so‘zni tanlang

1. I am Lola. ___ name is Lola. (My / Her)

2. He is Ben. ___ bike is black. (His / Her)

3. We live here. This is ___ house. (our / their)

4. The bird is small. ___ wings are blue. (Its / Their)

5. You have a pencil. Is this ___ pencil? (your / his)

6. They are my friends. ___ names are Ali and Sam. (Our / Their)

Javoblar:
1. My   2. His   3. our
4. Its   5. your   6. Their

Mashq 2 — Egaga mos possessive adjective

Avval egani toping, keyin mos egalik so‘zini yozing.

1. I have a ruler. This is ___ ruler.

2. She has a lunch box. This is ___ lunch box.

3. The dog has a tail. ___ tail is long.

4. We have a classroom. This is ___ classroom.

5. They have a teacher. ___ teacher is kind.

Javoblar:
1. my. 2. her. 3. Its. 4. our. 5. Their.

Mashq 3 — Xatoni to‘g‘rilang

1. She is my friend. His name is Maya.

2. This is a our classroom.

3. The dog is eating it's food.

4. He is Sam. Her father is a teacher.

Javoblar:
1. She is my friend. Her name is Maya.
2. This is our classroom.
3. The dog is eating its food.
4. He is Sam. His father is a teacher.

Eng qisqa xulosa

I → my · you → your · he → his

she → her · it → its · we → our · they → their

Egalik bildiruvchi so‘z otning oldidan keladi: my book, their house.

Its = uning · It’s = it is.

a/an ni possessive adjective bilan birga ishlatmang: my pen.
