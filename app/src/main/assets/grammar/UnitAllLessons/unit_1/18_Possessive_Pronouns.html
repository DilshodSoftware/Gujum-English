18-DARS. POSSESSIVE PRONOUNS

Mine / Yours / His / Hers / Ours / Theirs · Egalik olmoshlari

Possessive pronouns biror narsa kimga tegishli ekanini bildiradi. Ular takrorlanmasligi uchun otning o‘rnida ishlatiladi.

This is my book.Bu mening kitobim.
This book is mine.Bu kitob meniki.
mine = my book · yours = your book

2. My va Mine

My dan keyin ot keladi. Mine esa otning o‘rnida, mustaqil ishlatiladi.

This is my pen.Bu mening ruchkam.
This pen is mine.Bu ruchka meniki.
My bag is black. Yours is blue.Mening sumkam qora. Sizniki ko‘k.
my + noun: my pen
mine: The pen is mine.

3. Your va Yours

Your dan keyin ot keladi. Yours “seniki / sizniki” deb, otning o‘rnida turadi.

Is this your phone?Bu sizning telefoningizmi?
Is this phone yours?Bu telefon siznikimi?
Your room is big. Ours is small.Sizning xonangiz katta. Bizniki kichik.

4. His va Hers

His erkak kishiga, hers ayol kishiga tegishli narsani bildiradi.

This is his coat.Bu uning (erkakning) paltosi.
This coat is his.Bu palto uniki (erkakniki).
That is her bag.Anavi uning (ayolning) sumkasi.
That bag is hers.Anavi sumka uniki (ayolniki).
his ikkala vazifada ham bir xil: his coat / The coat is his.
her → hers: her bag / The bag is hers.

5. Our / Ours va Their / Theirs

This is our classroom.Bu bizning sinfxonamiz.
This classroom is ours.Bu sinfxona bizniki.
Those are their books.Anavilar ularning kitoblari.
Those books are theirs.Anavi kitoblar ularniki.
our + noun → ours · their + noun → theirs

6. “Whose?” — “Kimniki?” deb so‘rash

Whose “kimning?” yoki “kimniki?” deb so‘rash uchun ishlatiladi. Javobda otni aytish mumkin, yoki otni takrorlamaslik uchun possessive pronoun qo‘yamiz.

Whose book is this?Bu kimning kitobi?
It is my book. → It is mine.Bu mening kitobim. → Bu meniki.
Whose bags are these?Bular kimning sumkalari?
They are her bags. → They are hers.Bular uning sumkalari. → Bular uniki.

7. Otni takrorlamaslik uchun

Bir narsaning kimga tegishli ekanini aytib bo‘lgan bo‘lsak, otni ikkinchi marta takrorlamaymiz. Uning o‘rniga egalik olmoshini qo‘yamiz.

This is my pencil. That is your pencil.(pencil takrorlandi)
This is my pencil. That is yours.Ikkinchi marta pencil kerak emas.

9. Apostrof qo‘shilmaydi

Mine, yours, hers, ours va theirs so‘zlarida apostrof yo‘q. Yours — your’s emas.

This seat is yours. ✅
This seat is your’s. ❌
The house is theirs. ✅
The house is their’s. ❌

10. “Its” haqida

Its odatda otdan oldin keladi: its name — uning nomi. Boshlang‘ich darajada “uniki” ma’nosida alohida ishlatiladigan its shaklini odatda ishlatmaymiz.

The dog has a ball. Its ball is red.
Itning koptogi bor. Uning koptogi qizil.

Mashq 1 — My yoki Mine?

1. This is ___ book. (my / mine)

2. This book is ___. (my / mine)

3. ___ bag is on the chair. (My / Mine)

4. The blue bag is ___. (my / mine)

Javoblar:
1. my   2. mine   3. My   4. mine

Mashq 2 — Mos egalik olmoshini yozing

1. This is her pencil. The pencil is ___.

2. This is our classroom. The classroom is ___.

3. Those are their books. The books are ___.

4. This is his coat. The coat is ___.

5. Is this your phone? Is this phone ___?

Javoblar:
1. hers   2. ours   3. theirs
4. his   5. yours

Mashq 3 — My yoki Mine kabi mos shaklni tanlang

1. The book is (my / mine).

2. Is this (your / yours) bag?

3. The red bicycle is (her / hers).

4. These seats are (our / ours).

5. The toys are (their / theirs).

Javoblar:
1. mine. 2. your. 3. hers. 4. ours. 5. theirs.

Eng qisqa xulosa

I → mine · you → yours · he → his

she → hers · we → ours · they → theirs

Egalik so‘zi ot bilan: my book. Egalik olmoshi otning o‘rnida: The book is mine.

Mine / yours / hers / ours / theirs apostrofsiz yoziladi.
