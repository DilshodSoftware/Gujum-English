11-AMALIYOT. TO BE — INKOR VA SAVOL

Am / Is / Are + not · Am / Is / Are gap boshida · qisqa javoblar
Qoidani eslang: inkor uchun am/is/are dan keyin not keladi. Ha/yo‘q savolida esa am/is/are egadan oldinga o‘tadi. Do/does ishlatmang.

1-mashq — Inkorni to‘ldiring

Not qo‘yib, gapni “emas” ma’nosiga o‘zgartiring.

1. I am cold. → I ___ cold.

2. He is a doctor. → He ___ a doctor.

3. You are late. → You ___ late.

4. The room is clean. → The room ___ clean.

5. We are at work. → We ___ at work.

6. They are hungry. → They ___ hungry.

Javoblar: 1. am not; 2. is not / isn’t; 3. are not / aren’t; 4. is not / isn’t; 5. are not / aren’t; 6. are not / aren’t.

2-mashq — Darak gapni savolga aylantiring

1. You are a student.

2. It is your bag.

3. Sam is at school.

4. The children are happy.

5. I am early.

Javoblar:
1. Are you a student? 2. Is it your bag? 3. Is Sam at school?
4. Are the children happy? 5. Am I early?

3-mashq — Qisqa javobni tanlang

Qavsdagi ma’noga mos, grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri qisqa javobni yozing.

1. Are you tired? (ha)

2. Is Anna a teacher? (yo‘q)

3. Are the boys at home? (ha)

4. Is it cold today? (yo‘q)

5. Are we ready? (ha)

Javoblar:
1. Yes, I am. 2. No, she isn’t. 3. Yes, they are.
4. No, it isn’t. 5. Yes, we are.

4-mashq — To‘g‘ri variantni belgilang

1. (Is / Are) your sister at home?

2. (Am / Are) I in the right room?

3. They (isn’t / aren’t) busy.

4. (Do / Are) you a new student?

5. My phone (isn’t / aren’t) old.

6. (Is / Are) Ali and Vali brothers?

Javoblar: 1. Is; 2. Am; 3. aren’t; 4. Are; 5. isn’t; 6. Are.

5-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. She not is my sister.

2. Is they at the park?

3. Do you are happy?

4. No, I am. (savolga “yo‘q” deb javob)

5. He aren’t at work.

Javoblar:
1. She is not my sister. 2. Are they at the park?
3. Are you happy? 4. No, I’m not. 5. He isn’t at work.

6-mashq — Mini-dialogni to‘ldiring

A: ___ you new here?

B: Yes, I ___.

A: ___ your friends here too?

B: No, they ___.

A: ___ your teacher kind?

B: Yes, she ___.

Javoblar: Are, am, Are, aren’t, Is, is.

7-mashq — Inglizchaga tarjima qiling

1. Ular band emas.

2. Siz tayyormisiz?

3. Ha, men tayyorman. (qisqa javob)

4. Akam shifokor emas.

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. They aren’t busy. 2. Are you ready?
3. Yes, I am. 4. My brother isn’t a doctor.
Tekshirish usuli: inkorda not fe’ldan keyinmi? Savolda to be egadan oldindami? Qisqa javobda savolga mos fe’l takrorlandimi?
