Oldingi dars: gapning egasi I, you, he, she, it, we yoki they bo‘lishi mumkin. Endi har bir egaga mos to be shaklini tanlaymiz.

1. To be nima uchun kerak?

To be ingliz tilidagi juda muhim fe’l. Odam yoki narsaning kimligini, nima ekanini, qandayligini yoki qayerdaligini aytishda ishlatiladi.

O‘zbek tilida “Men o‘quvchiman”, “U xursand”, “Kitob stol ustida” deyishimiz mumkin. Ingliz tilida bunday gaplarda ega bilan qolgan ma’lumot orasiga albatta am, is yoki are qo‘yiladi.

I am a student. ✅ — Men o‘quvchiman.
I a student. ❌ — inglizcha gapda fe’l yetishmayapti.

2. To be ning uch shakli

Hozirgi zamonda to be uch xil ko‘rinadi: am, is, are. Ularning ma’nosi gapga qarab “-man”, “-san”, “-dir”, “-miz”, “-lar” kabi tarjima qilinishi mumkin.

EgaTo beOddiy misol
IamI am happy.
youareYou are happy.
heisHe is happy.
sheisShe is happy.
itisIt is new.

theyareThey are happy.
I → am   he / she / it → is   you / we / they → are

3. I bilan doimo AM

I — “men”. I dan keyin doimo am ishlatamiz.

I am Ali.Men Aliman.
I am a student.Men o‘quvchiman.
I am happy.Men xursandman.
I am at home.Men uydaman.
I + am + ...
Hech qachon I is yoki I are demaymiz.

4. He, She va It bilan IS

He — erkak kishi, she — ayol kishi, it — narsa yoki hayvon. Uchalasidan keyin is keladi.

He is a teacher.U o‘qituvchi. (erkak)
She is a doctor.U shifokor. (ayol)
It is a cat.Bu mushuk.
The bag is black. It is black.Sumka qora. U qora.
he / she / it + is

5. You, We va They bilan ARE

You bir kishiga aytilganda ham are oladi. We — biz, they — ular; ikkalasi ham are bilan ishlatiladi.

You are my friend.Sen mening do‘stimsan / Siz mening do‘stimsiz.
You are students.Sizlar o‘quvchisizlar.
We are friends.Biz do‘stmiz.
They are at school.Ular maktabda.

6. Ism bilan to be ni tanlaymiz

Gapda olmosh emas, ism yoki ot kelsa, avval u bitta yoki ko‘p ekanini aniqlaymiz.

Ali is a student.Ali bitta odam: he → is.
Madina is happy.Madina bitta odam: she → is.
The phone is new.Bitta narsa: it → is.
Ali and Sam are friends.Ikki kishi: they → are.
Bitta odam yoki narsa: odatda is. Bir nechta: odatda are. I esa o‘ziga xos: doimo am.

7. To be bilan uch xil ma’lumot

Kim yoki nima ekanini aytamiz

I am a student.Men o‘quvchiman.
He is a doctor.U shifokor.

Birlikdagi kasb nomi oldidan ko‘pincha a yoki an keladi. Bu artikllarni 12-darsda o‘rganamiz.

Qandayligini aytamiz

She is kind.U mehribon.
They are tired.Ular charchagan.

Qayerda ekanini aytamiz

We are at home.Biz uydamiz.
The book is on the table.Kitob stol ustida.

9. To be oddiy harakat fe’li emas

Am/is/are ko‘pincha kimligini, qandayligini yoki qayerdaligini bildiradi. Oddiy harakatni esa alohida fe’l aytadi.

I am a student.Men o‘quvchiman. (kim?)
I work.Men ishlayman. (harakat)
She is happy.U xursand. (qanday?)
She reads.U o‘qiydi. (harakat)

10. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

I is a student. ❌ → I am a student. ✅
He are happy. ❌ → He is happy. ✅
They is here. ❌ → They are here. ✅
She teacher. ❌ → She is a teacher. ✅
I am student. ❌ → I am a student. ✅
