17-AMALIYOT. HAVE / HAS

Bor / ega bo‘lmoq · darak · inkor · savol · qisqa javob
Qolip: I/you/we/they have; he/she/it has. Inkor va savolda do/does ishlatilganda asosiy shakl have bo‘ladi: Does she have…?, She doesn’t have…

1-mashq — Have yoki Has?

1. My friends ___ a new teacher.

2. My sister ___ a red bicycle.

3. I ___ two brothers.

4. The house ___ three windows.

5. You ___ a good idea.

6. Ali and I ___ English books.

7. The baby ___ blue eyes.

8. Our parents ___ a small garden.

Javoblar: 1. have; 2. has; 3. have; 4. has; 5. have; 6. have; 7. has; 8. have.

2-mashq — Inkor shaklini yozing

1. I have a computer.

2. He has a sister.

3. They have a car.

4. The dog has a long tail.

5. We have a lot of time.

Javoblar:
1. I don’t have a computer. 2. He doesn’t have a sister.
3. They don’t have a car. 4. The dog doesn’t have a long tail.
5. We don’t have a lot of time.

3-mashq — Do yoki Does bilan savol tuzing

1. you / have / a pencil?

2. Sara / have / a phone?

3. your friends / have / bikes?

4. the cat / have / green eyes?

5. we / have / homework?

Javoblar:
1. Do you have a pencil? 2. Does Sara have a phone?
3. Do your friends have bikes? 4. Does the cat have green eyes?
5. Do we have homework?

4-mashq — Qisqa javoblar

Qavsdagi ha/yo‘q javobiga mos qisqa javob yozing.

1. Do you have a pet? (ha)

2. Does Tom have a sister? (yo‘q)

3. Do they have a garden? (ha)

4. Does your phone have a camera? (yo‘q)

Javoblar:
1. Yes, I do. 2. No, he doesn’t.
3. Yes, they do. 4. No, it doesn’t.

5-mashq — Aralash test

1. (Do / Does) your brother have a bike?

2. My sister (have / has) a blue bag.

3. They (don’t have / doesn’t have) a car.

4. Does Anna (have / has) a cat?

5. He doesn’t (have / has) a computer.

Javoblar: 1. Does; 2. has; 3. don’t have; 4. have; 5. have.

6-mashq — Dialogni yakunlang

A: ___ you have any brothers?

B: Yes, I ___. I have one brother.

A: ___ he have a bike?

B: No, he ___. But he ___ a skateboard.

Javoblar: Do, do, Does, doesn’t, has.

7-mashq — Xatolarni tuzating

1. Does she has a dog?

2. He don’t have a watch.

3. They doesn’t have books.

4. Does your parents have a car?

5. She doesn’t has a sister.

Javoblar:
1. Does she have a dog? 2. He doesn’t have a watch.
3. They don’t have books. 4. Do your parents have a car?
5. She doesn’t have a sister.
