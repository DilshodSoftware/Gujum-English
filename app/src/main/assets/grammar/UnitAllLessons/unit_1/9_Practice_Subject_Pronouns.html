9-AMALIYOT. SUBJECT PRONOUNS

I / You / He / She / It / We / They
Vazifa: ism yoki ot o‘rniga mos ega olmoshini tanlash. Avval kim yoki nima haqida gap ketayotganini aniqlang, keyin olmoshni tanlang.

1-mashq — Olmoshlarni tarjimasi bilan bog‘lang

1. I   2. you   3. he   4. she

5. it   6. we   7. they

Ma’nolari: u (narsa/hayvon) · biz · men · ular · u (erkak) · sen/siz/sizlar · u (ayol)

Javoblar:
1. men. 2. sen/siz/sizlar. 3. u (erkak). 4. u (ayol).
5. u (narsa/hayvon). 6. biz. 7. ular.

2-mashq — Mos subject pronounni tanlang

1. Tom is a boy. (He / She)

2. Sara is my friend. (He / She)

3. The phone is new. (It / They)

4. My parents are at home. (We / They)

5. You and I are classmates. (We / They)

6. I am talking to you and your sister. (You / We)

Javoblar:
1. He. 2. She. 3. It. 4. They. 5. We. 6. You.

3-mashq — Ismni olmosh bilan almashtiring

Ikkinchi gapda ism yoki otni qaytarmasdan mos olmoshni ishlating.

1. Ali is a student. Ali is happy.

2. Malika is at home. Malika is tired.

3. The book is new. The book is on the table.

4. The boys are friends. The boys are here.

5. My brother and I are students. My brother and I are ready.

Javoblar:
1. Ali is a student. He is happy.
2. Malika is at home. She is tired.
3. The book is new. It is on the table.
4. The boys are friends. They are here.
5. My brother and I are students. We are ready.

4-mashq — We yoki They?

1. Ali and I = ______

2. Ali and Madina (men ular bilan gaplashmayapman) = ______

3. My friends and I = ______

4. My friends (men boshqa joydaman) = ______

Javoblar:
1. we. 2. they. 3. we. 4. they.

5-mashq — I katta harf bilan yoziladimi?

Gaplarni to‘g‘ri yozing:

1. my sister and i are friends.

2. i am from uzbekistan.

3. ali and i are students.

Javoblar:
1. My sister and I are friends.
2. I am from Uzbekistan.
3. Ali and I are students.

6-mashq — To‘g‘ri yoki noto‘g‘ri?

1. He odatda erkak yoki o‘g‘il bola haqida ishlatiladi.

2. She odatda ayol yoki qiz haqida ishlatiladi.

3. We gapirayotgan odamni o‘z ichiga oladi.

4. They faqat narsalar uchun ishlatiladi.

Javoblar:
1. To‘g‘ri. 2. To‘g‘ri. 3. To‘g‘ri. 4. Noto‘g‘ri — they odamlar va narsalar uchun ishlatiladi.
