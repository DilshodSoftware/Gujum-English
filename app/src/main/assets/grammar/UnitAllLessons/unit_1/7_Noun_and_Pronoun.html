7-DARS. NOUN AND PRONOUN

Ot va olmosh · Noun va pronounni farqlash
Bu darsda: avval ot nima ekanini ko‘ramiz. Keyin bir ism yoki narsaning nomini qayta-qayta takrorlamaslik uchun olmosh qanday ishlatilishini o‘rganamiz.

1. Noun — ot nima?

Noun — odam, hayvon, joy yoki narsaning nomi. O‘zbek tilida ham otlarni “Kim?” yoki “Nima?” deb so‘rab topamiz.

Noun = nom. boy, girl, cat, phone, park, Tashkent — bularning hammasi ot, chunki odam, hayvon, narsa yoki joyni nomlaydi.

2. Oddiy va atoqli otlar

Oddiy ot — tur yoki guruh nomi: teacher (o‘qituvchi), city (shahar). Atoqli ot esa aniq bir odam yoki joyning maxsus nomi: Ali, Tashkent.

a boy — Alio‘g‘il bola — Ali (ism)
a city — Tashkentshahar — Toshkent (shahar nomi)
an animal — a cathayvon — mushuk

Ingliz tilida ism, shahar va davlat nomlari katta harf bilan boshlanadi: Ali, Madina, Tashkent, Uzbekistan. Oddiy otlar esa gap boshida bo‘lmasa, ko‘pincha kichik harf bilan yoziladi: boy, book, school.

3. Pronoun — olmosh nima?

Pronoun — otning o‘rnida ishlatiladigan so‘z. U bir ismni gapda qayta-qayta aytmaslikka yordam beradi.

Takror bilan:

Ali is a student. Ali is happy. Ali has a book.

Ali o‘quvchi. Ali xursand. Alida kitob bor.

Olmosh bilan:

Ali is a student. He is happy. He has a book.

Ali o‘quvchi. U xursand. Unda kitob bor.

Ikkinchi va uchinchi gapda Ali ismini takrorlamadik. Uning o‘rniga he (“u”, erkak kishi haqida) ishlatdik.

4. Subject pronouns — ega olmoshlari

Bu olmoshlar gapda kim yoki nima haqida gap ketayotganini bildiradi. Oddiy darak gaplarda ega ko‘pincha gap boshida keladi.

6. Olmosh gapda qayerda keladi?

Oddiy darak gapda ko‘pincha avval ega keladi. Agar ismni olmoshga almashtirsak, olmosh ham egasining o‘rnida turadi.

Subject + gapning qolgan qismi
She is a teacher.U o‘qituvchi.
It is new.U yangi. (narsa)
They live in Tashkent.Ular Toshkentda yashaydi.

7. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

Madina is a teacher. He is kind. ❌
Madina is a teacher. She is kind. ✅

Ali and I are friends. They are happy. ❌
Ali and I are friends. We are happy. ✅

i am a student. ❌
I am a student. ✅ — I har doim katta harf bilan yoziladi.

Javoblar:

Mashq 2 — Ismni olmosh bilan almashtiring

1. Ali is a student. Ali is happy.

2. Sara is my friend. Sara is kind.

3. The phone is new. The phone is black.

4. Ali and I are students. Ali and I are here.

5. Ali and Sara are friends. Ali and Sara are happy.

Javoblar:
1. Ali is a student. He is happy.
2. Sara is my friend. She is kind.
3. The phone is new. It is black.
4. Ali and I are students. We are here.
5. Ali and Sara are friends. They are happy.

Mashq 3 — To‘g‘ri olmoshni tanlang

1. Tom is a boy. (He / She) is a student.

2. My mother is kind. (He / She) is kind.

3. The book is new. (It / They) is new.

4. My brother and I are here. (We / They) are here.

5. The cats are small. (It / They) are small.

6. You and your friend are students. (You / We) are students.

Javoblar:
1. He. 2. She. 3. It. 4. We. 5. They. 6. You.

Eng qisqa xulosa

Noun — odam, hayvon, joy yoki narsaning nomi: Ali, cat, book, school.
Pronoun — noun o‘rniga keladi: I, you, he, she, it, we, they.
Ali → he · Sara → she · book → it · Ali and I → we · Ali and Sara → they.
