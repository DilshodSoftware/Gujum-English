10-AMALIYOT. AM / IS / ARE

To be · egaga mos fe’lni tanlash · darak gaplar
Esda tuting: to be odamning kimligini, qandayligini yoki qayerdaligini bildiradi. I → am, he/she/it → is, you/we/they → are. Avval topshiriqlarni mustaqil bajaring, keyin javoblar bilan tekshiring.

1-mashq — Am, Is yoki Are?

Bo‘sh joyga egaga mos shaklni yozing.

1. I ___ from Uzbekistan.

2. My brother ___ twelve years old.

3. You ___ a good friend.

4. The books ___ on the desk.

5. It ___ a small dog.

6. Sara and I ___ classmates.

7. The weather ___ nice today.

8. Ali ___ at home.

9. We ___ ready for the lesson.

10. My parents ___ teachers.

Javoblar: 1. am; 2. is; 3. are; 4. are; 5. is; 6. are; 7. is; 8. is; 9. are; 10. are.

2-mashq — Ega va fe’lni juftlang

Har bir ega uchun mos to be shaklini yozing.

1. I   2. he   3. we   4. the cat   5. you   6. they   7. my sisters

Tanlov: am / is / are

Javoblar: 1. am; 2. is; 3. are; 4. is; 5. are; 6. are; 7. are.

3-mashq — Gap bo‘laklarini tartiblang

So‘zlardan to‘g‘ri inglizcha gap tuzing.

1. happy / am / I

2. a doctor / is / She

3. at school / are / They

4. new / The phone / is

5. friends / We / are

6. my teacher / You / are

Javoblar:
1. I am happy. 2. She is a doctor. 3. They are at school.
4. The phone is new. 5. We are friends. 6. You are my teacher.

4-mashq — Xatoni topib tuzating

1. I is a student.

2. They is at home.

3. My mother are kind.

4. We am happy.

5. The dogs is small.

Javoblar:
1. I am a student. 2. They are at home.
3. My mother is kind. 4. We are happy.
5. The dogs are small.

5-mashq — Kim? Qanday? Qayerda?

Gapdagi ma’lumot turini belgilang: kim/nima, qanday yoki qayerda.

1. I am a student.

2. The soup is hot.

3. They are in the garden.

4. He is a driver.

5. We are tired.

Javoblar: 1. kim?; 2. qanday?; 3. qayerda?; 4. kim?; 5. qanday?

6-mashq — O‘zbekchadan inglizchaga

1. Men xursandman.

2. U (qiz) uyda.

3. Biz do‘stmiz.

4. Mushuk kichkina.

5. Siz tayyorsiz.

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. I am happy. 2. She is at home. 3. We are friends.
4. The cat is small. 5. You are ready.
O‘zingizni tekshiring: gapda ega bormi? Undan keyin to be ning mos shakli bormi? Har bir javobni ovoz chiqarib o‘qing.
