16-DARS. POSSESSIVE ’S

Ali’s book · Egalikni ’s bilan ifodalash

Biror narsa kimga tegishli ekanini ingliz tilida ot yoki ismga ’s qo‘shib ham aytish mumkin.

egasi + ’s + narsa

Dars lug‘ati

Bu darsda uchraydigan so‘zlar — oldin eshitib, keyin darsni o‘qing.

pen — ruchka
bag — sumka
car — mashina
girl — qiz

the girl’s bag — qizning sumkasi

Bu yerda ’s o‘zbek tilidagi “-ning” ma’nosini beradi.

1. Bitta odam yoki narsa egasi bo‘lsa

Birlikdagi egadan keyin apostrof va s qo‘shamiz: ’s.

2. Apostrof qayerga qo‘yiladi?

Apostrof egadan keyin keladi, egalik qilinayotgan narsadan keyin emas.

Ali’s book
Ali = egasi · book = narsa
Apostrof + s Ali so‘zidan keyin qo‘yildi.
the teacher’s desk ✅
the teachers desk ❌ (egalik apostrofi yo‘q)
the teacher desk’s ❌ (apostrof noto‘g‘ri joyda)

3. Ko‘plikdagi egalar

Agar ko‘plikdagi egalar so‘zi allaqachon -s bilan tugasa, yana s qo‘shmaymiz. Faqat apostrof qo‘yamiz: -s’.

plural noun ending in -s + ’
the students’ classroomo‘quvchilarning sinfxonasi
my parents’ houseota-onamning uyi
the teachers’ bookso‘qituvchilarning kitoblari

Apostrofning joyi ma’noni o‘zgartiradi. Avval otning o‘zi birlikmi yoki ko‘plikmi, shuni aniqlang:

4. Ko‘plik, ammo -s bilan tugamasa

Ba’zi ko‘plikdagi otlar -s bilan tugamaydi. Bunday so‘zlarga odatdagidek ’s qo‘shamiz.

children → children’s toysbolalar → bolalarning o‘yinchoqlari
men → men’s shoeserkaklar → erkaklarning oyoq kiyimlari
women → women’s bagsayollar → ayollarning sumkalari
Taqqoslang: one child’s toy — bitta bolaning o‘yinchog‘i; children’s toys — bolalarning o‘yinchoqlari.

5. ’s va egalik qilinayotgan narsa

Egalik shaklida avval egasi, keyin unga tegishli narsa keladi.

Sara’s phoneSaraning telefoni
the boy’s bicyclebolaning velosipedi
the boys’ bicyclesbolalarning velosipedlari
the boy’s bicycle — bitta bolaning velosipedi
the boys’ bicycles — bir nechta bolaning velosipedlari

6. Possessive ’s va my / your / his...

Egalikni ikki xil usulda aytishimiz mumkin:

Ali’s bookAlining kitobi
His bookUning kitobi
Sara’s bagSaraning sumkasi
Her bagUning sumkasi

Ali’s / Sara’s — egasining ismini aytamiz. His / her — uning deb olmosh ishlatamiz.

7. ’s har doim egalikni bildirmaydi

’s ba’zan is ning qisqa shakli bo‘ladi. Ma’noni gapdan bilamiz.

Ali’s book is new.Alining kitobi yangi. (egalik)
Ali’s happy.Ali xursand. (= Ali is happy)
It’s a cat.Bu mushuk. (= It is a cat)
The cat’s tail is long.Mushukning dumi uzun. (egalik)
Ali’s book — ’s dan keyin ot (book) keladi, demak egalik.
Ali’s happy — ’s dan keyin sifat (happy) keladi, bu yerda ’s = is.

8. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

Ali book ❌ → Ali’s book ✅
Alis’ book ❌ → Ali’s book ✅ (bitta Ali)
the students’s room ❌ → the students’ room ✅ (ko‘plik -s bilan tugagan)
childrens’ toys ❌ → children’s toys ✅ (children -s bilan tugamaydi)
The cat’s sleeping. (egalik) ❌ → The cat’s tail is long. ✅ (egalik)
The cat’s sleeping. = The cat is sleeping. ✅ (’s = is)

Mashq 1 — ’s yoki s’ qo‘ying

Qavsdagi egani egalik shakliga o‘tkazing.

1. (Ali) ___ book

2. (the girl) ___ bicycle

3. (the students) ___ classroom

4. (the children) ___ toys

5. (my parents) ___ car

6. (the cat) ___ tail

Javoblar:
1. Ali’s book   2. the girl’s bicycle
3. the students’ classroom   4. the children’s toys
5. my parents’ car   6. the cat’s tail

Mashq 2 — To‘g‘ri shaklni tanlang

1. This is (Sara’s / Saras’) pencil.

2. The (boys’ / boys’s) room is clean. (bir nechta bola)

3. The (childrens’ / children’s) books are here.

4. (Mikes / Mike’s) mother is a doctor.

5. “It’s a dog.” — ’s nimaning qisqartmasi? (is / egalik)

Javoblar:
1. Sara’s   2. boys’   3. children’s
4. Mike’s   5. is (It’s = It is)

Mashq 3 — Ma’noga mos shaklni tanlang

1. Bitta bolaning sumkasi: (the boy’s bag / the boys’ bag)

2. Bir nechta qizning kitoblari: (the girl’s books / the girls’ books)

3. Bolalarning o‘yinchoqlari: (children’s toys / childrens’ toys)

4. Bitta mushukning dumi: (the cat’s tail / the cats’ tail)

Javoblar:
1. the boy’s bag. 2. the girls’ books.
3. children’s toys. 4. the cat’s tail.

Eng qisqa xulosa

Bitta egaga: Ali + ’s → Ali’s book.

-s bilan tugagan ko‘plikka: students + ’ → students’ books.

-s bilan tugamagan ko‘plikka: children + ’s → children’s toys.

Egalikda tartib: egasi + ’s + narsa.

’s ba’zan egalikni, ba’zan is ni bildiradi: Ali’s book / Ali’s happy.
