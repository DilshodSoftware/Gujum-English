8-AMALIYOT. SINGULAR AND PLURAL NOUNS

Birlik · Ko‘plik · Imlo qoidalari · A/An
Eslatma: singular — bitta; plural — ikki yoki undan ko‘p. Har bir mashqda avval otning birlikmi yoki ko‘plikmi ekanini aniqlang.

1-mashq — Singular yoki Plural?

1. book   2. books   3. child

4. children   5. buses   6. baby

7. cities   8. women

Javoblar:
Singular: book, child, baby.
Plural: books, children, buses, cities, women.

2-mashq — Oddiy ko‘plikka -s qo‘shing

1. pen → ___   2. car → ___   3. dog → ___   4. teacher → ___

Javoblar: pens, cars, dogs, teachers.

3-mashq — -es qo‘shing

1. bus → ___   2. box → ___   3. dish → ___   4. watch → ___

Javoblar: buses, boxes, dishes, watches.

4-mashq — Y bilan tugagan otlar

1. baby → ___   2. city → ___   3. boy → ___   4. toy → ___

Maslahat: undosh + y bo‘lsa y → ies; unli + y bo‘lsa faqat -s.

Javoblar: babies, cities, boys, toys.

5-mashq — Ko‘plikdagi qoidaga bo‘ysunmaydigan otlar

1. man → ___   2. woman → ___   3. child → ___

4. person → ___   5. foot → ___   6. tooth → ___

Javoblar: men, women, children, people, feet, teeth.

6-mashq — Son bilan mos shaklni tanlang

1. one (book / books)

2. three (box / boxes)

3. two (child / children)

4. one (apple / apples)

5. five (student / students)

Javoblar:
1. book. 2. boxes. 3. children. 4. apple. 5. students.

7-mashq — A/An bilan ko‘plikni tekshiring

1. Qaysi biri to‘g‘ri: a book / a books?

2. Qaysi biri to‘g‘ri: an apple / an apples?

3. Xatoni tuzating: two a cars.

4. Bitta olma: ___ apple. Bir nechta olma: ___ apples.

Javoblar:
1. a book. 2. an apple. 3. two cars.
4. an apple; apples.

8-mashq — Xatoni topib tuzating

1. two book

2. three childs

3. four boxs

4. two babys

5. an books

Javoblar:
1. two books. 2. three children. 3. four boxes. 4. two babies. 5. books.

9-mashq — Gap ichidagi birlik va ko‘plik

1. I have one pen and two ___ (book).

2. She has three ___ (box).

3. We see one ___ and four ___ (child).

4. He has a ___ (baby) and two ___ (toy).

Javoblar:
1. books. 2. boxes. 3. child, children. 4. baby, toys.
