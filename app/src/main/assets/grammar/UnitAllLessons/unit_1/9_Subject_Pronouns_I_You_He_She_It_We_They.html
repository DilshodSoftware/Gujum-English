Oldingi dars: noun — ot, ya’ni odam yoki narsaning nomi. Bu darsda otning o‘rnida ishlatiladigan so‘zlarni o‘rganamiz. Ular bir xil ismni qayta-qayta aytmaslikka yordam beradi.

1. Subject va pronoun nima?

Subject — gap kim yoki nima haqida ekanini bildiradigan ega. Pronoun — otning o‘rnida keladigan olmosh. Subject pronoun esa ega o‘rnida ishlatiladigan olmoshdir.

Ali is a student. — Ali o‘quvchi.
He is a student. — U o‘quvchi.
Ikkinchi gapda he Ali ismining o‘rniga keldi.

3. Gapiruvchi va tinglovchi

I — men

I gapirayotgan odamning o‘zini bildiradi.

I am Ali.Men Aliman.
I am a student.Men o‘quvchiman.
I ingliz tilida har doim katta harf bilan yoziladi, hatto gapning o‘rtasida kelsa ham: Ali and I.

You — sen, siz yoki sizlar

You bitta odamga ham, bir nechta odamga ham murojaat qilganda ishlatiladi. Ma’nosi vaziyatga qarab “sen”, “siz” yoki “sizlar” bo‘ladi.

You are my friend.Sen mening do‘stimsan / Siz mening do‘stimsiz.
You are students.Sizlar o‘quvchisizlar.

4. He, She va It

O‘zbek tilida he va she ikkalasi ham “u” deb tarjima qilinadi. Ingliz tilida esa odatda odamning jinsiga qarab farqlanadi.

Ali is a boy. He is a student.Ali o‘g‘il bola. U o‘quvchi.
Sara is a girl. She is a student.Sara qiz bola. U o‘quvchi.
The book is new. It is new.Kitob yangi. U yangi. (narsa)
He — erkak kishi haqida. She — ayol kishi haqida. It — narsa yoki hayvon haqida. Hayvonning jinsi ma’lum bo‘lsa, ba’zan he yoki she ham ishlatiladi.

5. We va They

Ikkala olmosh ham bir nechta odamni bildiradi, lekin farqi bor:

We = men va men bilan birga bo‘lgan odam(lar). Gapiruvchi guruh ichida.
They = ular. Gapiruvchi bu guruh ichida emas.
Ali and I are friends. We are friends.Ali va men do‘stmiz. Biz do‘stmiz.
Ali and Sara are friends. They are friends.Ali va Sara do‘stlar. Ular do‘stlar.
The books are new. They are new.Kitoblar yangi. Ular yangi.

7. Subject pronoun gapda qayerda turadi?

Oddiy inglizcha darak gaplarda subject ko‘pincha gap boshida keladi. Hozircha misollarda olmoshning joyiga e’tibor bering; am/is/are ning qoidasini keyingi darsda o‘rganamiz.

She is at home.U uyda.
They are students.Ular o‘quvchilar.

8. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

Ali is a student. She is happy. ❌
Ali is a student. He is happy. ✅

Ali and I are here. They are students. ❌
Ali and I are here. We are students. ✅

i am Sara. ❌
I am Sara. ✅

Mashq 1 — To‘g‘ri olmoshni tanlang

1. Tom is a boy. (He / She) is a boy.

2. My mother is at home. (He / She) is at home.

3. The phone is new. (It / They) is new.

4. Ali and I are students. (We / They) are students.

5. Ali and Sam are brothers. (We / They) are brothers.

6. I am speaking to you and your friend. (You / We) are students.

Javoblar:
1. He. 2. She. 3. It. 4. We. 5. They. 6. You.

Mashq 2 — Ismni olmoshga almashtiring

1. Ali is a student. Ali is happy.

2. Madina is a teacher. Madina is kind.

3. The dog is small. The dog is brown.

4. Ali and I are friends. Ali and I are here.

5. Ali and Madina are students. Ali and Madina are here.

Javoblar:
1. Ali is a student. He is happy.
2. Madina is a teacher. She is kind.
3. The dog is small. It is brown.
4. Ali and I are friends. We are here.
5. Ali and Madina are students. They are here.

Mashq 3 — To‘g‘ri yoki noto‘g‘ri?

1. You faqat bitta odamga ishlatiladi.

2. I doimo katta harf bilan yoziladi.

3. We gapirayotgan odamni o‘z ichiga oladi.

4. They faqat odamlar uchun ishlatiladi, narsalar uchun emas.

Javoblar:
1. Noto‘g‘ri. 2. To‘g‘ri. 3. To‘g‘ri. 4. Noto‘g‘ri.
