25-AMALIYOT. SHORT ANSWERS

Do / Does savollari · To Be savollari · savoldagi egani o‘zgartirish
Qisqa javobda butun gapni takrorlamaymiz. Do savoliga do/don’t, does savoliga does/doesn’t bilan javob beramiz. Are you…? kabi to be savoliga esa Yes, I am / No, I’m not deyiladi.

1-mashq — Bo‘sh joyni to‘ldiring

Ha yoki yo‘q ma’nosiga mos qisqa javobni yozing.

1. Do you like apples? (ha) — Yes, ___ ___.

2. Does he work here? (yo‘q) — No, ___ ___.

3. Do they study English? (ha) — Yes, ___ ___.

4. Does your sister drive? (yo‘q) — No, ___ ___.

5. Are you tired? (ha) — Yes, ___ ___.

6. Is the shop open? (yo‘q) — No, ___ ___.

Javoblar: 1. I do; 2. he doesn’t; 3. they do; 4. she doesn’t; 5. I am; 6. it isn’t.

2-mashq — Savoldagi egani javobga moslang

1. Do you live here? (o‘zingiz javob bering)

2. Does Ali play football? (Ali haqida)

3. Do Ali and Sam work together? (ular haqida)

4. Does the cat sleep outside? (mushuk haqida)

Javoblar (ha deb javob berilganda):
1. Yes, I do. 2. Yes, he does. 3. Yes, they do. 4. Yes, it does.

3-mashq — Qisqami yoki to‘liq javobmi?

Quyidagi javoblar qisqa yoki to‘liq ekanini belgilang.

1. Yes, I do.

2. No, she doesn’t work at night.

3. Yes, they are.

4. No, he doesn’t.

Javoblar: 1. qisqa; 2. to‘liq; 3. qisqa; 4. qisqa.

4-mashq — Do/Doesmi yoki Am/Is/Are?

1. Do you play chess? — Yes, I ___.

2. Are you a student? — Yes, I ___.

3. Does she like tea? — No, she ___.

4. Is he at school? — No, he ___.

5. Do they work here? — No, they ___.

Javoblar: 1. do; 2. am; 3. doesn’t; 4. isn’t; 5. don’t.

5-mashq — To‘g‘ri qisqa javobni tanlang

1. Does Mina read? (Yes, she does. / Yes, she do.)

2. Do you eat meat? (No, I don’t. / No, you don’t.)

3. Is Tom ready? (Yes, he is. / Yes, he does.)

4. Are they at home? (No, they aren’t. / No, they don’t.)

Javoblar: 1. Yes, she does. 2. No, I don’t. 3. Yes, he is. 4. No, they aren’t.

6-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. Does your dad work here? — Yes, he do.

2. Do you like tea? — Yes, you do.

3. Are you happy? — Yes, I do.

4. Does Sara drive? — No, she don’t.

Javoblar:
1. Yes, he does. 2. Yes, I do.
3. Yes, I am. 4. No, she doesn’t.

7-mashq — O‘zingiz haqida javob bering

1. Do you read every day?

2. Do you like music?

3. Are you at home now?

Namunaviy javoblar: Yes, I do. / No, I don’t. · Yes, I am. / No, I’m not. Javoblar siz haqingizda to‘g‘ri bo‘lishi mumkin.
