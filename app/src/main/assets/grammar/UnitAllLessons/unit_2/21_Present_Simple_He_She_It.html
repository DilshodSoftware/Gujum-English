Oldingi darsda Present Simple darak gapining umumiy qolipini ko‘rdik. Bu darsda bitta odam yoki narsa — he, she, it bilan fe’lning oxiri qanday o‘zgarishini batafsil mashq qilamiz.

1. “U” haqida gapirganda nima o‘zgaradi?

Ingliz tilida he (u — erkak), she (u — ayol) va it (u — narsa/hayvon) bilan Present Simple darak gapida fe’l odatda -s oladi.

he / she / it + verb-s
I work. → He works.Men ishlayman. → U ishlaydi.
You read. → She reads.Sen o‘qiysan. → U o‘qiydi.
They sleep. → The baby sleeps.Ular uxlaydi. → Chaqaloq uxlaydi.

Bu yerda fe’l — ish-harakatni bildiradigan so‘z: work (ishlamoq), read (o‘qimoq), sleep (uxlamoq).

2. He / She / It dan tashqari bitta ism ham shu qoida

Agar ega bitta odam yoki narsa nomi bo‘lsa, uni he, she yoki it o‘rniga qo‘yib tekshirish mumkin.

Ali = he → Ali works.Ali ishlaydi.
Madina = she → Madina reads.Madina o‘qiydi.
The dog = it → The dog sleeps.It uxlaydi.

Bir nechta ism bilan esa fe’lga -s qo‘shmaymiz: Ali and Madina work. — Ali va Madina ishlaydi.

Fe’l oxiri -s, -sh, -ch, -x bilan tugasa, he/she/it uchun odatda -es qo‘shiladi. Ayrim ko‘p ishlatiladigan -o bilan tugagan fe’llar ham -es oladi.

5. Undosh harf + Y: Y → IES

Fe’l undosh harf bilan tugab, undan keyin y kelsa, y ni i ga o‘zgartirib, -es qo‘shamiz. Natijada -ies hosil bo‘ladi.

6. Unli harf + Y: faqat S

Y dan oldin unli harf (a, e, i, o, u) bo‘lsa, y o‘zgarmaydi. Faqat oxiriga -s qo‘shamiz.

study → studies: undan oldin d undoshi bor.
play → plays: undan oldin a unlisi bor.

7. Have va Be fe’llari

Ba’zi fe’llarning he/she/it bilan shakli oddiy qoidaga aynan o‘xshamaydi. Ularni alohida eslab qolamiz.

Oddiy shaklHe / She / ItMisol
havehasShe has a dog.
beisHe is happy.
To be fe’lining shakllari: I am, you/we/they are, he/she/it is. Uni oddiy fe’lga -s qo‘shish qoidasi bilan aralashtirmang.

8. Bu qoida faqat darak gapga tegishli

Hozir biz tasdiq ma’nosidagi darak gaplarni o‘rganyapmiz: biror ishni bajarishini aytamiz.

He plays football.U futbol o‘ynaydi.
She does her homework.U uy vazifasini bajaradi.
He do not play. ❌ — bu inkor gap bo‘lib, alohida qoidaga ega.
He plays. ✅ — ushbu darsdagi darak gap.

9. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

I work. ✅   I works. ❌
She reads. ✅   She read. ❌
He studies. ✅   He studys. ❌
They go. ✅   They goes. ❌
Tom has a bike. ✅   Tom have a bike. ❌

Mashq 1 — Fe’lning to‘g‘ri shaklini tanlang

1. I (like / likes) apples.

2. She (read / reads) every evening.

3. They (play / plays) football.

4. My brother (watch / watches) TV.

5. We (study / studies) English.

6. The baby (cry / cries) at night.

7. You (go / goes) to school.

Javoblar:
1. like. 2. reads. 3. play. 4. watches. 5. study. 6. cries. 7. go.

Mashq 2 — Qavsdagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri yozing

1. He ___ to school every day. (go)

2. I ___ my room on Saturdays. (clean)

3. Anna ___ English at home. (study)

4. My father ___ a car. (have)

5. You ___ very fast. (run)

6. The teacher ___ the lesson. (teach)

7. My friends ___ music. (enjoy)

Javoblar:
1. goes. 2. clean. 3. studies. 4. has. 5. run. 6. teaches. 7. enjoy.

Mashq 4 — So‘zlardan gap tuzing

1. every day / I / English / study

2. watches / my father / TV / in the evening

Javoblar:

Present Simple odat, takroriy ish va umumiy haqiqatni bildiradi.

I / you / we / they + oddiy fe’l: They play.

He / she / it + fe’l-s / -es / -ies: She plays; he watches; Anna studies.

have → has; to be alohida: he is.
