Bu darsda: “har doim”, “odatda”, “ba’zan” kabi so‘zlar bilan odatimiz qanchalik tez-tez bo‘lishini aytamiz.

1. Adverb of frequency nima?

Adverb of frequency — ish-harakat qanchalik tez-tez sodir bo‘lishini bildiradigan so‘z. U Present Simple bilan juda ko‘p ishlatiladi.

I read every day.Men har kuni o‘qiyman.
I sometimes read.Men ba’zan o‘qiyman.

Birinchi gapda har kuni takrorlanadi. Ikkinchi gapda esa ba’zi paytlarda sodir bo‘ladi.

Tez-tezlikni taxminan shunday tasavvur qiling:

always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never
Bu tartib aniq foizli o‘lchov emas. U so‘zlarning ko‘proqdan kamroqqa qarab taxminiy ketma-ketligini ko‘rsatadi.

3. Oddiy fe’l bilan: ravish fe’ldan oldin

Oddiy harakat fe’li bilan tez-tezlik ravishi odatda ega va fe’l orasida keladi.

Subject + adverb + main verb + ...
I always drink water.Men har doim suv ichaman.
We usually walk to school.Biz odatda maktabga piyoda boramiz.
They often play football.Ular tez-tez futbol o‘ynaydi.
She sometimes reads at night.U ba’zan kechasi kitob o‘qiydi.
He rarely watches TV.U kamdan-kam televizor ko‘radi.
I never eat meat.Men hech qachon go‘sht yemayman.

He/she/it bilan fe’lga -s/-es/-ies qo‘shish qoidasi o‘zgarishsiz qoladi: She often reads.

4. To Be bilan: ravish am/is/are dan keyin

Agar gapda am, is, are bo‘lsa, tez-tezlik ravishi odatda ulardan keyin keladi.

Subject + am/is/are + adverb + ...
I am always busy.Men har doim bandman.
She is usually happy.U odatda xursand.
They are often at home.Ular tez-tez uyda bo‘ladi.
Taqqoslang:
Oddiy fe’l: She often reads. — ravish fe’ldan oldin.
To be: She is often tired. — ravish is dan keyin.

5. Har kuni / haftada bir marta kabi iboralar

Tez-tezlikni bitta ravish bilan emas, bir necha so‘zli vaqt iborasi bilan ham aytish mumkin. Bular ko‘pincha gap oxirida keladi.

I call my grandmother every week.Men buvimga har hafta qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.
He plays tennis on Sundays.U yakshanba kunlari tennis o‘ynaydi.

6. Ravish joyida ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

I drink always tea. ❌
I always drink tea. ✅

She reads often books. ❌
She often reads books. ✅

He always is late. ❌
He is always late. ✅ (to be dan keyin)

Mashq 1 — Ma’nosiga mos ravishni tanlang

1. 100% ga yaqin: (always / never)

2. “Ba’zan”: (sometimes / usually)

3. Juda kam: (rarely / often)

4. “Hech qachon”: (never / always)

Javoblar:
1. always. 2. sometimes. 3. rarely. 4. never.

Mashq 2 — Ravishni to‘g‘ri joyga qo‘ying

1. I / drink / always / tea.

2. She / is / usually / happy.

3. They / often / play / football.

4. He / late / is / never.

Javoblar:
1. I always drink tea. 2. She is usually happy.
3. They often play football. 4. He is never late.

Mashq 3 — O‘zbekchadan inglizchaga

1. Men odatda uyda ishlayman.

2. U (qiz) hech qachon qahva ichmaydi.

3. Biz ba’zan futbol o‘ynaymiz.

Javoblar:
1. I usually work at home.
2. She never drinks coffee.
3. We sometimes play football.
