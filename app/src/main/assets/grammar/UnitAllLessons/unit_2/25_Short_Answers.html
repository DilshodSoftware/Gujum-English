25-DARS. SHORT ANSWERS

Present Simple savollariga qisqa javob · Yes, I do / No, I don’t
Short answer — qisqa javob. Savoldagi butun gapni qayta aytmaymiz; Yes/No va yordamchi so‘z bilan javob beramiz.

1. Do bilan berilgan savolga javob

Savol Do bilan boshlangan bo‘lsa, qisqa javobda odatda do yoki don’t ishlatamiz.

Yes, + subject + do.

No, + subject + don’t.
Do you like tea?Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.
Do they live here?Yes, they do. / No, they don’t.
Do we need a ticket?Yes, we do. / No, we don’t.

Savolning o‘zbekcha ma’nosiga qarab Yes yoki No ni tanlang. Ha javobida do, yo‘q javobida don’t ishlatiladi.

2. Does bilan berilgan savolga javob

Savol Does bilan boshlangan bo‘lsa, qisqa javobda does yoki doesn’t ishlatamiz.

Yes, + he / she / it + does.

No, + he / she / it + doesn’t.
Does he play football?Yes, he does. / No, he doesn’t.
Does Sara speak English?Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.
Does the shop open early?Yes, it does. / No, it doesn’t.

Ismni takrorlamaslik uchun qisqa javobda olmosh ishlatamiz: Sara → she, the shop → it.

3. Savoldagi you javobda I ga o‘zgaradi

Bir odam sizdan Do you...? deb so‘rasa, siz o‘zingiz haqingizda I deb javob berasiz.

Do you work here?Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.
Do you like apples?Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.
Do you like coffee? — Yes, I do. ✅
Do you like coffee? — Yes, you do. ❌
Javob berayotgan odam o‘zi haqida I deydi.

4. Ism yoki boshqa ega bilan qisqa javob

Does Ali study English?Yes, he does. / No, he doesn’t.
Does Madina work here?Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.
Do Ali and Sam play?Yes, they do. / No, they don’t.

5. Qisqa javob va to‘liq javob

Qisqa javobda asosiy fe’l odatda qayta aytilmaydi. To‘liq javobda esa butun fikrni aytish mumkin.

Do you read books?Qisqa: Yes, I do.
Do you read books?To‘liq: Yes, I read books.
Does she work?Qisqa: No, she doesn’t.
Does she work?To‘liq: No, she doesn’t work.
Qisqa ijobiy javobda Yes, I do deymiz; Yes, I don’t demaymiz. Don’t/doesn’t — inkor javoblar uchun.

6. To Be savollarining qisqa javobi boshqacha

Agar savol am, is yoki are bilan boshlangan bo‘lsa, javobda ham shu to be shaklini ishlatamiz. Do/does qo‘shmaymiz.

Are you tired?Yes, I am. / No, I’m not.
Is she at home?Yes, she is. / No, she isn’t.
Do you work? → Yes, I do.
Are you tired? → Yes, I am.
Savolning boshidagi yordamchi so‘zga qarab, javobdagi fe’lni tanlang.

7. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

Does she read? — Yes, she does. ✅
Yes, she do. ❌

Do they work? — No, they don’t. ✅
No, they doesn’t. ❌

Are you happy? — Yes, I am. ✅
Yes, I do. ❌ (savolda are ishlatilgan)

Mashq 1 — Qisqa javobni to‘ldiring

1. Do you like tea? (ha) — Yes, ___ ___.

2. Does he play tennis? (yo‘q) — No, ___ ___.

3. Do they study English? (ha) — Yes, ___ ___.

4. Does Sara work here? (yo‘q) — No, ___ ___.

5. Are you ready? (ha) — Yes, ___ ___.

Javoblar:
1. I do. 2. he doesn’t. 3. they do. 4. she doesn’t. 5. I am.

Mashq 2 — To‘g‘ri javobni tanlang

1. Does your brother read? — (Yes, he do. / Yes, he does.)

2. Do you play tennis? — (No, I don’t. / No, you don’t.)

3. Are they at home? — (Yes, they are. / Yes, they do.)

4. Does the cat sleep? — (No, it doesn’t. / No, it don’t.)

Javoblar:
1. Yes, he does. 2. No, I don’t.
3. Yes, they are. 4. No, it doesn’t.
