22-DARS. PRESENT SIMPLE — NEGATIVE

Present Simple zamonida inkor gaplar · don’t / doesn’t
Oldingi mavzu: darak gapda I work, She works dedik. Endi biror ishni qilmasligimizni, yoqtirmasligimizni yoki muntazam bajarmasligimizni aytamiz.

1. Inkor gap nima?

Inkor gap biror harakat bajarilmasligini bildiradi. O‘zbekchadagi “bormayman”, “ishlamaydi”, “yoqtirmaymiz” kabi ma’nolarni beradi.

Oddiy harakat fe’li bo‘lsa, inkor qilish uchun fe’lning oldiga shunchaki not qo‘ymaymiz. Ingliz tilida don’t yoki doesn’t yordam beradi.

2. Don’t va Doesn’t nimani bildiradi?

Don’t — do not ning qisqa shakli. Doesn’t — does not ning qisqa shakli. Ular Present Simple inkor gap yasashga yordam beradi.

3. I / You / We / They bilan Don’t

Bu egalar bilan don’t ishlatamiz. Undan keyingi fe’l oddiy shaklda qoladi.

I don’t drink coffee.Men qahva ichmayman.
You don’t work here.Sen / siz bu yerda ishlamaysan / ishlamaysiz.
We don’t play tennis.Biz tennis o‘ynamaymiz.
They don’t live in London.Ular Londonda yashamaydi.

4. He / She / It bilan Doesn’t

He, she, it va bitta ism bilan doesn’t ishlatiladi.

He doesn’t drink coffee.U qahva ichmaydi.
She doesn’t work on Sundays.U yakshanba kunlari ishlamaydi.
The shop doesn’t open at night.Do‘kon tunda ochilmaydi.
Ali doesn’t play tennis.Ali tennis o‘ynamaydi.

5. Doesn’t dan keyin fe’lga -s qo‘shmaymiz

Darak gapda he/she/it bilan fe’l ko‘pincha -s oladi: She works. Inkor gapda esa bu -s yordamchi doesn’t ichiga o‘tadi, asosiy fe’l oddiy shaklda qoladi.

Darak: She likes tea.U choyni yoqtiradi.
Inkor: She doesn’t like tea.U choyni yoqtirmaydi.
He doesn’t work. ✅
He doesn’t works. ❌
Anna doesn’t study French. ✅
Anna doesn’t studies French. ❌

6. To‘liq va qisqa shakllar

To‘liq shaklQisqa shaklMa’nosi
do notdon’t...mayman / ...maysan / ...maymiz / ...maydi
does notdoesn’t...maydi (he/she/it)

Kundalik nutqda qisqa shakllar ko‘p ishlatiladi. Don’t va doesn’t dagi apostrof tushib qolgan harflar o‘rnida turadi.

8. To Be bilan adashtirmang

Am / is / are bilan inkor gapda do/does kerak emas. To be dan keyin bevosita not qo‘yamiz.

She is not tired.U charchagan emas. (to be + not)
She doesn’t work.U ishlamaydi. (oddiy harakat fe’li)
He is not at home. ✅
He doesn’t be at home. ❌
He doesn’t live here. ✅

9. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

She don’t like tea. ❌ → She doesn’t like tea. ✅
They doesn’t work. ❌ → They don’t work. ✅
He doesn’t plays. ❌ → He doesn’t play. ✅
I not like coffee. ❌ → I don’t like coffee. ✅

Mashq 1 — Don’t yoki Doesn’t?

1. I ___ eat meat.

2. She ___ work here.

3. They ___ study at night.

4. My brother ___ watch TV.

5. You ___ need a car.

6. The dog ___ like milk.

Javoblar:
1. don’t. 2. doesn’t. 3. don’t. 4. doesn’t. 5. don’t. 6. doesn’t.

Mashq 2 — Inkor shakliga o‘tkazing

1. I play tennis.

2. He likes coffee.

3. We live in Samarkand.

4. Anna studies Spanish.

Javoblar:
1. I don’t play tennis.
2. He doesn’t like coffee.
3. We don’t live in Samarkand.
4. Anna doesn’t study Spanish.

Mashq 3 — Xatolarni tuzating

1. He don’t read books.

2. I doesn’t eat fish.

3. She doesn’t likes music.

4. They not work here.

Javoblar:
1. He doesn’t read books. 2. I don’t eat fish.
3. She doesn’t like music. 4. They don’t work here.

I / you / we / they → don’t + fe’lning oddiy shakli.

He / she / it → doesn’t + fe’lning oddiy shakli.
