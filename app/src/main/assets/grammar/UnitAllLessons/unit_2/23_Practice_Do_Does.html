23-AMALIYOT. DO / DOES

Yordamchi fe’lmi yoki “qilmoq” ma’nosidagi asosiy fe’lmi?
Tanlash: I/you/we/they va ko‘plikdagi ega → do; he/she/it va bitta ism/narsa → does. Savol va inkorda yordamchi fe’ldan keyin asosiy fe’l oddiy shaklda keladi. Am/is/are bilan do/does ishlatilmaydi.

1-mashq — Egasiga mos Do yoki Does

1. ___ you speak English?

2. ___ your sister work here?

3. ___ the children need help?

4. ___ the bus stop near the school?

5. ___ we have enough time?

6. ___ Ali and Sam play tennis?

Javoblar: 1. Do; 2. Does; 3. Do; 4. Does; 5. Do; 6. Do.

2-mashq — Savolni to‘liq tuzing

Yordamchi fe’l va qavsdagi fe’lning mos shaklidan foydalaning.

1. ___ he ___ (like) pizza?

2. ___ they ___ (watch) films?

3. ___ Sara ___ (study) English?

4. ___ your parents ___ (work) here?

Javoblar:
1. Does he like pizza? 2. Do they watch films?
3. Does Sara study English? 4. Do your parents work here?

3-mashq — Asosiy “Do” va yordamchi “Do”

Qalin so‘zlarning vazifasini belgilang: yordamchi yoki “qilmoq” fe’li.

1. Do you like music?

2. I do my homework after school.

3. Does he do the dishes?

4. They do exercise every morning.

Javoblar:
1. Do = yordamchi. 2. do = “qilmoq” asosiy fe’li.
3. Does = yordamchi; do = “qilmoq” asosiy fe’li.
4. do = “qilmoq” asosiy fe’li.

4-mashq — Savolmi, inkormi yoki to be gapimi?

1. ___ you play chess? (savol)

2. He ___ not like tea. (inkor)

3. ___ she happy? (to be bilan savol)

4. They ___ not work here. (inkor)

5. My brother ___ a doctor. (to be bilan tasdiq)

Javoblar: 1. Do; 2. does; 3. Is; 4. do; 5. is.

5-mashq — Xatoni toping

1. Does she likes cats?

2. Do your father work here?

3. Does he be tired?

4. Do they plays football?

5. Does Anna do her homework? (to‘g‘rimi?)

Javoblar:
1. Does she like cats? 2. Does your father work here?
3. Is he tired? 4. Do they play football?
5. To‘g‘ri: Does yordamchi; ikkinchi do — “qilmoq” fe’li.

6-mashq — Darakni inkorga va savolga o‘tkazing

1. They live here. (inkor)

2. He plays tennis. (inkor)

3. You need a pen. (savol)

4. Sara reads at night. (savol)

Javoblar:
1. They don’t live here. 2. He doesn’t play tennis.
3. Do you need a pen? 4. Does Sara read at night?
