Asosiy farq: Present Simple odat va takrorlanadigan ishlar uchun. Present Continuous esa ayni hozir yoki vaqtincha davom etayotgan ish uchun ishlatiladi.

1. Odat va hozirgi harakat

Bir xil fe’l bilan ikki zamonni taqqoslaymiz. O‘zbekchadagi “o‘qiyman” va “o‘qiyapman” farqiga e’tibor bering.

Present Simple: I read every evening.Men har oqshom kitob o‘qiyman. (odat)
Present Continuous: I am reading now.Men hozir o‘qiyapman. (ayni paytda)
O‘zingizdan so‘rang: “Bu odat bo‘lib takrorlanadimi?” — Present Simple. “Bu ish ayni hozir davom etyaptimi?” — Present Continuous.

2. Present Simple ni eslaymiz

Odatlar, jadval bo‘yicha takrorlanadigan ishlar va umumiy haqiqatlar uchun ishlatiladi.

I / you / we / they + verb

he / she / it + verb-s / -es / -ies
She works every day.U har kuni ishlaydi.
They play football on Sundays.Ular yakshanba kunlari futbol o‘ynaydi.
Plants need water.O‘simliklarga suv kerak.

3. Present Continuous ni eslaymiz

Ayni paytda bajarilayotgan yoki vaqtinchalik davom etayotgan ish uchun ishlatiladi.

Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing
She is working now.U hozir ishlayapti.
They are playing at the moment.Ular ayni paytda o‘ynashyapti.
I am staying with my aunt this week.Men bu hafta xolamnikida turibman. (vaqtinchalik)

4. Bir xil fe’l bilan taqqoslaymiz

Present Simple — odatPresent Continuous — hozir

U bugun uyda ishlayapti.

5. Vaqt bildiradigan so‘zlar yordam beradi

Bu so‘zlar ma’noni anglashga yordam beradi, lekin faqat shu so‘zga qarab zamonni tanlamaymiz. Gap nimani anglatayotganini ham o‘qiymiz.

7. Ba’zi fe’llar odatda Present Simple da ishlatiladi

Like (yoqtirmoq), know (bilmoq), want (xohlamoq) kabi fe’llar ko‘pincha holat yoki fikrni bildiradi. Boshlang‘ich darajada ularni odatda Present Simple da ishlatamiz.

I like this song.Men bu qo‘shiqni yoqtiraman.
She knows my name.U mening ismimni biladi.
They want some water.Ular biroz suv xohlaydi.
Boshlang‘ich qoida sifatida I like deb eslab qoling. Bunday fe’llar bilan hozirgi zamonning -ing shakli ko‘pincha ishlatilmaydi.

Mashq 1 — Present Simple yoki Present Continuous?

Qavs ichidan gapning ma’nosiga mos variantni tanlang.

1. I (drink / am drinking) tea every morning.

2. Look! The baby (sleeps / is sleeping).

3. She (works / is working) at a bank every day.

4. We (eat / are eating) dinner right now.

5. He usually (walks / is walking) to school.

6. They (play / are playing) football at the moment.

7. I (like / am liking) this book.

Javoblar:
1. drink. 2. is sleeping. 3. works. 4. are eating.
5. walks. 6. are playing. 7. like.

Mashq 2 — Qavsdagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing

1. She ___ (read) every night.

2. She ___ (read) a book now.

3. They ___ (play) tennis on Sundays.

4. They ___ (play) tennis at the moment.

5. I ___ (write) a message right now.

6. My brother ___ (go) to work every day.

Javoblar:
1. reads. 2. is reading. 3. play.
4. are playing. 5. am writing. 6. goes.

Mashq 3 — Xatoni to‘g‘rilang

1. I am eat now.

2. She go to school every day.

3. They is playing now.

4. He is works in a shop.

Javoblar:
1. I am eating now. 2. She goes to school every day.
3. They are playing now. 4. He works in a shop.
Tanlash uchun oxirgi savol:
Bu ish odatmi yoki umumiy haqiqatmi? → Present Simple.
Bu ish hozir davom etyaptimi? → Present Continuous.
