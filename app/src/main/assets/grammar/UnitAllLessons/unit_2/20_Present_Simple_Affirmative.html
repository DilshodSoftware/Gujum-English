Bu darsda: Present Simple zamonidagi oddiy tasdiq gaplarni o‘rganamiz. Hali ingliz tilini boshlamagan bo‘lsangiz ham, yangi so‘zlarning ma’nosi misollar yonida berilgan. Avval ma’noni o‘qing, keyin gap tuzilishiga qarang.

1. Present Simple nima?

Present Simple — hozirgi oddiy zamon. U odat tusiga kirgan, tez-tez takrorlanadigan ishlar va odatda o‘zgarmaydigan umumiy haqiqatlar haqida gapirishda ishlatiladi.

I go to school every day.Men har kuni maktabga boraman. (odat)
She reads before bed.U uxlashdan oldin kitob o‘qiydi. (takroriy ish)
The sun rises in the east.Quyosh sharqdan chiqadi. (umumiy haqiqat)
Present Simple “aynan hozir shu lahzada qilyapman” degani bo‘lavermaydi. Ko‘pincha odat yoki muntazam ishni bildiradi: I read every day. — Men har kuni o‘qiyman.

2. Avval darak gapni tushunamiz

Darak gap — biror narsa haqida xabar beradigan oddiy tasdiq gap. Bu darsda hozircha “qilaman / qiladi” shaklidagi darak gaplarni o‘rganamiz.

Subject + Verb (+ Object)

Subject — kim yoki nima? Verb — nima qiladi? Object — kimni yoki nimani?

I read books.Men kitob o‘qiyman.
My father drives a car.Dadam mashina haydaydi.

3. Fe’lning oddiy shakli nima?

Fe’l — harakat bildiruvchi so‘z. Lug‘atda fe’lni ko‘pincha to work (ishlamoq), to play (o‘ynamoq) deb ko‘ramiz. Gapda esa odatda boshidagi to ni ishlatmaymiz: work, play.

to likelikeyoqtirmoq

4. I / You / We / They bilan fe’l o‘zgarmaydi

I, you, we, they bilan fe’lning oddiy shakli keladi. Odatdagi tasdiq gapda fe’lga -s qo‘shmaymiz.

I work.Men ishlayman.
You work.Sen / siz ishlaysan / ishlaysiz.
We work.Biz ishlaymiz.
They work.Ular ishlaydi.
I play football. ✅
I plays football. ❌
I / you / we / they bilan fe’lga -s qo‘shmang.

5. He / She / It bilan fe’lga -s

He, she, it yoki bitta odam/narsa haqida gapirganda, tasdiq gapda fe’lga ko‘pincha -s qo‘shamiz.

he / she / it + verb-s
I work. → He works.Men ishlayman. → U ishlaydi.
We read. → She reads.Biz o‘qiymiz. → U o‘qiydi.
They live here. → Ali lives here.Ular shu yerda yashaydi. → Ali shu yerda yashaydi.
The cat sleeps.Mushuk uxlaydi.

Bitta ism ham he/she/it kabi ishlaydi: Tom works, Anna reads, the dog sleeps.

She likes music. ✅
She like music. ❌
She bitta odamni bildiradi, shuning uchun like → likes.

6. Fe’lga -es qo‘shiladigan holatlar

Fe’l -s, -sh, -ch, -x bilan tugasa, he/she/it bilan odatda -es qo‘shiladi. Ayrim ko‘p ishlatiladigan -o fe’llari ham -es oladi.

7. Undosh harf + Y: y → ies

8. Unli harf + Y: faqat -s

9. Have va Be — alohida shakllar

Juda ko‘p ishlatiladigan ikki fe’lni alohida eslab qolamiz:

Oddiy shaklHe / She / ItMisol

Be oddiy -s qoidasiga kirmaydi: I am, you/we/they are, he/she/it is. Bu darsda to be ning to‘liq qoidalarini yana takrorlash mumkin.

10. Present Simple qaysi paytda ishlatiladi?

Odat va takrorlanadigan ish

I brush my teeth every morning.Men har kuni ertalab tishimni yuvaman.
He plays football on Sundays.U yakshanba kunlari futbol o‘ynaydi.
We visit our grandmother every week.Biz buvimiznikiga har hafta boramiz.

Umumiy haqiqat

Birds have wings.Qushlarning qanotlari bor.
The sun rises in the east.Quyosh sharqdan chiqadi.

Every day — har kuni, usually — odatda, often — tez-tez kabi iboralar muntazam ishni ko‘rsatadi.

11. He / She / It bilan fe’lni tekshirish yo‘li

1. Gapda kim yoki nima borligini toping.
2. Bitta odam/narsa bo‘lsa, he/she/it qoidasi kerak bo‘lishi mumkin.
3. Fe’lga qarang: odatda -s, ayrim fe’llarga -es yoki -ies qo‘shiladi.
4. I/you/we/they yoki ko‘plikdagi ega bo‘lsa, oddiy shaklni ishlating.
My brother (bitta) + playMy brother plays.
My brothers (ko‘p) + playMy brothers play.

Mashq 1 — To‘g‘ri fe’lni tanlang

1. I (like / likes) apples.

2. She (read / reads) every evening.

3. They (play / plays) football.

4. My brother (watch / watches) TV.

5. We (study / studies) English.

6. The baby (cry / cries) at night.

7. You (go / goes) to school.

Javoblar:
1. like. 2. reads. 3. play. 4. watches. 5. study. 6. cries. 7. go.

Present Simple: odat, takroriy ish, umumiy haqiqat.

I / you / we / they + fe’l: They play.

He / she / it + fe’lga -s/-es/-ies: She plays; he watches; Anna studies.

have → has, be → is — alohida yodlanadi.
