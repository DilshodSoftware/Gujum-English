20-AMALIYOT. PRESENT SIMPLE — DARAK GAPLAR

Odatlar · muntazam ishlar · umumiy haqiqatlar
Qolip: I/you/we/they + oddiy fe’l; he/she/it yoki bitta ega + fe’lga -s/-es/-ies. Present Simple odat va muntazam takrorlanadigan ishlar uchun ishlatiladi.

1-mashq — Odatmi yoki ayni hozirgi ishmi?

Present Simple bilan ishlatiladigan gaplarni belgilang.

1. I walk to school every day.

2. She is cooking now.

3. Birds fly.

4. We visit Grandma on Sundays.

5. They are playing at the moment.

6. The sun rises in the east.

Javoblar: Present Simple: 1, 3, 4, 6. Present Continuous: 2, 5.

2-mashq — To‘g‘ri fe’l shaklini tanlang

1. I (read / reads) before bed.

2. My parents (work / works) in a school.

3. The bus (stop / stops) here.

4. You (like / likes) music.

5. My friends (go / goes) swimming on Fridays.

6. The shop (open / opens) at nine.

Javoblar: 1. read; 2. work; 3. stops; 4. like; 5. go; 6. opens.

3-mashq — Qavsdagi fe’lni yozing

1. We ___ English at school. (study)

2. My father ___ a car. (drive)

3. They ___ in Samarkand. (live)

4. A baby ___ a lot. (sleep)

5. I ___ my room every Saturday. (clean)

Javoblar: 1. study; 2. drives; 3. live; 4. sleeps; 5. clean.

4-mashq — So‘zlarni gap tartibiga qo‘ying

1. every morning / tea / drink / I

2. English / studies / Sara / at home

3. play / on Sundays / they / football

4. water / plants / need

Javoblar:
1. I drink tea every morning. 2. Sara studies English at home.
3. They play football on Sundays. 4. Plants need water.

5-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. He play tennis every week.

2. I goes to work by bus.

3. My sisters likes music.

4. A dog chase cats.

5. She studys English.

Javoblar:
1. He plays tennis every week. 2. I go to work by bus.
3. My sisters like music. 4. A dog chases cats.
5. She studies English.

6-mashq — Tarjima qiling

1. Men har kuni kitob o‘qiyman.

2. Akam yakshanba kunlari futbol o‘ynaydi.

3. Biz maktab yaqinida yashaymiz.

4. Mushuklar sut ichadi.

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. I read a book every day. 2. My brother plays football on Sundays.
3. We live near the school. 4. Cats drink milk.
Yakuniy eslatma: gap odatmi? Ega he/she/it yoki bitta ismmi? Shunda darak gapdagi fe’l oxirini tekshiring.
