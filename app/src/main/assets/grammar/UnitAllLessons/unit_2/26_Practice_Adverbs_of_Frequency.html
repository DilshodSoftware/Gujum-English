26-AMALIYOT. ADVERBS OF FREQUENCY

Always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never
Joylashish: oddiy harakat fe’lidan oldin: I often read. To be dan keyin: She is often busy. Every day, once a week kabi vaqt iboralari esa ko‘pincha gap oxirida keladi.

1-mashq — Ma’nosiga mos ravishni toping

1. Hech qachon: (never / always)

2. Kamdan-kam: (rarely / often)

3. Tez-tez: (often / never)

4. Odatda: (usually / sometimes)

5. Ba’zan: (always / sometimes)

Javoblar: 1. never; 2. rarely; 3. often; 4. usually; 5. sometimes.

2-mashq — Tez-tezlik tartibini tuzing

Eng ko‘p takrorlanadigandan eng kam takrorlanadiganga qarab joylashtiring.

never · often · always · rarely · usually · sometimes

Javoblar: always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never.

3-mashq — Ravishni to‘g‘ri joyga qo‘ying

1. I / drink / always / water.

2. They / are / often / late.

3. My sister / usually / walks / to school.

4. He / is / never / angry.

5. We / sometimes / watch / films.

Javoblar:
1. I always drink water. 2. They are often late.
3. My sister usually walks to school. 4. He is never angry.
5. We sometimes watch films.

4-mashq — Oddiy fe’lmi yoki To Be?

Ravish to‘g‘ri joylashgan gaplarni belgilang.

1. I always eat breakfast.

2. She always is kind.

3. He is usually at home.

4. They play often football.

5. We never are tired.

6. Sara sometimes reads at night.

Javoblar: To‘g‘ri: 1, 3, 6. Tuzatish: 2. She is always kind. 4. They often play football. 5. We are never tired.

5-mashq — Vaqt iborasini gap oxiriga qo‘ying

1. I call my aunt. (once a week)

2. We play football. (on Fridays)

3. He visits the library. (every month)

Javoblar:
1. I call my aunt once a week. 2. We play football on Fridays.
3. He visits the library every month.

6-mashq — O‘zbekchadan inglizchaga

1. Men odatda ertalab choy ichaman.

2. U (qiz) ba’zan kitob o‘qiydi.

3. Biz hech qachon kechikmaymiz.

4. Ular tez-tez parkda bo‘ladi.

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. I usually drink tea in the morning. 2. She sometimes reads books.
3. We are never late. 4. They are often in the park.
