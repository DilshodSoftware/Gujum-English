Oldingi darslardan: tasdiq gapda They play. deymiz; inkor gapda They don’t play. Endi “Ular o‘ynaydimi?” deb savol berishni o‘rganamiz.

1. Ha yoki yo‘q deb javob beriladigan savol

Oddiy harakat fe’li bilan Present Simple savol tuzishda do yoki does gapning boshiga chiqadi.

Do / Does + Subject + fe’lning oddiy shakli + ... ?
You like tea.Sen choyni yoqtirasan. (darak)
Do you like tea?Sen choyni yoqtirasanmi? (savol)

2. Do kimlar bilan ishlatiladi?

Ega I, you, we, they yoki ko‘plikdagi odam/narsalar bo‘lsa, savol boshida do qo‘yamiz.

Do I need a pen?Menga ruchka kerakmi?
Do you speak English?Siz inglizcha gapirasizmi?
Do we start now?Biz hozir boshlaymizmi?
Do they live here?Ular shu yerda yashaydimi?

3. Does kim bilan ishlatiladi?

Ega he, she, it yoki bitta odam/narsa bo‘lsa, savol boshida does ishlatamiz.

Does he work here?U shu yerda ishlaydimi?
Does Sara like music?Sara musiqani yoqtiradimi?
Does the bus stop here?Avtobus shu yerda to‘xtaydimi?
I / you / we / they → Do   he / she / it → Does

4. Does dan keyin fe’lga -s qo‘shmaymiz

Darak gapda he plays bo‘ladi. Savol tuzganda -s yordamchi does ga o‘tadi, asosiy fe’l oddiy shaklga qaytadi.

Darak: She reads books.U kitob o‘qiydi.
Savol: Does she read books?U kitob o‘qiydimi?
Does he play football? ✅
Does he plays football? ❌
Does dan keyin: play, read, go kabi oddiy shakl ishlatiladi.

5. Ism bilan savol tuzish

Ism bitta odam yoki narsani anglatsa, uni he/she/it kabi ko‘rib does ishlatamiz. Bir nechta odam yoki narsada do ishlatiladi.

Does Tom play tennis?Tom tennis o‘ynaydimi?
Does your sister read?Singlingiz / opangiz o‘qiydimi?
Do Ali and Sam work here?Ali va Sam shu yerda ishlaydimi?

6. Savol tuzish tartibini eslab qoling

1. Ega kimligini toping.
2. I/you/we/they bo‘lsa do; he/she/it yoki bitta ism bo‘lsa does tanlang.
3. Yordamchi so‘zni gap boshiga qo‘ying.
4. Asosiy fe’lni oddiy shaklda yozing.
5. Oxiriga so‘roq belgisi (?) qo‘ying.
she + like + teaShe bitta ega → Does she like tea?
they + work + hereThey → Do they work here?

7. What, Where, When bilan savollar

Javobda aniq ma’lumot so‘rash uchun savol so‘zini gap boshiga qo‘yamiz. Keyin do/does, ega va oddiy fe’l keladi.

Question word + do/does + subject + verb?
What do you eat?Sen nima yeysan?
Where does she live?U qayerda yashaydi?
When do they study?Ular qachon o‘qiydi?
Why does he run?U nima uchun yuguradi?

8. Oddiy fe’l va To Be savollarini farqlaymiz

Am/is/are bo‘lsa, ular o‘zlari gap boshiga o‘tadi. Oddiy harakat fe’li bo‘lsa, do/does yordam beradi.

Are you happy?To be: are gap boshiga chiqdi.
Do you like music?Oddiy fe’l like: do yordam beradi.
Is she a teacher?To be: is gap boshiga chiqdi.
Does she teach English?Oddiy fe’l teach: does yordam beradi.
Does he work here? ✅
Is he work here? ❌
Bu gapda asosiy fe’l work, shuning uchun do/does kerak.

9. Ko‘p uchraydigan xatolar

You like tea? ❌ → Do you like tea? ✅
Does she likes music? ❌ → Does she like music? ✅
Do Ali work here? ❌ → Does Ali work here? ✅
Does they play? ❌ → Do they play? ✅

Mashq 1 — Do yoki Does ni tanlang

1. ___ you read every day?

2. ___ he speak English?

3. ___ the children play here?

4. ___ your mother work?

5. ___ it rain here?

6. ___ we need a ticket?

Javoblar:
1. Do. 2. Does. 3. Do. 4. Does. 5. Does. 6. Do.

Mashq 2 — So‘zlarni savol tartibiga qo‘ying

1. you / do / coffee / like / ?

2. does / where / he / work / ?

3. she / does / books / read / ?

4. do / they / when / study / ?

Javoblar:
1. Do you like coffee? 2. Where does he work?
3. Does she read books? 4. When do they study?
