27-AMALIYOT. PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing · ayni paytda davom etayotgan ish
Formula: avval egaga mos am/is/are, keyin fe’lning -ing shakli: She is reading now. Fe’lga -ing qo‘shishda ba’zan oxirgi e tushadi yoki oxirgi undosh ikkilanadi.

1-mashq — Am, Is yoki Are?

1. I ___ writing a message.

2. The children ___ playing outside.

3. My mother ___ cooking dinner.

4. You ___ listening to music.

5. The dog ___ running in the garden.

6. We ___ learning English.

Javoblar: 1. am; 2. are; 3. is; 4. are; 5. is; 6. are.

2-mashq — Fe’lga -ing qo‘shing

Oddiy qoida, oxirgi e tushishi yoki undoshning ikkilanib yozilishini aniqlang.

1. read → ___   2. make → ___   3. sit → ___

4. play → ___   5. write → ___   6. swim → ___

7. watch → ___   8. come → ___

Javoblar: 1. reading; 2. making; 3. sitting; 4. playing; 5. writing; 6. swimming; 7. watching; 8. coming.

3-mashq — Fe’lning o‘zgarish turini aniqlang

1. work → working

2. dance → dancing

3. run → running

Tanlov: -ing qo‘shiladi / e tushadi / oxirgi undosh ikkilanadi

Javoblar: 1. -ing qo‘shiladi; 2. e tushadi; 3. oxirgi undosh ikkilanadi.

4-mashq — Gaplarni to‘ldiring

1. She ___ (read) a story now.

2. They ___ (swim) at the moment.

3. I ___ (make) breakfast.

4. Tom ___ (sit) by the window.

5. We ___ (watch) a film right now.

Javoblar:
1. She is reading a story now. 2. They are swimming at the moment.
3. I am making breakfast. 4. Tom is sitting by the window.
5. We are watching a film right now.

5-mashq — Rasmni tasavvur qiling, mos fe’lni tanlang

1. Look! The baby (sleeps / is sleeping).

2. Listen! The birds (sing / are singing).

3. Right now, Dad (drives / is driving) home.

4. At the moment, we (have / are having) lunch.

Javoblar: 1. is sleeping; 2. are singing; 3. is driving; 4. are having.

6-mashq — To‘liq gap tuzing

1. (I / study / now)

2. (The boys / run / in the park)

3. (Mina / write / a letter / at the moment)

Namunaviy javoblar: 1. I am studying now. 2. The boys are running in the park. 3. Mina is writing a letter at the moment.

7-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. He reading a book.

2. They are swiming.

3. I am makeing tea.

4. She are watching TV.

Javoblar:
1. He is reading a book. 2. They are swimming.
3. I am making tea. 4. She is watching TV.
