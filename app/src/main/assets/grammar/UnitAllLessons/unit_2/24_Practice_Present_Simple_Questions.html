24-AMALIYOT. PRESENT SIMPLE — SAVOLLAR

Do / Does + ega + oddiy fe’l? · What / Where / When / Why
Harakat fe’li bilan: Do/Does + subject + oddiy fe’l? He/she/it bilan does ishlatiladi, ammo keyingi fe’lga -s qo‘shilmaydi. Savol so‘zi bo‘lsa, u gap boshida keladi. To be savollarida esa am/is/are oldinga chiqadi.

1-mashq — Do yoki Does?

1. ___ your friends speak English?

2. ___ your mother drive?

3. ___ we start now?

4. ___ this bus go to the station?

5. ___ I need a coat?

Javoblar: 1. Do; 2. Does; 3. Do; 4. Does; 5. Do.

2-mashq — So‘zlarni savol tartibiga keltiring

1. you / do / live / where / ?

2. does / what / she / cook / ?

3. they / when / do / study / ?

4. why / he / does / run / ?

5. do / your friends / music / like / ?

Javoblar:
1. Where do you live? 2. What does she cook?
3. When do they study? 4. Why does he run?
5. Do your friends like music?

3-mashq — Savol so‘zini tanlang

1. ___ do you live? (joy)

2. ___ does he have for breakfast? (narsa)

3. ___ do you go to bed? (vaqt)

4. ___ does Sara walk to school? (sabab)

5. ___ do they travel? (usul)

Javoblar: 1. Where; 2. What; 3. When; 4. Why; 5. How.

4-mashq — Oddiy fe’lmi yoki To Be?

Qavsdan to‘g‘ri savolni tanlang.

1. ___ you tired? (Are / Do)

2. ___ you like apples? (Are / Do)

3. Where ___ she? (is / does)

4. Where ___ she work? (is / does)

5. ___ Ali a student? (Does / Is)

Javoblar: 1. Are; 2. Do; 3. is; 4. does; 5. Is.

5-mashq — Gapni savolga aylantiring

1. They play chess. (ha/yo‘q savoli)

2. He works at a bank. (qayerda?)

3. You eat lunch at twelve. (qachon?)

4. She likes this song. (ha/yo‘q savoli)

Javoblar:
1. Do they play chess? 2. Where does he work?
3. When do you eat lunch? 4. Does she like this song?

6-mashq — Savolga mos javob turini belgilang

1. Where do you live? → joy / vaqt

2. Who is your teacher? → odam / sabab

3. Why does he study? → sabab / narsa

4. Do you like tea? → ha/yo‘q / joy

Javoblar: 1. joy; 2. odam; 3. sabab; 4. ha/yo‘q.
