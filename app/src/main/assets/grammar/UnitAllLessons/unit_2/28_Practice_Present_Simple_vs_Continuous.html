28-AMALIYOT. PRESENT SIMPLE vs CONTINUOUS

Odatmi? · Ayni hozirgi ishmi? · Vaqtinchalik holatmi?
Tanlash uchun: odat, jadval yoki umumiy haqiqat → Present Simple. Hozir/ayni paytda yoki vaqtinchalik davom etayotgan ish → Present Continuous. Like, know, want kabi holat fe’llari boshlang‘ich bosqichda ko‘pincha Simple da ishlatiladi.

1-mashq — Zamonni aniqlang

1. I walk to work every day.

2. Look! It is raining.

3. My sister is staying with us this week.

4. They play tennis on Saturdays.

5. We are having dinner now.

6. He likes orange juice.

Javoblar: 1. Present Simple; 2. Present Continuous; 3. Present Continuous (vaqtinchalik); 4. Present Simple; 5. Present Continuous; 6. Present Simple.

2-mashq — To‘g‘ri variantni tanlang

1. She (reads / is reading) before bed every night.

2. Be quiet! The baby (sleeps / is sleeping).

3. I (work / am working) at a café this month. (vaqtinchalik)

4. He (washes / is washing) his car every Sunday.

5. We (eat / are eating) lunch right now.

6. They (know / are knowing) the answer.

7. My father (drives / is driving) to work every day.

Javoblar: 1. reads; 2. is sleeping; 3. am working; 4. washes; 5. are eating; 6. know; 7. drives.

3-mashq — Fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklda yozing

1. Sara ___ (study) English every evening.

2. Sara ___ (study) now.

3. I ___ (not / like) cold coffee.

4. The children ___ (play) outside at the moment.

5. My uncle ___ (visit) us once a month.

6. We ___ (stay) at a hotel this week.

Javoblar: 1. studies; 2. is studying; 3. don’t like; 4. are playing; 5. visits; 6. are staying.

4-mashq — Vaqt iboralarini moslang

1. every morning   2. right now   3. usually   4. at the moment   5. on Sundays   6. this week (vaqtinchalik)

Tanlov: odat / hozir / vaqtinchalik

Javoblar: Odat: 1, 3, 5. Hozir: 2, 4. Vaqtinchalik: 6. Vaqt iborasi yordam beradi, lekin gap ma’nosini ham tekshiring.

5-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. She is go to school every day.

2. I am read a book now.

3. They are play football on Fridays. (odat)

4. He is knowing my name.

5. My brother work in a bank.

Javoblar:
1. She goes to school every day. 2. I am reading a book now.
3. They play football on Fridays. 4. He knows my name.
5. My brother works in a bank.

6-mashq — Bir fe’l bilan ikki ma’no

read fe’lidan foydalanib, odat va ayni hozir bo‘layotgan ish haqida bittadan gap tuzing.

Namunaviy javoblar:
I read every evening. / I am reading now.
