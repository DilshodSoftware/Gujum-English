22-AMALIYOT. PRESENT SIMPLE — INKOR

Don’t / Doesn’t + fe’lning oddiy shakli
Qoidani eslang: I/you/we/they + don’t + fe’l; he/she/it yoki bitta ega + doesn’t + fe’l. Doesn’t dan keyin asosiy fe’lga -s qo‘shilmaydi. To be bilan esa am/is/are + not ishlatiladi.

1-mashq — Don’t yoki Doesn’t?

1. My friends ___ eat meat.

2. My uncle ___ work on Sundays.

3. I ___ watch TV in the morning.

4. The shop ___ open at night.

5. You ___ need a ticket.

6. A cat ___ usually like water.

7. We ___ live near the airport.

Javoblar: 1. don’t; 2. doesn’t; 3. don’t; 4. doesn’t; 5. don’t; 6. doesn’t; 7. don’t.

2-mashq — Gaplarni inkor shakliga o‘tkazing

1. I drink coffee.

2. She speaks Spanish.

3. They play basketball.

4. The bus stops here.

5. My brother studies at night.

Javoblar:
1. I don’t drink coffee. 2. She doesn’t speak Spanish.
3. They don’t play basketball. 4. The bus doesn’t stop here.
5. My brother doesn’t study at night.

3-mashq — Yordamchi va asosiy fe’lni belgilang

Inkor gapda qaysi so‘z don’t/doesn’t, qaysi biri asosiy fe’l ekanini aniqlang.

1. He doesn’t drive a car.

2. We don’t eat fish.

3. Anna doesn’t go to school by bus.

Javoblar: 1. doesn’t = yordamchi; drive = asosiy fe’l. 2. don’t; eat. 3. doesn’t; go.

4-mashq — To be bilan inkorni farqlang

Bo‘sh joyni don’t / doesn’t / isn’t / aren’t / am not dan mosini tanlab to‘ldiring.

1. I ___ like cold tea.

2. She ___ at home today. (to be)

3. They ___ work on Fridays.

4. He ___ a teacher. (to be)

5. My dog ___ sleep in the kitchen.

Javoblar: 1. don’t; 2. isn’t; 3. don’t; 4. isn’t; 5. doesn’t.

5-mashq — Xatolarni tuzating

1. She don’t like apples.

2. He doesn’t watches TV.

3. They doesn’t live here.

4. I not speak French.

5. My sister doesn’t studies English.

6. We don’t are tired.

Javoblar:
1. She doesn’t like apples. 2. He doesn’t watch TV.
3. They don’t live here. 4. I don’t speak French.
5. My sister doesn’t study English. 6. We aren’t tired.

6-mashq — O‘zbekchadan inglizchaga

1. Men qahva ichmayman.

2. U (erkak) yakshanba kuni ishlamaydi.

3. Biz bu shaharda yashamaymiz.

4. Mushuklar sabzi yemaydi.

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. I don’t drink coffee. 2. He doesn’t work on Sundays.
3. We don’t live in this city. 4. Cats don’t eat carrots.
