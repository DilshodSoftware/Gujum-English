21-AMALIYOT. HE / SHE / IT + -S

Bitta ega · fe’l + s / es / ies · istisno: have → has, be → is
Eslab qoling: Present Simple darak gapida he/she/it yoki bitta odam/narsa bo‘lsa, fe’l o‘zgaradi: ko‘pincha -s, -s/-sh/-ch/-x oxirida -es, undosh + y bo‘lsa -ies. Unli + y bo‘lsa faqat -s: plays.

1-mashq — Fe’l shakllarini guruhlang

Quyidagi fe’llarni mos qoidaga ajrating.

Fe’llar: read, watch, study, play, wash, go, enjoy, fix, cry, have

A. +s   B. +es   C. undosh + y → ies   D. alohida shakl

Javoblar: A. reads, plays, enjoys; B. watches, washes, goes, fixes; C. studies, cries; D. have → has. Play va enjoy unli + y bilan tugagani uchun y o‘zgarmaydi, faqat -s qo‘shiladi.

2-mashq — Fe’lni he/she/it bilan o‘zgartiring

1. work → he ___

2. wash → she ___

3. study → my brother ___

4. play → the girl ___

5. go → Tom ___

6. fix → it ___

7. have → Anna ___

8. enjoy → he ___

Javoblar: 1. works; 2. washes; 3. studies; 4. plays; 5. goes; 6. fixes; 7. has; 8. enjoys.

3-mashq — Qaysi ega bilan fe’lga -s qo‘shiladi?

1. my uncle   2. the children   3. you   4. the bus   5. Ali and Sara   6. it

Javoblar: -s/-es/-ies shakli kerak: 1. my uncle; 4. the bus; 6. it. Ko‘plik yoki you: 2, 3, 5 — oddiy fe’l.

4-mashq — Xatoni tuzating

1. The baby crys at night.

2. My father watch TV after dinner.

3. She studys French.

4. He play the piano.

5. My friends goes to school by bus.

Javoblar:
1. The baby cries at night. 2. My father watches TV after dinner.
3. She studies French. 4. He plays the piano.
5. My friends go to school by bus.

5-mashq — To‘g‘ri shaklni tanlang

1. The bus (pass / passes) our house.

2. My sister (enjoy / enjoys) drawing.

3. A bird (fly / flies) in the sky.

4. They (watch / watches) cartoons.

5. The dog (have / has) a long tail.

Javoblar: 1. passes; 2. enjoys; 3. flies; 4. watch; 5. has.

6-mashq — Ism bilan gap tuzing

1. (Malika / read / every evening)

2. (my brother / fix / bicycles)

3. (the cat / sleep / on the sofa)

Namunaviy javoblar:
1. Malika reads every evening. 2. My brother fixes bicycles. 3. The cat sleeps on the sofa.
Tekshirib ko‘ring: asosiy qoida faqat darak gapda. He plays to‘g‘ri, ammo savol va inkorda fe’l o‘zgarishi boshqa darslarda ko‘riladi.
